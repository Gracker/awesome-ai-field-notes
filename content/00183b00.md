## 中文导读

Anthropic 11-05 推出 Claude Personal Finance（beta）：连接银行与卡账户后用自然语言提问，Claude 基于余额与交易流水回答并解释术语；访问为只读，不能移动、管理或投资资金；无广告与赞助推荐，也不自营信用卡/贷款/储蓄产品。本周起在美国 Pro/Max/Ultra 计划灰度。示例场景：「这个月钱花哪了」自动跨账户汇总分类——$4,309.37 中过半是房租水电、$1,024.51 是餐饮。

## 为什么值得关注

只读权限加无自营产品的金融问答是克制的好设计，剩下的问题只是账户授权面能否过合规

模型厂正面进入个人金融数据面的标志动作；「只读 + 不自营」的边界声明写得比功能本身更值得抄。

## 关键信息

- 发布：2026-11-05，beta，本周起美国 Pro/Max/Ultra 灰度
- 接入：银行与卡账户，只读；不能移动、管理或投资资金
- 边界：无广告/赞助推荐；不自营信用卡、贷款、储蓄产品
- 示例：跨账户月度消费汇总 $4,309.37（过半房租水电、$1,024.51 餐饮）

## 原文摘录

> With Personal Finance in Claude, you can securely connect your bank and card accounts and ask in your own words. Access is read-only, and Personal Finance can't move, manage, or invest your money.
>
> Like the rest of Claude, Personal Finance has no ads or sponsored recommendations, and it doesn't offer credit cards, loans, or savings accounts of its own.

## English Summary

Anthropic introduced Personal Finance in Claude (Nov 5, 2026, beta): connect bank and card accounts read-only, then ask questions in plain language - Claude answers from balances and transactions and explains unfamiliar terms. It cannot move, manage, or invest money, shows no ads or sponsored recommendations, and doesn't sell its own credit cards, loans, or savings accounts. Rolling out this week to Pro, Max, and Ultra plans in the US; the launch demo aggregates cross-account spending ($4,309.37, over half rent/utilities, $1,024.51 food and drink) from a single question.
