# SQLite compressed text-history prototypes

> Source: https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/9/sqlite-text-history-prototype/
> Author: Simon Willison

## Summary (EN)

Simon Willison prototyped a SQLite-based text revision history store: bundle all prior versions of a document into a JSON array, then compress the whole blob with zlib or zstd. 1,000 simulated revisions of a 20.4 MB document compressed to 80.3 KB. To avoid decompressing/recompressing the entire history on each edit, the chunked variant breaks history into rows of max 128 revisions or 3MB uncompressed JSON. Two approaches compared: WholeBlobHistoryStore (rewrite one compressed blob per edit) and ChunkedHistoryStore (sealed compressed chunks for scaling). Both skip unchanged replacements and serialize writers with BEGIN IMMEDIATE.
