## 中文摘要

Jim Nielsen 10 月 1 日借 Marques Brownlee 的「Dear YouTube」视频讨论创作与优化的张力。YouTube 正在内测 A/B variant 功能，让 creator 发布同一视频的多版本比较表现；Nielsen 认同 Brownlee 的反对：创作的核心能力是找到有创造性的方式分享经验、教概念、探想法，views 与 retention 都是这个能力的下游；一旦聚焦优化，「不会立即折算成指标」的创造性部分会先被剪掉——Moby Dick 不是讲这个故事最高效的方式。他把这一功能归因于平台自身的不安全感——「花太多时间追竞争对手，不够时间做 YouTube」，并把「坚持做完一件、学到东西、再做下一件」的态度推荐给软件行业。

## One-liner

Nielsen：创作能力是目的，指标只是下游，别把创作 A/B 化

## English Summary

Nielsen riffs on Marques Brownlee's 'Dear YouTube' plea against A/B variant testing of videos: the core creator skill is finding creative ways to share experience and teach ideas, and views and retention are downstream of that. Optimizing trims out the creative parts first ('Moby Dick was not the most efficient way to tell that story'). He ties the feature to YouTube's own insecurity - 'spending way too much time chasing their competitors and not enough time being YouTube' - and recommends the make-it-then-learn-then-make-the-next-thing conviction to software makers.

## 原文摘录

> When you focus on optimizing, you end up trimming out all the creative (and fun) stuff because those things won't, by definition, ladder up to metrics of efficiency. (Moby Dick was not the most efficient way to tell that story.)
