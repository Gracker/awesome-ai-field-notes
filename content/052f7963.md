## English Abstract / Summary

The paper audits offline KV-cache quantization for RAG systems and shows faithfulness and accuracy can diverge. On Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct with RGB and HotpotQA, INT8 is near-lossless but INT4 causes over 90% of faithfulness changes to be negative even among answers that remain factually correct; standard accuracy metrics are blind to this regression and the damage grows with retrieval noise and chunk count. The authors recommend pairing quantization releases with faithfulness audits.
