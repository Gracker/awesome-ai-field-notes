## 摘要 (Summary)

现代 AI 在推理编码定理证明工具使用与长程研究任务上被广泛评估，但都共享一个结构性限制：其运行的表征框架（概念词汇可搜索解空间成功判定标准）通常是预设且固定的本文论证：构建真正具备开放式创新能力的强 AI 需要额外的运算类创造稳定化复用新的表征基元，从而改变被搜索的空间本身而非仅在空间内搜索用'词汇鸿沟'（vocabulary gap：发明并稳定新表征基元的难度）与'验证器鸿沟'（verifier gap：判定新基元价值的难度，其完整收益要等到未来复用才显现）刻画当前 AI 与真正开放式智能的距离两个鸿沟被纳入统一的'智能 = 认知差异缩减'框架，区分空间内变换（固定表征下操作）与生成性变换（可修改表征自身）提出创新自主性阶梯，并指出多个方向：奖励有用表征变化的目标为发明基元的持久记忆架构能随被评估表征共同演化的自适应验证机制

## English Abstract / Excerpt

Modern AI systems are increasingly evaluated for reasoning, coding, theorem proving, tool use, and long-horizon research. These capabilities share a structural limitation: the representational frame in which the model operates, including its conceptual vocabulary, the space of admissible solutions it can search, and the criteria by which success is evaluated, is typically fixed and supplied in advance. This paper argues that building stronger intelligent systems capable of open-ended innovation requires additional classes of operations, namely the creation, stabilization, and reuse of new representational primitives, which alter the space being searched rather than searching within it. We characterize the distance between current AI and genuinely open-ended intelligence through two gaps: the vocabulary gap and the verifier gap....
