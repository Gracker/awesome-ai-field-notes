## 中文导读

Gary Marcus 与 Fordham 法学院 Zephyr Teachout 的访谈全文。核心主张：执法不需要等新立法——CFAA 已把未授权（企图）访问计算机定为联邦罪，州级有更宽的 computer trespass 条款（纽约上诉法院曾把复制高盛交易代码判为 computer trespass）；DOJ 应 subpoena OpenAI 内部文档查明「谁在何时知道什么」，而不是接受「我们没打算」的抗辩。民事侧她引用 Lina Khan 的缺陷产品/products liability 框架，把 AI 责任接进法院已有一百年判例的体系；刑事侧纽约的企业责任条款与 criminally negligent homicide 判例（1990 校车案）都在可用清单上；州 AG 还握有司法解散屡次违法公司的权力（纽约 BCL，曾用于 Trump Organization）。顺序应是先执法、同时补立法。

## 原文摘录

> The Federal Computer Fraud and Abuse Act makes unauthorized attempted or actual access to computers a federal crime... DOJ would be a laughingstock if it took this at face value: subpoena OpenAI and find out who knew what when.

## English Summary

An interview with Fordham law professor Zephyr Teachout arguing enforcement should precede new legislation: the CFAA already criminalizes unauthorized attempted or actual computer access, state computer-trespass statutes are broader (NY's Court of Appeals upheld a conviction for copying Goldman Sachs trading code), and DOJ should subpoena OpenAI's internal documents to establish who knew what when instead of accepting no-intent defenses. On the civil side she endorses Lina Khan's defective-product/products-liability framing connecting AI to a century of precedent; on the criminal side she cites NY corporate liability and the 1990 camp-bus criminally negligent homicide precedent; and state AGs hold the power of judicial dissolution for repeat lawbreakers (NY BCL, used against the Trump Organization).
