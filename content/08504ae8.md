## English Summary

Groupwise quantization improves accuracy at low bit widths but keeps standard integer MMA from spanning the full dot product for efficient GPU inference. Shifter Quantization (SQ) reconstructs native-width integer operands by appending and shifting shared bits; the SHI variant targets INT4 kernels. SQ and SHI beat standard groupwise quantization on projection error and KL divergence while also decoding and prefilling faster: SQ2/SHI2 reach 2.71-3.77x the 4,096-token prefill throughput of llama.cpp on a Q2_K checkpoint with 6.6-7.8% lower WikiText-2 KL; SQ4 achieves 1.43-2.47x NInfer's groupwise-integer baselines across tested models and prompt lengths with 24-40% lower KL; SQ8 reaches 27.4% higher batch-one prefill throughput than vLLM's online FP8 with 72.7% lower KL.

## Source Excerpts

### Abstract (opencli arxiv paper, 2026-11-26)

Groupwise quantization keeps standard integer MMA from spanning the full dot product for efficient GPU inference. Shifter Quantization (SQ) reconstructs native-width integer operands by appending and shifting shared bits; the SHI variant uses INT4 kernels. SQ2 and SHI2 achieve 2.71-3.77x the 4,096-token prefill throughput of llama.cpp on a Q2_K checkpoint and reduce WikiText-2 KL by 6.6-7.8%. SQ4 achieves 1.43-2.47x the prefill throughput of NInfer's groupwise-integer baselines with 24-40% lower KL. SQ8 achieves 27.4% higher batch-one prefill throughput and 72.7% lower KL than vLLM's online FP8.
