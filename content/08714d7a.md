We live in a scam economy... Then there's AI: voice cloning, deepfakes, and image-genned documents (including convincing IDs) have ushered in a golden age of account takeovers, identity theft and good, old-fashioned impersonation.

The harder it is to tell a scam from a shitty business practice, the easier life gets for scammers.
