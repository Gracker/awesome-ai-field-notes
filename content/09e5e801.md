## English Summary

Cloudflare open-sourced Kitesurf, its agent-first browser runtime, on Nov 13 under Apache-2.0 (github.com/cloudflare/kitesurf). It drops the Chromium container and speaks CDP directly: Engine/PageScript/Renderer components plus a SandboxOutbound egress-control layer; Dynamic Workers are a paid tier and self-hosted deployments ship without auth, flagged in README warnings. Vendor docs claim 3-7x lower CPU/memory versus Chromium containers at 1.7-1.8x slower wall time; video/WebGL/bot-TLS/long-lived logins are not yet supported. An n=1 classmethod test found agents slower with more steps but 8/9 tasks correct. For Playwright/Browser Use style toolchains, CDP compatibility concentrates the switching cost in the unsupported-sites list and login state.
