## 中文导读

Cory Doctorow 在 Pluralistic 的这篇把当下 AI 的核心特征概括为『无理论』（theory-free）：系统不依赖人写的因果模型，直接用数据和算力找统计关系。这个范式能下棋、能写代码，也能撞出医学上『不知道为什么 A 能治 B』的有效相关；代价是幻觉、不连续和离谱的成本。他算了下棋那笔账：50 年代真空管计算机就能跑出合法的国际象棋程序，如今一个理论下棋 AI 消耗的算力是当年的天文倍数。文章真正重的部分在后半段的政治经济学：AI 替代的与其说是工程师个体，不如说是『世界必须由专家解释』这个前提——把解释权从劳动者的专业知识换成资本可以购买的算力，资本对劳动的话语权随之收紧。他把 hybrid（符号系统回归）视为无理论 AI 不得不承认自身政治经济学局限的开始。

## English Abstract

Current AI is theory-free: it finds statistical relations with data and compute instead of human causal models, which works for chess, code, and medically useful correlations at the price of hallucination, discontinuity, and cost. The political economy follows: AI displaces the premise that the world must be interpreted by experts, replacing expert labor with capital-purchased compute and tightening capital's leverage over labor; hybrid symbolic systems mark the point where theory-free AI must admit its limits.

## English Summary

Doctorow's essay frames today's AI as theory-free statistical curve-fitting and traces its labor-market consequence: expertise as an interpretation monopoly gives way to compute owned by capital, shifting bargaining power away from workers.
