## English Summary

OpenAI ships Images 2.6 across ChatGPT, Codex, and the API: GIF generation (brand mascots, memes, animated storyboards, sketches turned into expressive animated characters), 50% faster generation, and two API models - GPT-Image-2.6 Flare (as fast as six seconds at low quality, improved layout for posters and product imagery) and GPT-Image-2.6 Sunburst (higher fidelity and cross-edit consistency at medium/high quality). New API controls: image markup for pinpointing edit locations (previously ChatGPT-only), SVG exports, and a complexity parameter that adjusts how much planning the model does (low for quick edits, medium/high for scenes with many elements or constraints). OpenAI says 4 billion images per week are now created across the three surfaces; a system card documents safety evaluations.

## 原文摘录（openai.com, 2026-11-04）

We're introducing Images 2.6 in ChatGPT, Codex, and the API, bringing your ideas to life faster. With this, you can bring motion to your images with GIFs - from animating a brand mascot or character to turning a product shot into an eye-catching loop. You can also create polished visuals faster and refine them with greater precision - keeping characters, products, and brand details consistent as you edit.

People around the world now create 4 billion images per week across ChatGPT, Codex, and the API. On average, that's more than half a billion images created per day.

Images 2.6 generates images 50% faster than before. In the API, we offer GPT-Image-2.6 Flare and Sunburst for developers to choose from. When quality is set to low, Flare can generate images in as little as six seconds. For work that calls for greater detail and precision, GPT-Image-2.6 Sunburst brings higher-quality image generation and even greater image consistency across edits when quality is set to medium or high.

With Images 2.6, developers get more control over how images are created, edited, and carried into the next stage of a project. Image markup, already available in ChatGPT, comes to the API so teams can pinpoint exactly where to make specific changes. SVG exports make designs ready to use across websites, apps, and print. A new complexity parameter lets developers adjust how much planning the model does to meet a request, with lower settings suited to quick edits.

Images 2.6 is available globally today in ChatGPT and Codex across all subscription tiers on desktop, mobile, and web. GPT-Image-2.6 Sunburst and GPT-Image-2.6 Flare are available in the API.
