## Abstract（节选原文）

> A frontier AI model is trained in an AI data center, on tens of thousands of accelerators joined by a dedicated network, and every training step ends with all of them exchanging data at once. Between racks, that network is optical, and each of its four layers, from lasers to switches, runs a fixed or after-the-fact policy. Lasers sharing an enclosure, cooling, and power can fail together. At a fixed spare ratio, correlated failure provably caps a pool's availability however large it grows. … Halley, that reads each resource's next state and acts before the failure occurs. It has four components, each carrying a formal guarantee: LightReserve that sizes the laser pool to provide a coverage bound, FoldFit that schedules the switch within 11/9 of the optimal, ThermaLead that holds a microring with one-step-ahead control, and TriageGuard that protects the highest-risk modules first.

## 中文摘要

AI 数据中心机架间光网络的四层（激光器、交换、微环温控、高风险模块）此前都靠固定或事后策略；共享机壳/冷却/电源的激光器会相关性失效，固定备件比下可用性有可证明的上限。Halley 读每个资源的下一状态、在失败前动手：LightReserve 给激光池 sizing 出覆盖界，FoldFit 把交换调度到最优 11/9 以内，ThermaLead 对微环做单步前瞻控制，TriageGuard 优先保护最高风险模块，各组件带形式化保证。摘要吞吐收益（65.2%→76.5% 口径）依赖整体窗口丢失假设，属组合模拟结论，另有回退电路形式验证与 FPGA/130nm 检查。
