Doctorow 从“AI 眼镜的 killer app 是帮你记名字”这个卖点入手：他承认自己轻度脸盲、这个承诺对他有真实吸引力，然后指出实际已上线的 pervert glasses 主要用途是 covert capture——偷拍陌生人并羞辱式传播；一旦接上面部识别，就是高产量 doxing 工厂，把公共场合的普通人批量转成可消费的内容，并给渴望走红的用户提供病毒式曝光的回报通道。

## One-liner

## English Summary

Doctorow argues Meta's AI camera glasses are not about remembering names: coupled with facial recognition they become infrastructure for content-creator surveillance - high-volume doxing factories. He rewrites Bentham's panopticon as 'scrutiny': continuous judgement is what makes people abandon their authentic selves. Evidence: Ellison's AI-supervision vision, Zuckerberg buying privacy at home while enforcing Real Names on Facebook. The scrutiny economy is a net loss for anyone outside Silicon Valley.

## 原文摘录（节选）

We're repeatedly told that the killer app for pervert glasses – Meta's AI-enabled camera glasses and their imitators – is that they will remind you of someone's name and spare you the social awkwardness of not recognizing them. [...] If there were some way to confine pervert glasses to helping people remember names, I'd be all over them. But when it comes to actual, existing pervert-glasses, the main purpose is to covertly capture strangers and shame them. And when (not if) these pervert glasses get hooked up to facial recognition, they will become high-volume doxing factories that will reward the attention-hungry with viral fame.
