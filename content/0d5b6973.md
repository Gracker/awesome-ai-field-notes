## 中文导读

NEO-VLA（2611.06918，2026-11-04，cs.CV）提出结构上去除视觉编码器的 VLA 框架：图像经轻量 patch 卷积 tokenize 后与语言在主干中共同演化，flow-matching 动作专家可在不同深度取用指令条件化的视觉表征。对照实验显示原生架构稳定优于 encoder-based 对应物；全量微调缩小但不消除差距，单纯加大规模无法弥补。逐层探测发现动作关键信号往往远早于最终视觉表征就达到峰值——具身控制需要贯穿计算层级的视觉表征访问，而非固定的编码器输出。代码开源（EvolvingLMMs-Lab/NEO VLAKit）。

## 为什么值得关注

给动作专家开一条直达中间视觉表征的通路，encoder-free VLA 把「固定编码端点」假设打上了问号

「动作关键信号在最终视觉表征之前就到峰」这个逐层探测结果，对所有「VLM 骨干 + action head」式 VLA 都是结构性提醒：动作需要的几何/边界/深度信号恰恰是编码器压缩时最容易丢的。

## English Summary

NEO-VLA (arXiv 2611.06918, Nov 4, 2026, cs.CV) asks whether VLA policies should inherit a fixed visual encoder endpoint or access evolving visual computation directly. The framework is structurally encoder-free: images are tokenized by lightweight patch convolution layers and evolve jointly with language through the backbone, letting a flow-matching action expert draw on instruction-conditioned visual representations at different depths. Under controlled comparisons, native architectures consistently outperform encoder-based counterparts; full fine-tuning narrows but does not erase the gap, and scale alone cannot recover it. Layer-wise probing shows action-critical signals often peak well before the final visual representation. Code released (EvolvingLMMs-Lab/NEO VLAKit).
