## 中文导读

Agent 权重固定、靠经验跨 episode 自我改进，现有基准只测单一维度：任务进度、世界知识问答或情景记忆。答对不代表见过证据，有进度不代表学到东西。AstronautBench 建在游戏 Outer Wilds 上：每局 22 分钟循环后世界重置，只有知识可继承，进度由知识门控。每局结束测三件事：到达地点与船日志传闻事实、164 个世界问题的回答对错、每个回答是否真被 agent 自己引用的画面和位置支撑，两个不同模型族的 LLM judge 逐条核查。GPT-6.1 Sol 与 Gemini 4 Argon 规划器的八局基线显示三个视图不同步：Sol 答对率从 14.6% 涨到 26.8%，但第 8 局只有 59-68% 的正确回答有证据支撑，第 3 局后不再发现新事实，而到达地点和记忆还在涨。

## 为什么值得关注

"agent 自我改进"是当下最容易被单指标粉饰的声明之一，AstronautBench 用证据核查把"答对"和"真学到了"分开——过半新变对的回答只引用更早 episode 的证据，这类数字对评估任何"跨会话记忆/经验积累"产品都是清醒剂。Outer Wilds 的知识门控世界设计也让评测不可轻易刷分。

## English Summary

LLM agents are expected to improve across episodes with fixed weights, but existing benchmarks measure only one view: task progress, world-knowledge QA, or episodic memory. A correct answer does not show the agent ever observed evidence for it, and progress does not show what it learned. AstronautBench is built on the game Outer Wilds: each episode is a 22-minute loop after which the world resets, only knowledge carries over, and progress is gated by knowledge. After every episode it measures places reached and ship-log rumor facts revealed, correctness on 164 questions about the world, and whether each answer is supported by the frames and positions the agent cites from its own recorded play, checked by two LLM judges from different model families. Eight-episode baselines with GPT-6.1 Sol and Gemini 4 Argon planners show the three views do not move together: Sol's correct answers grow from 14.6% to 26.8%, but only 59-68% are evidence-supported at episode 8, no new rumor facts appear after episode 3, while places reached and memory keep growing.
