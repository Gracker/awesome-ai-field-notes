## English Abstract / Summary

Simon Willison runs the same SVG-of-a-pelican prompt across five reasoning efforts on Anthropic Fable 5.1, recording cost, output token count, latency, and reasoning style. low/medium cost ~$0.10 for 1.9k tokens in 24s with no reasoning trace; high is $0.13 for 2.6k tokens in 30s with brief reasoning; xhigh is 36.7k tokens in 7m51s for $1.83 and starts planning SVG geometry; max is 65.9k tokens in 13m54s for $3.30 with reasoning that visibly adjusts geometric control points and ships a finished scene (basket, fish, wings on handlebars). The piece is the cleanest 2026 quantification of the reasoning-effort price/quality curve.
