## 中文导读

越来越多抛给 LLM 的问题没有可对分的正确答案：一项政策值多少钱、用户该选哪个、互相冲突的价值观怎么权衡。陈述偏好经济学（stated-preference economics）几十年前就面对同样处境，发展出一套无需真值即可评判调查回答的效度框架：内容效度、结构效度、效标效度、信度、激励相容与后果性。作者论证这是评估 LLM 的通用方法，并逐概念落到 LLM 评估上；用 Vossler et al. 2023 已发表的水质陈述偏好调查测六个模型，效度检验变成经济学理论的预测：需求应向下倾斜，支付意愿应对商品范围与收入有响应。模型被尖锐地分层：两个较老模型在家庭收入 7.5 万美元档连最基本的检验都过不了，两个最新模型通过全部可打分的理论效度检验，却在 convergent validity 上分化。通过效度检验说明回答自洽，不说明正确。

## English Summary

Many questions now put to LLMs have no correct answer to score against: what a policy is worth, which option a user should choose, how to weigh competing values. Stated-preference economics has faced this for decades, judging survey responses without true values via a framework of content, construct, and criterion validity, reliability, incentive compatibility, and consequentiality. The authors argue this is a general method for evaluating language models and map each concept onto LLM evaluation, demonstrating with the published Vossler et al. 2023 water-quality stated-preference survey administered to six models, where validity tests become predictions from economic theory: demand should slope down, willingness to pay should respond to scope and income. Two older models fail the most basic test at a household income level of $75,000; the two newest pass every theoretical-validity test they can score but diverge on convergent validity. Passing validity tests shows a model's answers are coherent, not that they are correct.
