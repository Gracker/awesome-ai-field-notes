## English Abstract / Summary

Fernando Simes instruments a Turso database backend running io_uring + O_DIRECT to explain why an application-level readahead patch accelerated it. Without readahead, only one SQE is in flight at a time, the kernel cannot merge bios, and the device sees ~196,000 requests at near-zero %rrqm. With a 32-page readahead window, SQE count rises modestly to 218,212 but the device receives only ~16,300 requests at %rrqm 91-93% because adjacent sectors can now be coalesced. The sqqoll polling thread consumes 65% of cycles on a 4-vCPU box and disabling it cuts system time from 8.46s to 1.27s. Final hypothesis: O_DIRECT eliminates one CPU copy and the cache-warming side effect, costing ~4.8M cache misses versus the syscall path.
