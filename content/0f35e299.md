## English Summary

Ripple-65B Preview (Ricursive Intelligence, Nov 3, 2026) is a dense 65B model pretrained on 12T tokens with 1M context, claiming U.S. open-weight SOTA on agentic coding: 80.52 on TerminalBench 2.1, above Reflection Beam 501B (80.1), Meta Muse Spark Open 816B (76.4), Thinking Machines Inkling 975B (63.8), and NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Ultra 550B (56.4). Full R&D cost stated at $32M, roughly one-tenth of the prior open-weight SOTA; RL reached 80.5 with only 1.4M rollouts (~70x fewer than Reflection Beam) using truncated importance sampling, score centering, and a linear length penalty, with 40% MFU, simple sharding, no custom kernels, no quantization. It is the general-purpose variant of the proprietary model powering their AI chip-design platform; weights on Hugging Face, Together AI, GMI Cloud, OpenRouter.
