## English Summary

Anthropic has released Claude Sonnet 5, positioned as the most agentic Sonnet model yet: capable of planning, calling tools like browsers and terminals, and running autonomously. It substantially improves over Sonnet 4.6 on reasoning, tool use, coding, and knowledge work, narrowing the gap to Opus 4.8 at lower price. Safety evaluations show a lower rate of undesirable behaviors than Sonnet 4.6 and significantly lower cybersecurity ability than current Opus models. Sonnet 5 is the default model on Free and Pro plans, and is available on Max, Team, and Enterprise, plus Claude Code and the Claude Platform, with introductory pricing of $2 per million input tokens.
