## 中文导读

IBM CEO Arvind Krishna 在 Q3 财报电话会上向投资者论证开源权重模型已经适合企业本地部署：2024 年宽基准测试上闭源前沿模型能拿约 70 分，开源模型只有 20-30 分；2025 年底差距缩到 70 对 50；如今开源模型约有前沿 90% 的效果，token 成本只有前沿模型的 10-25%。他的算术很直接：总结邮件、审合同、接客服、写邮件这类任务，为最后 10% 的效果付 10 倍价钱不划算。Krishna 称 IBM 因此看到本地 AI 基础设施需求火爆，多数企业两三个机柜就能搭出可用系统，但他也提醒不少企业会卡在部署运维上。

## English Summary

On IBM's Q3 earnings call, CEO Arvind Krishna told investors that open weight models now make compelling sense for on-prem enterprise AI. In 2024 a broad-based benchmark put the top closed model near 70 while open models scored 20-30; by late 2025 the gap was 70 to 50, and open models now deliver roughly 90% of frontier effectiveness at 10-25% of the token cost. For everyday enterprise work - summarizing email, examining contracts, answering customer calls, composing email - he argues paying 10x more for the last 10% of quality rarely pays, so IBM sees booming interest in on-prem AI infrastructure that most businesses can fit in two or three racks, though many will struggle to get it running.
