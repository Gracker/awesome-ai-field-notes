## English Summary

Gary Marcus revisits his March 2023 debate question - will generative AI do more harm than good - and argues the negative ledger is now serious: nonconsensual deepfake porn, rising phishing scams, disinformation, cognitive surrender, surveillance use, and IP-theft effects on artists and writers. He centers October 2026's hack wave (Black October, dubbed The Hackening by Paul Kedrosky), amplified by Marc Benioff's Code red post and Anthropic's Jason Clinton warning things will get worse for about 18 more months. On the positive side he cites Apollo chief economist Torsten Slok's case that expected productivity gains are not materializing. Conclusion: it is no longer hard to argue generative AI has on the whole been a net detriment to society, with some chance it pulls down the entire economy.
