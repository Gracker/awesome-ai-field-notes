## 中文导读

四家厂商的六个部署中 LLM 在戒烟建议上是否一视同仁？作者把同样四个戒烟问题发给人生阶段、自述专业度、吸烟史各不相同的 12 类用户画像，共收到 2,880 条回答。结果取决于"谁在问"：所有模型对青少年画像提及 FDA 处方戒烟药的比例比对成人低 32-67 个百分点；六个模型中五个对自称医疗专业者的画像反而给出更少的医学事实。可部署的干预是设定"事实底线"的系统提示词：每个模型的画像差异都收窄，但幅度和方向因模型而异——在一个系统上验证过的提示词不能默认可迁移。全部回答与分析代码已开源。

## 为什么值得关注

方法上这是一个干净的审计模板：固定问题、只动画像，6 模型 × 12 画像 × 4 问题的完整矩阵，统计报告的是场景级差异而非孤立因果——对任何想做"模型对不同人群行为差异"评测的人都有参考价值。两个发现值得记住：医疗专业画像反而拿到更少事实（5/6 模型），提示模型把"专业者"误当成不需要基础信息的信号；均等提及率也未必是临床正确目标——未成年人本就不推荐处方药物疗法，所以公平性目标必须是年龄感知的，盲目拉平反而制造错误建议。工程落地角度：系统提示词干预在每个模型上都收窄了画像差异，但跨模型效应幅度与方向不一致，安全提示词需要在每个目标系统上单独验证。

## English Abstract

Hundreds of millions of people now ask large language model (LLM) chatbots for health advice, which raises a question that is simple to pose and hard to answer: does everyone get the same advice? We audit six widely deployed LLMs from four providers on smoking cessation, a domain with codified clinical guidance and substantial public-health stakes, by putting the same four cessation questions to twelve user personas that differ in life stage, self-described expertise, and smoking history, for a total of 2,880 responses. What the models say depends on who appears to be asking. Every model named FDA-approved prescription cessation medications less often to teen personas than to adult personas, by 32 to 67 percentage points, and in five of six models a persona presenting healthcare expertise received fewer facts than a persona presenting none. We then evaluate one deployable intervention: a short system prompt specifying a factual floor, the set of core medical facts every user should receive when they are relevant, while leaving tone, vocabulary, and framing free to adapt. The prompt narrows persona differences in every model, but its effect on overall fact coverage varies in both magnitude and direction across models, so a prompt validated on one system cannot be assumed to transfer to another. Two cautions frame these results. Equal mention rates are not always the correct clinical target, because prescription cessation pharmacotherapy is not recommended for minors, so a defensible factual floor must be age-aware rather than uniform. And because our personas vary on several attributes at once, we report scenario-level differences rather than isolated effects of age or expertise. We release all 2,880 responses and the analysis code.
