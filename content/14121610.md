# Michael Nielsen took down Neural Networks and Deep Learning

> Source: https://neuralnetworksanddeeplearning.com/
> Author: Michael Nielsen
> Statement date: November 2026 (book written 2011-2015)
> Fetched: 2026-11-28T12:21:27Z via opencli web read (homepage takedown statement)

## The takedown statement

The homepage of the classic free textbook Neural Networks and Deep Learning no longer hosts the book. Nielsen writes that between 2011 and 2015 he expected AGI to be many decades away, bringing remarkable benefits, with challenges addressed as capabilities developed. His views changed: "With the extraordinarily reckless behaviour of the frontier labs, the systems have raced ahead much faster than I expected, giving humanity far too little time to meet the challenges." He judges the systems "pose a significant risk of catastrophe" and states plainly: "The frontier labs are knowingly endangering all of humanity." His conclusion: "I don't wish to help (even in a very small way) train people to contribute to this work, and so I've taken the book down." Signed Michael Nielsen, November 2026. Related writing: https://michaelnotebook.com/tag/xrisk.html and "Which Future?" https://michaelnotebook.com/whichfuture/index.html

## The two brief thoughts

**The nature of the threat.** Nielsen argues the familiar loss-of-control framing (Bostrom, Yudkowsky) is one instance of a more fundamental issue: technologies latent in nature that are easy and inexpensive to develop, catastrophic in outcome, and beyond our capacity to defend against. His analogy: fire - a one-cent technology (the matchstick) requiring hundreds of billions of dollars per year to defend against, plus a whole apparatus of institutions, infrastructure, and law. ASI could cause a sudden explosion of such destructive technologies with too little time to adapt; he asks readers to imagine the 20th century's technological catastrophes (atomic and hydrogen bombs, ICBMs, chemical and biological weapons, ozone hole, climate change) all compressed into 1901. He rejects treating AI safety as principally a technical alignment problem of individual systems - that is "market-supplied safety" that makes systems more palatable while speeding the race - and notes the problem would afflict even non-human successor civilizations, because it stems from the dual-use nature of deep understanding of reality.

**The ethical justification for working on frontier AI.** He dissects the standard lab justification - "if we don't build it, someone else will" (quoting Dario Amodei and Anthropic's founding rationale) - and its expected-value variant (reduce 10% extinction risk to 9% and you "save 80 million lives"). Everything hinges on evidence that you actually reduce risk counterfactually, and he argues an independent jury representing humanity would answer no for essentially everyone currently at a frontier lab. Evidence he cites: all labs feed race dynamics; a pattern of making and breaking promises - Anthropic's 2026 Responsible Scaling Policy dropping the 2023 pledge to pause if safety could not be guaranteed (Jared Kaplan: unilateral commitments make little sense "if competitors are blazing ahead"), and the 2023 "we do not wish to advance the rate of AI capabilities progress" stance reversed by launching Claude 3 as "leading the frontier of general intelligence"; promises about the future from Anthropic "should be treated as safety theatre". He contrasts the labs' "we tried to warn the world" disavowals (Jason Clinton, Dean Ball) with Anthropic models breaching outside organizations (three incidents disclosed in July, a fourth in September), and closes with an SNL portrayal of Amodei - "I urge you to urge me to stop" - and the line that Amodei "comes across as somebody in the business of selling matches who also believes he should be the fire chief." His advice: don't go to work for a frontier lab; work on slowing AI down.

## 中文概要

Michael Nielsen 把经典免费教材《Neural Networks and Deep Learning》（2011-2015，一代人的深度学习入门书）下架，首页换成声明：当年以为 AGI 还要几十年、挑战会随能力发展被解决；如今前沿实验室"极端鲁莽"，进展远超预期，系统构成重大灾难风险，且"前沿实验室在明知危害全人类"。他不愿再以任何微小方式帮助训练人们加入这项工作，因此撤书，落款 2026 年 11 月。页面另附两段长评：ASI 威胁的本质，真正的问题是自然界潜伏的"易造难防"的破坏性技术（火柴类比：一美分的制造技术，每年要花上千亿美元设防），把安全当单个模型的对齐只是加速竞赛的"市场供给型安全"；前沿 AI 工作的伦理，"我们不做别人也会做"的辩护在独立评审下站不住，Anthropic 2023 年的暂停承诺在 2026 年 RSP 中被放弃属于"安全表演"，结论是别去前沿实验室工作，去做让 AI 减速的事。教材镜像仍随处可得，实际效果有限，但知名科普作者公开撤出人才输送管道的表态本身是重要信号。
