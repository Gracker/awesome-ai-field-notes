## 中文导读

用操作系统的 thrashing 比喻多 agent 并行疲劳：一个 agent 在跑，你反射式再开一个，像刷手机一样停不下来；agent 交回来你却要先弄懂它做了什么才能用——两小时后打开 diff 已想不起当初要什么，另一个 agent 又来叫，来回几次一天就过去。「我就问一问我的 agent」听着比开工单还轻，但同时认领了理解、检查、收尾三件事；任务变便宜于是越接越多，正是杰文斯悖论的形状。对策按挂起进程来：先判断值不值得现在接；把复核时间算进成本；先做完已开始的，别把「agent 说做完了」当「这件事做完了」。

## English Summary

Using the OS concept of thrashing, this post explains multi-agent fatigue: one agent runs, you reflexively start another, and when results come back you must understand what was done before using any of it - open the diff two hours later, forget what you asked for, and another agent wants attention now. "I'll just ask my agent" feels lighter than a ticket but volunteers you to understand, check and finish whatever comes back; cheaper tasks mean more tasks - Jevons paradox in practice. Remedies mirror suspending processes: decide if it's worth taking on now, budget your own review time, finish what you started, and never treat "the agent is done" as "the task is done".

## 原文摘录 / Evidence Excerpts

There's this concept in Computer Science called thrashing. ... at some point, so much time goes into moving things around that the programs barely make any real progress.

One agent is working, and you, almost by reflex, will start something else. Kind of like doomscrolling: you don't even realize you're doing it.

Because "I'll just ask my agent" feels like such a small commitment — and easier than creating a ticket, right? — that we forget we're also volunteering to understand, check, and finish whatever comes back.

We don't need to keep 10 agents busy all day every day just because we can; that feels like productivity but can leave us spending the whole day trying to catch up with work we asked for ourselves.
