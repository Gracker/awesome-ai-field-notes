## English Summary

Anil Dash distills Meta's decades-old five-step playbook into a citable list: launch a feature that obviously violates privacy or consent, get caught, issue a press release 'pausing' it while explaining it wasn't really that bad, wait for attention to shift, then relaunch and continue. Internally the relaunch is taken as proof the complaints were overblown, part of Silicon Valley's 'inevitability' strategy. He notes it is no coincidence that many former Facebook PMs work at OpenAI and ChatGPT has run the same playbook on privacy and trust. His landing point: no immediate fix, but naming the pattern is the basis for accountability when it repeats.
