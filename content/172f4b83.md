## English Summary

Following up his 2025 worry that AX would become software makers' top priority, Jim Nielsen observes the field's posture shifting from 'make this good for people to use' to 'people should use their agents to access our stuff' — 'is this accessible' has become 'can an AI agent access it'. He fears release conversations like 'does it work for AI? Yes, but not for you personally. Ship it — does anyone still do this themselves?' and support scripts like 'have you tried your agent?'. The precedent already exists: we built a world that excludes people without smartphones, and his parents get stuck on boarding passes, supermarket coupons, and identity checks. In ten to twenty years, 'have you downloaded our app' may become 'have you connected your agent'; the agent path is cheaper and draws fewer complaints, so it eats the human path.
