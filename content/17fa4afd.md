## 摘要（中文）

Gruber 解读欧委会 DMA 对 Google 的两套约束：Search 数据共享，以及 Android 端 AI 互通。后者要求第三方 AI 助手接近 Gemini 的系统能力——硬件键唤醒、读屏、传感器、后台常驻、并发热词/端侧 DSP 模型、跨 App 操作等，范围远超「多一个默认助手」。作者推演 Google 可能路径：高成本做 API 但大厂不接、接盘后隐私/耗电翻车、顺利互通、或在欧盟收缩系统级 Gemini。对 Android/端侧 Agent 产品与权限边界判断价值高。

## Summary (English)

John Gruber on EC DMA specification measures for Google: Search data sharing plus Android AI interoperability. The AI measures require APIs so third-party assistants can match Gemini-class on-device capabilities—hardware-button invocation, screen capture, sensor access, unrestricted background execution, always-on hotword models on DSP, cross-app actions, and more. Gruber walks plausible outcomes: costly APIs that big players ignore, privacy/battery blowups if they adopt, successful PC-like background software on phones, or Google downgrading system Gemini in the EU. High-signal OS/product read for on-device agents.

## One-liner
