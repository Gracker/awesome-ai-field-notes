## 中文导读

RL 后训练能明显改善扩散 transformer 的视觉质量、文字渲染和组合性，这些能力依赖 prompt，直觉上需要学复杂的输入相关变换。论文用最简单的参数化做隔离实验：在冻结基模型的 block 输出上加一组固定 steering 向量。SD3.5-M 上向量参数量只有 RL-tuned LoRA 的约 1/65（0.29M 对 18.8M），拟合公开 RL checkpoint 的速度预测、目标里没有 reward，却对每个 prompt、每个 token、每个采样种子完全相同——仍恢复了六个 RL 图像模型 90.0-96.3% 的 reward 增益，两个 RL 视频模型上是 89.8% 和 111.9%。零初始化加在线 RL 时，单靠向量就在文字渲染和人类偏好上超过权重级 RL。结论：policy-gradient RL 后训练的增益大头是一个常量偏移，把预训练生成器转向它本来就会算的东西。

## 为什么值得关注

"RL 后训练到底学到了什么"是后训练机理的核心问题，这篇用 1/65 参数的固定向量拿到九成以上增益，等于说大部分 RL 后训练没有学新的输入相关变换。对做图像/视频模型后训练的团队，这意味着先用激活转向做 baseline 可能便宜得多；对可解释性方向，这是 DiT 上 steering 机制的强证据。

## 关键信息

- 标题：RL Post-Training in Diffusion Transformers Is Activation Steering
- arXiv：2611.26627，2026-11-19，cs.CV/cs.LG；项目页 rl-is-steering.github.io
- 实验设置：冻结基模型 block 输出加固定 steering 向量组，拟合公开 RL-tuned checkpoint 的速度预测，目标函数无 reward
- 参数对比：0.29M vs RL-tuned LoRA 18.8M（约 1/65）

## English Summary

RL post-training substantially improves visual quality, text rendering, and compositional accuracy of diffusion transformers. Because these capabilities are inherently prompt-dependent, the natural hypothesis is that improving them requires complex input-dependent transformations. The paper isolates how much an input-independent shift explains using a deliberately simple parameterization: a fixed set of steering vectors added to block outputs of the frozen base model. On SD3.5-M the vectors hold about 65x fewer parameters than the RL-tuned LoRA (0.29M vs 18.8M); they are fit to match velocity predictions of public RL-tuned checkpoints with no reward in the objective, yet are identical for every prompt, token, and sampling seed. They still recover 90.0-96.3% of the reward gains of six RL-tuned image models, and 89.8% and 111.9% on two RL-tuned video models. Initialized at zero and trained with online RL, vectors alone exceed weight-based RL on text rendering and human preference. Most of policy-gradient RL post-training's gain is a constant shift steering the pretrained generator toward what it can already compute.
