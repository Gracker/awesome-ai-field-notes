## English Summary

In Anthropic's IPO roadshow video, Dario Amodei said cancer, Alzheimer's, heart disease and mental illness will be things of the past in less than ten years. Gary Marcus calls it Elizabeth Holmes-grade overpromise and argues it deserves securities-fraud scrutiny, noting no mainstream outlet has dismantled it. The strongest rebuttal comes from Eli Lilly chair/CEO Dave Ricks: most of biology's map is blank, randomized human trials remain the longest pole, and AI-driven progress will likely be steady and incremental. Other experts supply numbers: biomedical data covers under 1%, and the last 20 approved drugs averaged 14 years (median 10) from first-in-human to FDA approval - even starting today, the median path misses a ten-year promise.
