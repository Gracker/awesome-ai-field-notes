## English Summary

Full text and annotated slides of Simon Willison's closing keynote at WeAreDevelopers World Congress NA: a month-by-month retrospective of LLMs from November 2025 to September 2026. Milestones: Nov 2025 as the inflection when coding agents became daily-usable (Claude Code + Opus 4.5, Codex + GPT-5.1); Jan 2026 OpenClaw arrives 8,300 commits in under two months, over 100,000 today, 'the most vibe-coded piece of software in existence' plus the Mac-mini-as-aquarium digital-pet moment; his own January 'AI mania' (any time your agent isn't building something feels like wasted time) and Deep Blue, the AI-induced ennui of software engineers coined with Adam Leventhal and Bryan Cantrill on Oxide and Friends, as the year's throughline. Anchored throughout by the evolving pelican-on-a-bicycle prompt; talk video included.
