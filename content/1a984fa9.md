## 中文导读

视频前瞻推理要求 VLM 从当前画面预测未来事件或下一步动作。现有做法拟合未来字幕、理据或完整视觉状态，有两个瓶颈：依赖标注或外部大模型做监督，可扩展性受限；把整段未来当等价值信号，尽管大部分是无关上下文。Future-OPSD 用 on-policy 自蒸馏把过去-未来视频对变成天然自监督信号，无需额外标注或 LLM 监督；再定义 foresight score，得到对比性 privilege advantage，驱动模型只学最有价值的未来信息。分数本身不可解，论文优化一个可解的下界替代：在真实未来与随机参照未来之间插值该优势，再叠加师生蒸馏优势，放大决策相关信息、压制无关上下文。开放预测和封闭规划分别比基座提升最高 14.2 和 15.9 点，通用视频理解不受损。

## English Summary

Video foresight reasoning requires vision-language models to anticipate future events or next actions from observed videos. Existing methods fit future captions, rationales, or full visual states, with two limitations: they rely on annotations or external large models for supervision, and they treat the whole future clip as equally valuable although most of it is irrelevant context. Future-OPSD turns past-future video pairs into a natural self-supervised signal via on-policy self-distillation, requiring no extra annotations or LLM supervision. It defines a foresight score yielding a contrastive privilege advantage that drives the model to learn the most valuable future information. Since the score is intractable, the method optimizes a tractable lower-bound surrogate that interpolates this advantage between the true and a random reference future, combined with a teacher-student distillation advantage, amplifying decision-relevant content and suppressing irrelevant context. Future-OPSD improves base models by up to 14.2 points on open-ended prediction and 15.9 on closed-ended planning without compromising general video understanding.
