**[Introducing Our Smallest, Most Efficient Model: Muse Ember](https://research.meta.ai/blog/muse-ember)**. Meta AI just launched their entry in the Claude Haiku/GPT Luna category: Muse Ember, which they describe as:

> \[...\] our smallest and most efficient model designed for complex reasoning execution and the work agents repeat all day: tool calls, coding subtasks, routing, extraction, and long document parsing.

For shorter prompts it's priced the same as GPT-6 Luna and Claude Haiku 5.5 - both are $0.10/million input tokens and $0.50/million output. Today's other big model release, Claude Fable 5.2, is _80x that price_ at $8/$40.

The pricing is a little less straightforward as prompts get longer, though. Luna, Ember, and Haiku all support up to a million input tokens, but they also all have a threshold after which the price goes up. Taking _that_ threshold into account Ember is priced between the two ...

## English Summary

Meta shipped Muse Ember on Nov 10, its smallest model aimed at high-frequency agent work: tool calls, coding subtasks, routing, extraction, long-document parsing. Simon Willison's price table: at short prompts it matches GPT-6 Luna and Claude Haiku 5.5 at $0.10/M input and $0.50/M output, with cached input at $0.004/M - a quarter of the others; past long-prompt thresholds Ember jumps at 200k tokens to $0.30/$1.50 (Luna at 272k, Haiku 5.5 at 100k). Artificial Analysis per-task costs put Ember xhigh at ~34 points for $0.12 and max at ~36 for $0.14, both on the Pareto line, while Haiku 5.5 xhigh scores ~34.5 for $0.25, pushed off the curve. For agents making tens of thousands of tool calls daily, Ember max undercuts Haiku xhigh by ~40% at near-equal intelligence.
