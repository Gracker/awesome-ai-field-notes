## 中文摘要

论文提出 RECONTEXT（Recursive Evidence Replay as LLM Harness for Long-Context Reasoning），一种无需训练的长上下文推理增强方法它利用模型内部的相关性信号构建查询条件下的证据池，在最终生成前 replay 一次，同时保留原始完整上下文作者还从联想记忆角度给出理论分析，把上下文视为记忆存储问题视为检索线索attention 视为线索-痕迹关联replay 视为痕迹再激活在 8 个 128K 长上下文数据集上的实验显示 RECONTEXT 一致提升多种基线模型的表现

## English Abstract

Proposes RECONTEXT, a training-free inference method that improves long-context reasoning by using model-internal relevance signals to construct a query-conditioned evidence pool and replaying it before final generation, while preserving the full original context. The recursive selection separates evidence organization from answer generation without training, external memory, or context pruning. A theoretical analysis frames the context as a memory store, the question as a retrieval cue, attention as cue-trace association, and replay as trace reactivation. Experiments on eight 128K long-context datasets show consistent gains over strong baselines.
