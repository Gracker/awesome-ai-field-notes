## English Source Notes

As of November 2026, an explosion in bespoke inference engines has taken place, inspired by llama.cpp, SGLang, and vLLM. They share three traits: focus on a specific subset of models and/or hardware, use a frontier model to vibe-code the entire thing, and chase ever-higher token generation speeds. Results are striking (Strata runs a heavily-quantized 180B model on a 16GB VRAM card), but the chase has taken an unfortunate turn: optimizations are less about hardware-specific changes and more about changing how the model thinks so it goes faster. Strata, for example, prefers polling experts loaded in VRAM even when experts pushed to system RAM/disk would normally have been chosen. The author recommends llama-cpp-rdna-boosts instead: a minimal patchset applied against a pinned llama.cpp commit, continually re-applied to newer versions, with AMD-specific improvements. Measured gains: token generation from 35 to 55 tokens/second, prefill from 700 to 1300 tokens per second. Pre-built images: ghcr.io/stew675/llama-cpp-rdna-boosts:server-rocm-10.1.
