## English Summary

Consultant dermatologist Magnus Lynch tested GPT-6 Astra and Claude Fable 5.1 against a fine-tuned Inception v3 CNN (architecture first published by Google in 2015, fine-tuned for about twenty minutes on public dermatology images) on 200 biopsy-proven lesions drawn from MILK10k: 100 not safe to discharge (30 melanomas, 25 basal cell carcinomas, 25 squamous cell carcinomas, 20 actinic keratoses) and 100 safe to discharge. The CNN named the right diagnosis for 125 of 200 (62%) versus 84 and 85 (42%) for the two frontier LLMs; both LLMs misread an invasive ear melanoma under 0.8 mm as a seborrhoeic keratosis and recommended discharge, while the CNN was correct from the dermoscopic view. The lesions were never seen in tuning and were not used to choose the model or set its threshold; scoring was done by script against histology.
