## 中文导读

Jeremy Stern 给 Colossus 写的长篇 Zuck 传（约 1.5 万字），从 ZuckNet、12 岁游戏崩溃后哭完发誓重建 optimized version，讲到早年 Facebook 危机会上喊 Carthago delenda est、全员会喊 Domination、2024 Connect 穿 AUT ZUCK AUT NIHIL。最有判断密度的是 AI 战略段：Stern 写道 Zuck 把 AI 当产品而非上帝——AI 做的是数学和解有限问题，否则只是 useful & cool；他会用 ads 现金流加自有的数据中心、芯片等 Anthropic/OpenAI 必须付租的基础设施在价格上 undercut 对手；潜在装机量是 36 亿不在乎模型落后 frontier 六个月的人；若 AI 商品化，Anthropic/OpenAI 价值归零、价值沉淀到 Meta 已支配的互补品（分发、注意力、个性化、商务）；若不商品化，他至少摆脱对 Apple 的依赖。

## English Summary

Jeremy Stern's ~15,000-word Colossus profile of Mark Zuckerberg runs from ZuckNet and the childhood Risk-game rebuild vow to Carthago delenda est crisis speeches, 'Domination!' all-hands, and the AUT ZUCK AUT NIHIL shirt at Connect 2024. The densest judgment is his AI-strategy section: Zuckerberg treats AI as a product, not a god - it does math and solves bounded problems, else it is useful & cool; he will use ads cash flow plus owned data centers and chips that Anthropic and OpenAI must pay to rent, to undercut them on price; his potential install base is 3.6 billion people who don't care whether a model is six months behind the frontier; if AI commoditizes, Anthropic and OpenAI go to zero and value accrues to complements Meta already dominates - distribution, attention, personalization, commerce.

T**here they are.** The Zuckerbergs.

Both in black slacks and plain shoes. Both in zanily patterned shirts that look made to wick sweat. No heavy watches, no bright jewelry. They look you in the eye and don’t break contact, not even to sample the spa water or crudités provided by the Company on the glass table between us. He is a ham; she is shy. They speak, to my surprise, in unreconstructed New York accents. I don’t know what I expected them to be like, but for whatever reason this wasn’t it.

“Awl my children are amazing to me,” says Karen, when asked what it’s like.

“He didn’t stand out as the bright one in the family,” says Ed. “If anyone I would guess our middle dawtah Donna was considered the ‘nerd’ or the smart one in the family.”

“We just had four really bright, successful kids. We’re equally proud of all of them,” says Ed. “He just happened to be in the right place at the right time.
