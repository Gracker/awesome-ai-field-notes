## 中文导读

Kimi K3.1 Pro 发布：K3.1 系列首个模型，Moonshot 称其为当前最强 3T 级开源权重模型，1M token 上下文；Highspeed 版速度 2-3 倍，完整权重 11 月 4 日前放出。它是 Kimi K3 的大规模后训练版本：跨数百个下游域做联合 RL，并把 RL 框架、长上下文推理、训练效率、沙箱设施、rollout 调度和环境质量检查平台全栈优化，让每份 RL 算力有稳定回报。Terminal-Bench 4.0 得 65.7%（GPT-6 Astra 58.2%，Opus 5.5 66.4%），Kimi Code Bench 3.0 Long-horizon 从 K3 的 55.95% 升到 61.2%，BenchCAD 带工具 92.7%，OfficeQA Pro v2 拿到 64.4 的 SOTA；Kimi Hallu Bench Hard 上 claim 级幻觉率从 K3 的 23.4% 降到 8.3%。安全是这篇最值得记录的部分：开源版在中训语料与 RL 环境两处识别并清除网络攻击相关数据，xdev-bench-hard pass@3 从未清洗检查点的 90.5% 降到 81.0%——开放权重的攻击能力被刻意压在前沿之下，同时作者承认更强的通用编码与长程调试能力仍会外溢到攻击任务。

## English Summary

Moonshot introduces Kimi K3.1 Pro, the first model of the K3.1 series and its most capable 3T-class open-weight model, with a 1M-token context window; a Highspeed variant ships 2-3x the speed, and full weights are due by Nov 4, 2026. It is a post-trained Kimi K3 with substantially larger post-training compute: large-scale joint RL across hundreds of downstream domains, with the RL framework, long-context inference, training efficiency, sandbox infrastructure, and rollout scheduling optimized so each increment of RL compute yields stable returns. It scores 65.7% on Terminal-Bench 4.0 (GPT-6 Astra 58.2%, Claude Opus 5.5 66.4%), 61.2% on Kimi Code Bench 3.0 Long-horizon (K3: 55.95%), 92.7% on BenchCAD with tools, and SOTA 64.4 on OfficeQA Pro v2; claim-level hallucination on Kimi Hallu Bench Hard falls from K3's 23.4% to 8.3%. For the open-weight release, cyber-related data was identified and removed from both mid-training and RL environments: xdev-bench-hard pass@3 falls from 90.5% (uncleaned checkpoint) to 81.0%, deliberately keeping open-weight cyber capability below the frontier - though the authors note stronger general coding and long-horizon debugging still generalizes to cyber tasks.
