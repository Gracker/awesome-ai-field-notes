## English Summary

Simon Willison's 8/28 post on Anil Madhavapeddy's (Cambridge professor, OCaml compiler core maintainer) note: ten minutes after a private OCaml patch discussion went out, his own site was probed with percent-encoded traversal attacks proof that automated watchers are monitoring public repos. Anil reproduced this with his own agent: a task Claude Fable refused, DeepSeek V4 Pro picked up and exploited quickly. rclone maintainer Nick Craig-Wood commented with concrete numbers: ~20 security disclosures via GitHub in rclone's first decade, 40+ in the past month, ~75% containing real actionable issues. CVE numbers now take 3-4 weeks (vs 2-3 days pre-AI), forcing him to write 'CVE-PENDING' in the changelog....
