## 中文摘要

Addy Osmani 把 agentic engineering 的风险收敛到三个工程责任：上线前的质量证据 (Quality)、进入依赖系统前的裁决 (Verdict)、以及运行中的可解释性 (Answerability)。模型负责产生动作（capability），工程师负责决策、验证、批准和拥有结果 (agency)。文章还指出三个隐藏成本：认知投降（盲目接受 AI 输出）、认知债务（理解力侵蚀）、编排税（注意力无法并行）。引用 Anthropic 随机对照实验：用 AI 写代码的工程师在理解力测试中比手写者低 17 个百分点（50% vs 67%）。

## English Summary

Addy Osmani proposes Quality, Verdict, and Answerability as three engineering responsibilities for agent systems. Engineers own the outer loop—deciding what to ship, what to block, and being able to explain why. Three hidden costs: cognitive surrender, cognitive debt, and orchestration tax. Cites Anthropic RCT showing AI-assisted engineers scored 17 percentage points lower on comprehension.

## 推荐语

Agent 工程三支柱：Quality（质量证据）Verdict（裁决）Answerability（可解释性）
