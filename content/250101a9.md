## Summary (English)

Gary Marcus compresses the AI-bubble-unravelling narrative into 24 hours: Sam Altman's Aug 18 tweet admitting a pause on some frontier RL training; WSJ's Berber Jin revealing OpenAI grew Q2 revenue just 18% QoQ to $6.7B while losses widened by $3B to $12.3B - every new dollar of revenue costing three of losses; and NVIDIA's same-day disclosure that its 5GW Ohio data-center backstop guarantees up to $105B of built asset value rather than subsidizing compute. Marcus reads the convergence as deep partners hedging themselves ahead of the IPO.
