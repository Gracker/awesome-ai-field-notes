## 摘要 (Summary)

针对深度搜索 agent 强化学习中只给轨迹级奖励致'暴露支撑文档的动作无 credit'的 reward-credit mismatch，提出 STAMP：reference-based verifier 判被引文档是否支持训练时 evidence graph 的实体/关系，first-exposure attribution 把被支持的引用回追到第一次取出它的动作，step credit 通过 sign-preserving advantage modulation 注入，不改变轨迹级奖励也不破坏组内排序在 BrowseComp / BrowseComp-ZH / xbench-DS 比 GRPO 基线 +2.0/+5.5/+3.0 分，与纯 outcome / citation-rubric 奖励均可叠加；消融证实 provenance credit 与 sign-preserving 调制各自贡献

## English Abstract / Excerpt

Reinforcement learning for deep-search agents has largely focused on trajectory-level scoring -- outcome correctness, citation-aware rewards, and evidence coverage. Yet the actions that expose supporting documents receive no targeted credit, a gap we call the reward-credit mismatch. We propose STAMP, in which a reference-based verifier judges whether each cited document supports an entity or relation in a training-time evidence graph, and first-exposure attribution traces each supported citation back to the action that first surfaced it. This step credit is injected through sign-preserving advantage modulation, which redistributes advantage across steps without changing the trajectory-level reward or the relative ranking of trajectories within each group....
