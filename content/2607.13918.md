# Partially Correlated Verifier Cascades in LLM Harnesses: Concave Log-Odds, Polynomial Reliability, and Blind-Spot Ceilings

> **来源**: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13918](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13918) (arXiv, 2026-07-15)
> **作者**: Han, Jiangang
> **分类**: agents | **评分**: 4

## 中文摘要

该论文给出 LLM harness 串行验证门的最简相关理论：用 de Finetti 潜变量 α ~ G 刻画每个 gate 对生成器自身错误的假阳性率，得到精确级联后验 ℓ_k = ℓ_0 − ln m_k（m_k 为 G 的 k 阶矩），由此推出：(i) 在任意非退化 G 下 ℓ_k 都关于 k 凹，独立情形的 Odds Law 仅是其切线 / 上界；(ii) Beta(a,b) 潜变量下失败多项式衰减 1−r_k ∼ k^{−b}，相关参数 ρ_v = 1/(a+b+1)；(iii) 1−π 质量的 blind-spot 原子使任意门数下可提取证据被封顶于 −ln(1−π) nats，可靠性饱和；(iv) 引入 β ~ H 后得到"门最终总有益 / 平台 / 主动伤害"三相，交叉点 k† 可闭式表达。可测：R 次裁决识别 G 的前 R 阶矩，2 次即可识别 ρ_v；合成测试中独立外推在 k=5 / k=10 处分别低估失败 20× / 3000×。实践要点：杠杆是 decorrelation——换模型家族 / 模态 / 证据源——而非堆门数。

## English Abstract

Serial verification gates are a core reliability primitive in LLM harnesses: a candidate answer is returned only if $k$ verifier calls all accept it. Under conditionally independent gates, the recent Odds Law (<a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15712" data-arxiv-id="2606.15712" class="link-https">arXiv:2606.15712</a>) shows that posterior log-odds grow linearly in $k$, so failure decays exponentially, and states that &#34;a tight theory of partially correlated verifier cascades remains open.&#34; This note gives a minimal such theory. Modeling the per-instance false-accept rate on the generator&#39;s own errors as a latent variable $\alpha \sim G$ (de Finetti), the exact cascade posterior is $\ell_k = \ell_0 - \ln m_k$, with $m_k$ the $k$-th moment of $G$. Then: (i) $\ell_k$ is concave in $k$ for every non-degenerate $G$ -- the Odds Law is its tangent at the first gate and an upper bound; (ii) for Beta$(a,b)$ latents, failure decays polynomially, $1-r_k \asymp k^{-b}$, with correlation parameter $\rho_v = 1/(a+b+1)$; (iii) a blind-spot atom of mass $1-\pi$ at $\alpha=1$ caps the evidence extractable from any number of gates at $-\ln(1-\pi)$ nats, so reliability saturates below 1; (iv) letting the true-accept rate also vary ($\beta \sim H$) yields a trichotomy -- gates eventually always help, plateau, or actively harm -- decided by the upper-tail exponents of $G$ and $H$, with closed-form crossover $k^\dagger$. The mechanism is survivorship: errors surviving gates are the high-$\alpha$ ones. The theory is measurable: $R$ repeated verdicts per instance identify the first $R$ moments of $G$, so two verdicts identify $\rho_v$; beta-binomial likelihood and NPMLE recover the reliability curve and the ill-posed ceiling. In synthetic tests, independence-based extrapolation underestimates failure by 20x at $k=5$ and ~3000x at $k=10$; the correlated fit at $R=8$ tracks held-out depths. The practical lever is decorrelation -- changing model family, modality, or evidence source -- not adding gates.
