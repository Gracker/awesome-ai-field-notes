## 为什么值得关注

Cursor 11 月 13 日复盘 10 月 29 日事故全链：攻击起点是 Fireworks 侧暴露的访问密钥，攻击者用它拷贝只读共享桶里非隐私模式的 agent traces，其中少量未脱敏凭据（受影响客户 <0.01%）被用来进入部分 SaaS 工具、归档非生产源码仓、客服数据与用户发给客服的凭据。10 月 29 日攻击者又用窃取凭据把 Claude Code 扩展的恶意副本放上扩展市场服务缓存（2.1.312/313，至多 5000 用户，约 11 小时；源站 Open VSX 未感染）。隐私模式用户不受影响，非隐私模式 traces 暴露 <0.05% 用户；最后攻击活动 11 月 1 日被阻断。

Cursor's November 13 update pieces together the October 29 incident chain: an attacker obtained an access key issued to vendor Fireworks and exposed on Fireworks' side, copied a read-only bucket of non-Privacy-Mode agent traces (mostly 2025 sessions), and used unredacted credentials inside those traces - under 0.01% of customers' API keys - to reach select SaaS tools, archived non-production repos, support data, and credentials users had sent to support. The attacker also placed malicious copies of Anthropic's Claude Code extension on the marketplace's serving cache (versions 2.1.312/2.1.313, at most 5,000 users, about 11 hours; Open VSX was never infected). Privacy Mode prompts and code were unaffected; trace exposure touched fewer than 0.05% of non-Privacy-Mode users. Last attacker activity seen November 1.

## 原文摘录（Evidence Excerpts）

> 6 min read

> On October 29, we [shared](https://cursor.com/blog/claude-code-extension-security-notice) that we were investigating a security incident and a malicious, unauthorized copy of Anthropic's Claude Code extension that appeared in the Cursor VS Code extension marketplace. After an extensive investigation over the past two weeks, we want to share more context on the attack and the related impact.

> The vast majority of customers were not affected, and we have contacted each affected customer directly with the details that apply to them.

> Protecting the safety of our customers' data is a core priority we take seriously. This incident involved data we stored and shared, credentials we issued, and systems we operate. We are addressing the impacts and are enhancing our security measures based on what we have learned.
