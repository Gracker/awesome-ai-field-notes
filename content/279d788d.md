## English

> Source: https://quesma.com/blog/qwen-36-is-awesome (Piotr Migdał, June 29, 2026)

I've been disappointed by local models in the past. But then I checked Qwen 3.6, and I was in awe. For me it's the first local model that actually makes sense as a general intelligence.

It comes in two variants: a mixture-of-experts model **Qwen 3.6 35B A3B**, and a dense **Qwen 3.6 27B** — slower, but more powerful. The one I recommend!

**It's hot, literally.** When my knees started to melt, I grabbed a phone-attached thermal camera and took a photo. Qwen 3.6 rightfully got a lot of coverage on Hacker News. The most common statement about Qwen 3.6 27B is that it punches above its weight.

**Testing the waters.** Simon Willison uses "penguins on a bicycle" as a smoke test. The author goes with constrained writing — asked it about quantum mechanics, then to write an 8-line poem about Zouk dance and quantum physics (it worked, with deliberate rhymes). In OpenCode, it created a hexagonal minesweeper with `pnpm` on the first go from a single prompt. The MoE variant was faster but ignored the package instruction.

**Real work.** It handled a candle-shop landing page prompt from AI Tinkerers Warsaw — worked for a few minutes and produced a reactive, default-decent page from a single short prompt.

**Running Qwen 3.6 locally with llama.cpp.** Recommend llama.cpp directly (not Ollama) on ethical grounds. Steps: grab an 8-bit GGUF from Hugging Face (e.g. `unsloth/Qwen3.6-27B-MTP-GGUF:Q8_0` with multi-token prediction), then:

```
llama-server -hf unsloth/Qwen3.6-27B-MTP-GGUF:Q8_0 \
    --spec-type draft-mtp -ngl 999 -fa on -c 65536 --port 8080
```

Where `-ngl 999` puts all layers on the GPU, `-fa on` enables flash attention, `-c 65536` is the 64k context (Qwen 3.6 27B natively supports 256k). Hook into OpenCode by adding a `provider.llama` block pointing at `http://127.0.0.1:8080/v1`.

**Performance benchmarks (MacBook Max M5, 128 GB):**

| Model | Backend | tokens/s | RAM |
|-------|---------|----------|-----|
| Qwen3.6-35B-A3B 8-bit | MLX | 85 | 37 GB |
| Qwen3.6-35B-A3B 8-bit | llama.cpp | 93 | 44 GB |
| Qwen3.6-35B-A3B 8-bit | llama.cpp + MTP | **105** | 45 GB |
| Qwen3.6-27B 8-bit | MLX | 17 | 28 GB |
| Qwen3.6-27B 8-bit | llama.cpp | 18 | 41 GB |
| Qwen3.6-27B 8-bit | llama.cpp + MTP | **32** | 42 GB |
| DeepSeek-V4-Flash Q2–Q4 | llama.cpp | 33 | 103 GB |

Both Qwen 3.6 variants run within 48 GB of Apple Silicon unified memory. On a 5090 with Q6_K quantization and Q4_0 KV cache at 123k context, one HN user hit 50 tok/s consistently using ~28/32 GB VRAM. While 35B A3B is ~3× faster, the author prefers 27B: "I'd rather generate a third as much code, but of higher quality."

**Versus frontier closed-source.** Per Artificial Analysis: Gemma 4 31B ≈ late 2024 (≈ o1 / Claude 3.5 Sonnet, index 29); Qwen 3.6 35B A3B ≈ early 2025 (≈ o3 / Claude 4 Sonnet, 32); **Qwen 3.6 27B ≈ mid 2025 (≈ GPT-5 / Claude Sonnet 4.5, 37)**; DeepSeek V4 Flash ≈ late 2025 (≈ GPT-5.2 / Claude Opus 4.5, 40).

**What's next.** A locally set-up model can be fine-tuned to your needs and cannot be taken away. Businesses can use them for proprietary data; individuals for offline projects or sensitive medical data. With frontier-level open-weight GLM 5.2 now runnable locally on company budgets (not a MacBook, not a single RTX 5090), "we are entering a fascinating era, when it becomes feasible to run one's own models." Current models combine raw intelligence and factual knowledge in the same weights; future models will separate the two, offloading knowledge to tool calling.

## 中文

> 来源：https://quesma.com/blog/qwen-36-is-awesome（Piotr Migdał，2026-06-29）

我以前对本地模型一直挺失望的，但试过 Qwen 3.6 之后真的被震住了。对我来说，它是第一个真正能作为"通用智能"使用的本地模型。

它有两个版本：MoE 架构的 **Qwen 3.6 35B A3B**，以及密集型的 **Qwen 3.6 27B**——后者更慢但更强大，是我推荐的版本。

**真的烫——字面意义上的烫**。当我的膝盖快要被烤化时，我掏出手机接的热成像仪拍了张照片。Qwen 3.6 在 Hacker News 上热度很高，社区对 27B 版最常见的评价是"以小博大"（punches above its weight）。

**试水。** Simon Willison 用"骑自行车的企鹅"做冒烟测试，作者习惯用"受限写作"——先让它聊量子力学，再让它写一首八行的、关于 Zouk 舞和量子物理的诗（结果还真能押韵，思考过程也站得住）。在 OpenCode 里，让它用 `pnpm` 做一个六边形扫雷，从一条 Prompt 一把过。MoE 版本更快，但忽略了我"做成 npm 包"的指令，只做了一个单 `index.html`。

**真正的工作。** 处理一条来自 AI Tinkerers Warsaw 的"蜡烛店着陆页"Prompt——跑了几分钟就交出了一份有交互、默认值合理的页面，全部从一条简短的 Prompt 起步。

**用 llama.cpp 在本地跑 Qwen 3.6。** 出于伦理考虑，作者直接推荐 llama.cpp，不推荐 Ollama。步骤：从 Hugging Face 拉一份 8-bit GGUF（比如带 MTP 的 `unsloth/Qwen3.6-27B-MTP-GGUF:Q8_0`），然后：

```
llama-server -hf unsloth/Qwen3.6-27B-MTP-GGUF:Q8_0 \
    --spec-type draft-mtp -ngl 999 -fa on -c 65536 --port 8080
```

其中 `-ngl 999` 把全部层都丢进 GPU，`-fa on` 开启 Flash Attention，`-c 65536` 是 64k 上下文（Qwen 3.6 27B 原生支持 256k）。再把 OpenCode 的 `provider.llama` 指向 `http://127.0.0.1:8080/v1` 即可对接。

**性能基准（MacBook Max M5, 128 GB）：**

| 模型 | 后端 | tokens/s | 占用内存 |
|-------|------|----------|----------|
| Qwen3.6-35B-A3B 8-bit | MLX | 85 | 37 GB |
| Qwen3.6-35B-A3B 8-bit | llama.cpp | 93 | 44 GB |
| Qwen3.6-35B-A3B 8-bit | llama.cpp + MTP | **105** | 45 GB |
| Qwen3.6-27B 8-bit | MLX | 17 | 28 GB |
| Qwen3.6-27B 8-bit | llama.cpp | 18 | 41 GB |
| Qwen3.6-27B 8-bit | llama.cpp + MTP | **32** | 42 GB |
| DeepSeek-V4-Flash Q2–Q4 | llama.cpp | 33 | 103 GB |

两个 Qwen 3.6 变体都能在 48 GB Apple Silicon 统一内存内跑起来；4-bit 量化后小于 18 GB，能上 32 GB 的设备。5090 上用 Q6_K + Q4_0 KV cache 在 123k 上下文能稳定 50 tok/s，占 ~28/32 GB VRAM。35B A3B 虽然快 3 倍，但作者更偏爱 27B——"宁可少写三分之一的代码，但要更高质量"。

**与前沿闭源对比（Artificial Analysis 指数）：** Gemma 4 31B ≈ 2024 年末（≈ o1 / Claude 3.5 Sonnet, 29）；Qwen 3.6 35B A3B ≈ 2025 年初（≈ o3 / Claude 4 Sonnet, 32）；**Qwen 3.6 27B ≈ 2025 年中（≈ GPT-5 / Claude Sonnet 4.5, 37）**；DeepSeek V4 Flash ≈ 2025 年末（≈ GPT-5.2 / Claude Opus 4.5, 40）。

**展望。** 在本地部署的模型可以按需微调、不会被剥夺访问权。企业可以拿来处理敏感数据，个人可以拿来跑离线项目，或处理不想交给美中厂商的隐私数据。随着"前沿级开源权重"GLM 5.2 也能在企业预算下本地运行（MacBook 不行，单卡 5090 也不行），"我们正在进入一个迷人的时代——跑自己的模型变得可行"。当前模型把"原始智能"和"事实知识"糅在同一份权重里；未来的模型很可能会把二者解耦，把知识侧卸载给工具调用。
