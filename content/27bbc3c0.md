## English Summary

Drew Breunig's AGNTCon talk "Context Fails & Fixes" works as a state-of-context-engineering survey: from the end-2024 "RAG is dead" optimism that a large enough window makes retrieval unnecessary (Llama 4 Scout's 10-million-token window never caught on; about a million tokens became the practical baseline), to codified long-context failure modes including context poisoning, context distraction and context confusion. His running example comes from the Gemini 2.5 technical report's Pokemon agent: weights carrying FireRed-era "find tea" task knowledge poisoned the context when playing the older game, spending hours looking for tea that did not exist there. The closing frame: harness builders must choose when to be deterministic (what goes in code) and when to be probabilistic (when the model drives).

## 原文摘录 (Excerpts)

> The tea task for FireRed was in its weights, so it poisoned its context.
> When building intelligent applications, harnesses, and workflows, our job is to choose when to be deterministic (what to put in code) and when to be probabilistic (when to let the model drive).
> As model makers target new capabilities, they inadvertently create challenges we, as users and harness builders, must overcome.
