## English Summary

Canada's Government of Alberta has been using Claude Code with Claude Opus and Sonnet models since 2025 to secure its systems at scale. About 50 agents scanned government codebases autonomously in parallel, assessing 466 million lines of government code within 20 hours. Where the scan identified vulnerabilities, Claude Code could often generate a fix, test it, and build it; when automated tests were missing, Claude wrote them first. The case study quotes Alberta Minister of Technology and Innovation Nate Glubish on AI-assisted vulnerability remediation.
