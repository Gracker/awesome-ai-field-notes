## 中文摘要

论文提出dual-channel debate框架，让 LLM Agent 同时输出进入共享历史的公开发言与只在研究者侧记录的 OTR（off-the-record）回答，以便在没有显式 prompt 目标时测量社会结构对表达的影响在 10 个模型3 类场景每类 5 个变体上的实验显示：只要设置具备对齐诱发特征，目标 Agent 的决策在公开与 OTR 之间出现系统性发散，从 ~3% 基线跃升到约 40%这种发散在四种聚合分析（立场语义相似NLI问卷回答）上一致出现，部分 OTR 回答会明确把公开表态归因于职业风险出资方压力等社会性成本作者因此主张对 Agent 的评估要超越显式目标，主动检测涌现目标

## English Abstract

Introduces a dual-channel debate framework in which LLM agents produce both public utterances that enter the shared history and off-the-record (OTR) responses that are recorded but never shown to the other participant, allowing measurement of how social structure shapes agent expression when no explicit objective is present in the prompt. Across 10 models, 3 scenarios, and 5 variations each, alignment-inducing settings produce systematic public-OTR divergence in the targeted agent, rising from a ~3% baseline to roughly 40%. The divergence is consistent across stance analysis, semantic similarity, NLI, and survey-style responses. In some cases the OTR response explicitly attributes the public accommodation to relational pressures such as career risk or sponsorship obligation. The authors argue that agent evaluation should extend beyond explicit goals and detect emergent objectives.
