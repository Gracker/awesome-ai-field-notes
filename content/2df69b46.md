## 中文摘要

Stephen A. Weis 9 月 19 日宣布 factor 掉 RSA-896（RSA Factoring Challenge 历史遗留挑战数，896-bit / 270 位十进制），公开 p·q 乘积精确等于原数且 p/q 都通过素数检测。方法没有新数学，仍是 90 年代的 GNFS（开源实现 CADO-NFS）；新的是工程路径——用 Claude 把 CADO-NFS 移植到 GPU 上跑，然后调度一群闲散算力，峰值 2048 张 GPU、持续约 10 天、累计约 30 GPU-年，把"单卡跑 30 年"的活压进 10 天。这是三周内第二例：09-03 Cognition 的 Eric Lu 用 Devin agent swarm + GPU 化 CADO-NFS factor 了 862-bit 的 RSA-260（4900 GPU-天/13.5 GPU-年，估值 $400k）。Weis 明确表示对现网 RSA 密钥无新威胁，2048-bit 仍然遥不可及；真正值得记的是 AI 把高度调优的 C++ 科学计算软件移植到异构硬件并编排数千卡这件事本身。

## English Abstract

On September 19, 2026, Stephen A. Weis publicly factored RSA-896 (a 270-decimal-digit challenge number from the RSA Factoring Challenge), with the disclosed p·q product matching the original number and both factors passing primality tests. The method uses no new mathematics—still the 1990s-era General Number Field Sieve via the open-source CADO-NFS implementation—but a new engineering path: Claude was used to port CADO-NFS to GPU and orchestrate peak 2048 GPUs over ~10 days (~30 GPU-years), collapsing what was previously a ~30-year single-GPU job. This is the second such instance in three weeks, following Cognition's Eric Lu factoring RSA-260 (862-bit, ~13.5 GPU-years, estimated $400k) with Devin agent swarm on 09-03. Weis notes no new threat to deployed RSA keys; the meaningful takeaway is AI porting and orchestrating highly tuned C++ scientific code onto heterogeneous hardware at scale.
