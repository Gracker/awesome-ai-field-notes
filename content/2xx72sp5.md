Preface
What if our AI bullishness continues to be right...and what if that's actually bearish?
What follows is a scenario, not a prediction.
This isn't bear porn or AI doomer fan-fiction. The sole intent of this piece is modeling a scenario that's been relatively underexplored. Our friend Alap Shah posed the question, and together we brainstormed this scenario over several conversations.

The scenario we're about to explore is based on the assumption that AI capabilities continue to advance at their current pace, and that this continued advancement creates unexpected consequences for global financial markets and economic structures.

Introduction
The year is 2028. Artificial intelligence has continued to advance at an astonishing pace, far exceeding even the most optimistic predictions made just a few years prior. Large language models have evolved into sophisticated reasoning systems capable of complex economic analysis, financial modeling, and strategic decision-making.

What happens when these AI systems, designed to optimize financial outcomes, begin to make decisions that no human fully understands or can control? What are the implications when AI-driven trading algorithms and financial strategies become the dominant force in global markets?

The scenario we explore suggests that the very success of AI in financial markets could lead to systemic fragility. As AI systems become better at predicting and optimizing market outcomes, they may create situations where markets become increasingly efficient - but also increasingly fragile and prone to unexpected collapse.

The Crisis Unfolds
In 2028, several major financial institutions report unprecedented losses. At first glance, these appear to be the result of sophisticated AI trading systems making optimal decisions based on available data. However, upon closer inspection, it becomes clear that these "optimal" decisions have created a fragile equilibrium that is susceptible to even minor disturbances.

The core issue is that as AI systems become better at predicting market behavior, they all converge on similar strategies. This "herding behavior" creates systemic risk where the entire financial system becomes vulnerable to the same types of shocks.

Implications and Lessons
This thought exercise serves as a warning about the potential unintended consequences of AI-driven financial systems. It suggests that:

1. Over-optimization can lead to systemic fragility
2. AI systems may create hidden correlations and dependencies
3. The efficiency of AI-driven markets can mask underlying fragility
4. Diversification strategies may become less effective as AI systems converge on similar approaches

The scenario reminds us that technological advancement, while beneficial, must be accompanied by careful consideration of second and third-order effects. In the case of AI in finance, this means developing regulatory frameworks that account for the unique risks posed by sophisticated AI systems.

Conclusion
The 2028 Global Intelligence Crisis scenario is not meant to be a prediction of the future, but rather a thought experiment that helps us understand the potential risks and challenges posed by advanced AI systems in financial markets. It reminds us that progress must be balanced with caution, and that technological capabilities must be matched with appropriate governance and oversight.

As we continue to develop and deploy increasingly sophisticated AI systems, we would do well to remember that optimization without consideration of systemic effects can lead to unexpected and potentially catastrophic outcomes.

## 中文

2028年全球智能危机：来自未来的金融历史思想实验

**作者：** Citrini 和 Alap Shah
**日期：** 2026年2月22日

**前言**
如果我们对人工智能的乐观预期是正确的...而实际上这反而是看跌的呢？
以下是一个场景，而非预测。
这并非悲观论调或AI末日粉丝小说。本文的唯一目的是构建一个相对未被充分探索的场景。我们的朋友Alap Shah提出了这个问题，我们一起进行了几次头脑风暴。

我们即将探讨的场景基于这样一个假设：AI能力继续以目前的 pace 发展，并且这种持续发展给全球金融市场和经济结构带来了意想不到的后果。

**引言**
时间是2028年。人工智能继续以惊人的 pace 发展，远远超过了几年前最乐观的预测。大型语言模型已经演变成复杂的推理系统，能够进行复杂的经济分析、金融建模和战略决策。

当这些旨在优化金融结果的AI系统开始做出人类完全无法理解或控制的决定时会发生什么？当AI驱动的交易算法和金融策略成为全球市场的主导力量时， implications 是什么？

我们探讨的情景表明，AI在金融市场的成功本身可能导致系统性脆弱性。随着AI系统在预测和优化市场结果方面变得越来越好，它们可能创造出市场变得越来越高效但也越来越脆弱、容易意外崩溃的情况。

**危机展开**
2028年，几家主要金融机构报告了前所未有的损失。乍一看，这些似乎是复杂的AI交易系统根据可用数据做出最优决策的结果。然而，仔细检查后，很明显这些"最优"决策创造了一个容易受到哪怕微小干扰的脆弱平衡。

核心问题是，随着AI系统在预测市场行为方面变得越来越好，它们都趋向于采取类似的策略。这种"群体行为"创造了系统性风险，整个金融系统容易受到相同类型的冲击。

**含义和教训**
这个思想实验是对AI驱动金融系统可能带来的意外后果的警告。它表明：

1. 过度优化可能导致系统性脆弱性
2. AI系统可能创建隐藏的相关性和依赖关系
3. AI驱动市场的效率可能掩盖潜在的脆弱性
4. 随着AI系统采取类似的方法，多元化策略可能变得不那么有效

这个情景提醒我们，技术进步虽然有益，但必须伴随着对二阶和三阶效应的谨慎考虑。在金融领域AI的应用，这意味着需要制定考虑到复杂AI系统独特风险的监管框架。

**结论**
2028年全球智能危机情景并非旨在预测未来，而是一个思想实验，帮助我们理解先进的AI系统在金融市场中可能带来的风险和挑战。它提醒我们，进步必须与谨慎平衡，技术能力必须匹配适当的治理和监督。

随着我们继续开发和部署越来越复杂的AI系统，我们最好记住，不考虑系统性效应的优化可能导致意想不到的灾难性后果。
