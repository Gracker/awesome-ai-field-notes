## 中文导读

sudo L7 用 60 个由 staff 级工程师编写的软件工程任务衡量 coding agent 的工程判断力：任务多来自私有生产代码库，每个都围绕一个 frontier 模型真实翻车的地方构造，把不充分说明的现实请求和隐藏的专家 rubric（共 1,287 条原子判据，覆盖 8 个行为维度，从功能正确到如实汇报）配对。agentic grader 读取 agent 的 transcript、检查并运行其代码、把测试输出当证据；只有满足两个最高严重级的全部判据才算通过，而半数以上的门控判据考的不是功能正确性。2026 年 11 月公开榜（19 个模型，各用自家 harness 或 OpenCode，每任务 3 次）：Claude Fable 5.2 通过 52.2%、Sonnet 5.5 50.6%、Opus 5.5 46.1%；GPT-6.1 Sol、Claude Fable 5.1、GPT-6 Astra 为 31–34%；其余 13 个模型不超过 18.9%。人标研究中 3 个候选 grader 与工程师的判据一致率 85–86%，约等于工程师之间的一致率。

## 为什么值得关注

现有 SWE 基准多在功能正确性上收敛，这个基准把指出请求未提的风险、质疑错误前提、复用现成实现、如实说明查了什么没查什么都纳入判据，且过半门控判据非功能类——榜首也只过半，排名与常见 benchmark 明显拉开。做 agent 评测或选型的人值得看它公开的任务样例和排行榜。

## English Summary

sudo L7 is a benchmark of 60 software-engineering tasks authored by staff-level engineers, mostly in private production codebases, each built around a place where a frontier model failed: realistic underspecified requests paired with hidden expert rubrics of 1,287 atomic criteria across eight behavioral dimensions, from functional correctness to truthful reporting. An agentic grader reads the transcript, inspects and runs the code, and treats test output as evidence; an attempt passes only if it meets every criterion of the two highest severities, and more than half of these gating criteria test something other than functional correctness. On the November 2026 public leaderboard (19 models, three attempts per task), Claude Fable 5.2 passes 52.2%, Sonnet 5.5 50.6% and Opus 5.5 46.1%; GPT-6.1 Sol, Claude Fable 5.1 and GPT-6 Astra pass 31-34%; the other 13 models at most 18.9%. Three candidate model graders agreed with a human engineer on 85-86% of criterion verdicts, about as often as two engineers agreed with each other.
