## English Summary

Cory Doctorow's 18 Sep 2026 daily links opens with a concrete counter-example: predicting your spouse's next sentence is not the same as knowing them — it breaks the moment she says 'I want a divorce.' He traces every AI hype narrative to one root cause: we underestimate how much statistical regularity lives in natural language and overestimate how scarce 'consciousness' is. Models win on flatness; everything that produces surprise, value, and productivity lives below the texture, which the training data has already stripped out. He closes that 'AI is approaching AGI' is a story the AI capital narrative is buying itself — the same story with zero presence in actual output. Doctorow is an AI tooling supporter; the essay reads as a coolant for people battered by weekly 'passes Turing test' headlines.
