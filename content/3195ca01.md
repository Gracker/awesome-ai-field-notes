## English Summary

Qwen released Qwen3.8-Omni-Flash, its first omni-modal model built around agentic capabilities, packaging native audio-video understanding, reasoning, and tool use into one checkpoint. Key scores: +36.5 on WildClawBench-MM, +22.3 on AgenticVBench, and 69.6 on UniClawBench (OmniGAIA runs without a harness); on OmniVideoBench the 1M-token context reaches higher accuracy with 51.8% fewer tokens than static understanding, and video input cost is down ~89% versus Qwen3.5-Omni-Plus. The launch also ships Qwen-MM-Plugins (a multimodal Skill + MCP package; installer covers Claude Code, Codex, OpenClaw, Qwen Code, Hermes Agent and others) and Qwen-Live Harness. Boundaries: pricing follows the international Model Studio console; benchmark numbers come from the vendor's own evaluation; the Qwen-Live Harness official page may still 404.
