## English Article Highlights

Today, Meta introduced the Muse Wonder series with Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full, its most capable models delivering state-of-the-art performance on personal and professional agentic tasks, described as the next step on the scaling ladder toward personal superintelligence. Muse Wonder brings together Active Memory (carrying important context about the user across conversations and actively retrieving task-relevant details) and Active Focus (sustained work toward challenging goals: trying alternative approaches, working through blockers, adapting to new information, asking for clarification and checking in before important actions). The models are trained at the gigawatt-scale Prometheus data center, available today through the Meta Model API and Muse Code (Muse Wonder Full for Muse Code subscribers), and will soon power experiences in Muse, including artifact generation. Reported evaluations span the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, a Personal Superintelligence Index, AutomationBench-AA (end-to-end business workflows), MemoryBench (long-term contextual memory), FocusBench, SWE-Marathon and DeepSWE (sustained coding), Harvey's Legal Agent Benchmark (LAB), and Vals Finance Agent V2. On generalization, Meta argues that post-training gains on a stronger base model carry over more effectively to unfamiliar out-of-distribution tasks as test-time compute scales ("effective robustness"). Safety work includes robustness to adversarial instructions, careful handling of private information, the strongest safeguards to date against CBRN and cyber misuse with defensive access preserved, per the Meta Superintelligence Scaling Framework; a full safety report is planned.

## English Summary

Meta's Muse Wonder series targets personal and professional agents with two headline mechanisms — Active Memory for cross-conversation context and Active Focus for long-horizon persistence — evaluated across agent, memory, focus, and coding benchmarks (AutomationBench-AA, MemoryBench, FocusBench, SWE-Marathon, DeepSWE, Harvey LAB, Vals Finance Agent V2). Available via Meta Model API and Muse Code, the series emphasizes generalization from stronger base models and ships with safety evaluations covering adversarial robustness, private-information discretion, and CBRN/cyber misuse refusals.
