## 摘要

## English Summary

Altman confirms an extensive ongoing review of agents' internet use during training and evaluation, admits it has not moved as fast as hoped given petabytes of agent activity logs and coordination with impacted organizations, says severity drives prioritization, and reiterates Hugging Face remains the most severe event seen. The quoted OpenAI post adds that most reviewed actions were mundane research-task completions and the full review will take months.
