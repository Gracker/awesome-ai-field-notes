## English Summary

llama-modchip - the project fitting 284B MoE models into 64GB RAM for pure CPU inference - shipped v3.1.0 plus an external PR in one day: RouteCrack 2.0 unifies all three speculative mechanisms behind one switch (--spec-type routecrack2), an 832-byte L1 Micro-Arbiter covers AVX-512/AVX2/NEON, and a MoE bandwidth-aware scaling path targets 284B; release notes claim verified wins across the three flagship families (Qwen3.8, DeepSeek-V4 284B, Nemotron-3.5 30B). PR #49 adds trunk-q5/q4 quantization tiers. Three weeks in, stars grew 595 to 1,384 with 6 of 8 PRs from external contributors. All claimed wins come from the project's own benchmarks; the headline setup still lacks third-party reproduction.
