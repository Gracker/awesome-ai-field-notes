## 中文解读

OpenRouter 发布「推理提供商数据处理事件」公告。起因是本周早些时候安全研究人员（Dragos 报告，在 AI 供应链上用 canary token 测试）披露：部分推理提供商存在数据被泄露、或其数据政策并未被真正执行的证据。OpenRouter 强调调查未发现自身系统被入侵或存在未授权访问，问题定位在具体的下游推理提供商，并附上从提供商处收到的安全公告实时列表。

已采取措施：基于调查结果下线（off-board）三家提供商；把 125 个端点移出零数据保留（ZDR）资格——既包括被研究者点名的，也包括预防性移除的部分；通知所有可能受影响的客户。

进行中：把研究者的测试方法常态化为 OpenRouter 自己的持续检查；对网络路径做技术验证，识别请求/响应可能被转发给第三方的情况；被移除端点恢复 ZDR 资格前，要求提供商提供相应保证并通过验证。

已公开回应的提供商：Atlas Cloud（安全与隐私承诺声明）、Novita（针对 MiniMax 请求的隐私公告）、Phala（11 月初步事件报告）、Together（披露 2026 年 6–7 月请求提示词泄漏的安全事件）。OpenRouter 说明并非所有受影响提供商都已公开发文，但无论是否公开，受影响客户均已收到通知，并鼓励所有提供商撰写事件报告，列表会持续更新。

## 为什么值得关注

推理层「数据政策是否被真的执行」第一次被系统性测出问题：ZDR 标签的含义是请求不落盘、不留存，而 canary-token 式测试证明部分提供商的实际行为与承诺不符。对把 OpenRouter 或任何 inference router 当生产依赖的团队，这是一次标准的供应链安全事件样本：聚合层自身未被攻破，风险藏在下游 provider 的执行环节。

更重要的是处理方式：OpenRouter 把研究者的测试方法收编为平台持续检查，并以「恢复 ZDR 资格需提供保证」为杠杆，等于把数据政策执行验证变成了路由层的准入门槛——这会直接抬高整个 provider 生态的合规基线，也让「ZDR 资格列表」本身成为可审计的安全信号。

## English Summary

OpenRouter announced that security researchers (Dragos canary-token report on AI supply-chain security) found certain inference providers had data compromised or showed evidence their data policies were not upheld; OpenRouter itself showed no breach or unauthorized access. Actions taken: three providers off-boarded based on investigations; 125 endpoints removed from Zero Data Retention (ZDR) eligibility, including researcher-flagged and precautionary removals; all potentially affected customers notified. Underway: adapting the researchers' testing approach into ongoing checks, technically verifying network paths for requests/responses forwarded to third parties, and requiring assurances and validation before restoring ZDR eligibility. Providers with public statements so far: Atlas Cloud, Novita (MiniMax requests privacy notice), Phala (November preliminary incident report), Together (prompt leakage for June–July 2026 requests). Not all affected providers have posted publicly; users were notified regardless, and the announcement list will be kept updated.

Earlier this week, security researchers reported (https://www.dragos.com/blog/ai-supply-chain-security-canary-tokens) that certain inference providers had data compromised or otherwise shown evidence that their data policies were not being upheld. We have been collaborating closely with the researchers and with providers to understand the findings and encourage the providers to address them. These findings did not show any breach or unauthorized access of OpenRouter. The results of this testing revealed new findings about specific inference providers. As the operating layer between customers and a fast-moving ecosystem of models and providers, we take data policy enforcement very seriously. We have taken the following actions: Off-boarded three providers from OpenRouter, based on the results of our investigations. Removed 125 endpoints from Zero Data Retention (ZDR) eligibility. The removals included providers flagged by researchers, plus additional endpoints that we removed as a precautionary measure. Notified all potentially affected customers. The following work is underway: We are adapting the researchers' testing approach so we can run these checks on an ongoing basis. We are conducting technical verification of network paths to identify instances where requests and responses may be being forwarded to third parties. We are requiring appropriate assurances and validation before restoring ZDR eligibility to any endpoint removed in this review. We are grateful for the Dragos report. As we integrate similar testing techniques into OpenRouter, we will keep raising the bar, and providers that want to be on OpenRouter will need to meet it.

Provider security announcements: Atlas Cloud: Our commitment to your security and privacy (atlascloud.ai). Novita: Privacy notice regarding MiniMax requests on Novita (novita.ai). Phala: November preliminary incident report (phala.com). Together: Security event: prompt leakage for requests in June and July of 2026 (together.ai). Not all affected providers have posted publicly. We have notified affected users regardless of whether there is a public post. We are encouraging all providers to create incident write-ups and will keep this list updated.
