John D. Cook reports his AI coding assistant usage rising from 20% to 60% of coding workload over 8 months. Persistent issues include needing guidance to locate problems, lacking big-picture error visibility, over-optimizing test cases, and generating conceptually inconsistent code. Tools are improving rapidly but still heavily depend on human judgment.
