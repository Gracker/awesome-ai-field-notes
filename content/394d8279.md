## 原文要点

- Tencent announced Hy4; weights and system cards are to be released “in the coming days,” with stated emphasis on cybersecurity and biosafety.
- First-time visual understanding plus computer-use capabilities (CLI and GUI); specialized training on professional software such as Blender and CAD.
- Internal evaluations: hallucination rate cut from 12.95% (Hy4 Preview) to 7.2%; errors from unsupported assumptions fell from 23.1% to 5.6%.
- Blind evaluation with 213 financial professionals over 579 real-world financial tasks: Hy4 scored 3.04, ahead of DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 (2.97), Kimi K3.1 Pro (2.68) and GLM-5.3 (2.68).
- Game development via Unreal/Unity/Godot integration: +19.1% on GameDev and +9.8% on GameLogic benchmarks versus Hy4 Preview.
- Pricing unchanged versus Hy4 Preview: about US$0.90 per million input tokens, US$2.70 per million output tokens, US$0.045 per million cached tokens; Hy4 Flash announced.
- Recursive self-improvement stage via the Hyra 2.0 harness; HyCell (single-cell representations) and HyPDE (physics) foundation models open-sourced.

## English Summary

Tencent's Hunyuan lab released Hy4 on 2026-11-04, claiming its first entry into the top rank of open-source models; weights will be open-sourced shortly with a system card. Compared to Hy4-preview it significantly scales pre/post-training data, adds visual understanding and Computer Use for the first time, and improves instruction following and long-horizon agent performance, with targeted training for professional tools (Blender, CAD) and cross-app tasks. Reported numbers: hallucination rate cut from 12.95% to 7.2% after dedicated search optimization; top open-source score in a 579-task finance expert review (3.04 vs Kimi K3.1 Pro 2.68, GLM-5.3 2.68, DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 2.97). Cyber-security training data is defensive-only (PoC generation, vulnerability fixing); the team says Hy4 is being prepared for next-stage recursive self-improvement with the Hyra 2.0 scaffold.
