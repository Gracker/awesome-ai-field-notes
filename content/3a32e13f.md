## 中文导读

搜索工具通常在 post-training 才挂上模型，预训练仍只学静态文本预测。Search-Native Pretraining 主张把搜索能力前移到预训练阶段：在语料里大规模混入 web-search 查询，用本地索引取回网页响应，让模型在语言建模过程中就学会发起搜索、消化检索证据。在 Wikipedia 和 DCLM-Edu 语料、360M 到 3.3B 参数规模上，search-pretrained 模型（QueryLM）在知识密集任务上更强，对检索证据的可引导性更高；关键发现是优势在 supervised fine-tuning 之后仍然保留，说明测过的后训练流程补不回预训练阶段形成的能力。1.4B QueryLM 在全部知识密集任务上持平或超过 FLOP 对齐的 3.3B 基线，checkpoint 和训练数据都已放出。

## 为什么值得关注

"工具进预训练"这条路线此前缺少系统性消融，这篇论文用 FLOP 对齐对照说明搜索能力有预训练窗口，错过就要靠更贵的后训练补。1.4B 打平 3.3B 的数字如果在外部语料上复现，会直接影响小模型 + 搜索的产品配置思路，与 TOUMAII 这类工具调用偏好数据工作可以对照读。

## English Summary

Search tools are typically attached to language models during post-training, while pretraining remains focused on predicting static text. Search-Native Pretraining proposes teaching search capability during pretraining from ordinary text corpora: augment the pretraining corpus at scale with web-search queries and retrieve web responses via a local index, so models learn to generate search calls and incorporate retrieved evidence during language modeling itself. Across Wikipedia and DCLM-Edu corpora and 360M to 3.3B parameter scales, search-pretrained models (QueryLM) show improved performance on knowledge-intensive tasks and higher steerability toward retrieved evidence. Crucially, the advantage persists after supervised fine-tuning of both search-pretrained and conventionally pretrained models, indicating the tested post-training procedures do not recover capabilities developed during pretraining. A 1.4B QueryLM consistently beats or matches a FLOP-matched 3.3B baseline across all knowledge-intensive tasks; checkpoints and data are released.
