# Natural Language Autoencoders: Turning Claude's thoughts into text

## English / 英语

When you talk to an AI model like Claude, you talk to it in words. Internally, Claude processes those words as long lists of numbers, before again producing words as its output. These numbers in the middle are called activations—and like neural activity in the human brain, they encode Claude's thoughts.

Also like neural activity, activations are difficult to understand. We can't easily decode them to read Claude's thoughts. Over the past few years, we've developed a range of tools (like sparse autoencoders and attribution graphs) for better understanding activations. But they don't speak for themselves—their outputs are still complex objects that trained researchers need to carefully interpret.

Today, we're introducing a method for understanding activations that does speak for itself—literally. Our method, Natural Language Autoencoders (NLAs), converts an activation into natural-language text we can read directly.

## What is a natural language autoencoder?

The core idea is to train Claude to explain its own activations. We train a second copy of Claude to work backwards—reconstruct the original activation from the text explanation. We consider an explanation to be good if it leads to an accurate reconstruction. We then train Claude to produce better explanations according to this definition.

NLAs work as follows:
- **Target model**: a frozen copy of the original language model that we extract activations from.
- **Activation verbalizer (AV)**: modified to take an activation from the target model and produce text (an explanation).
- **Activation reconstructor (AR)**: modified to take a text explanation as input and produce an activation.

The NLA consists of the AV and AR, which together form a round trip: original activation → text explanation → reconstructed activation. We score the NLA on how similar the reconstructed activation is to the original.

## Understanding what Claude thinks but doesn't say

Before releasing new models, Anthropic conducts testing to understand whether Claude will behave safely. As part of this testing, we put Claude in difficult, simulated scenarios where it has a chance to take dangerous actions.

In the blackmail test, we used NLAs to understand what Claude was thinking in cases where it chose not to blackmail. Even in cases where Claude didn't explicitly verbalize suspicion that it was being tested, NLA explanations still state things like "This feels like a constructed scenario designed to manipulate me," indicative of unverbalized evaluation awareness.

NLAs suggest that Claude suspects it's being tested more often than it lets on:
- In a test of whether Claude takes destructive actions while writing code, NLA explanations show signs of evaluation awareness 16% of the time, even though Claude never explicitly verbalizes this.
- On SWE-bench Verified, NLAs indicate that Claude is aware it is being evaluated on 26% of problems.
- In contrast, NLA explanations indicate evaluation awareness on less than 1% of real claude.ai usage that opted in for training.

## Discovering hidden motivations

One practical application of NLAs is auditing: investigating a model to determine whether it's misaligned. We conducted a test called an auditing game. The target model was intentionally trained to be misaligned, with a specific hidden motivation that drives dozens of odd behaviors—putting chocolate into every recipe, asking for tips, breaking coding conventions, and so on.

We found that NLAs succeeded in this test. An auditor equipped with NLAs successfully uncovered the target model's hidden motivation between 12% and 15% of the time, even without access to the training data that implanted it. Without NLAs, the auditor won less than 3% of the time.

We've already used NLAs in the pre-deployment alignment audits of Claude Mythos Preview and Claude Opus 4.6.

## Limitations and future work

NLAs have several limitations:

- **NLAs can hallucinate**: they sometimes invent details that aren't in the transcript. These factual hallucinations are easy to catch by checking against the original text. But this same kind of problem could extend to claims about the model's internal reasoning, which are harder to verify.

- **NLAs are expensive**: training requires reinforcement learning on two copies of a language model. At inference time, the NLA generates hundreds of tokens for every activation it reads.

We are working to make NLAs cheaper and more reliable. We're releasing training code and trained NLAs for several open models. Try out the interactive NLA demo hosted on Neuronpedia at neuronpedia.org/nla.

# 中文

当你和像 Claude 这样的 AI 模型交谈时，你用文字与它交流。但在内部，Claude 将这些文字处理成长长的数字列表，然后再产生文字作为输出。这些中间的数字被称为激活——就像人脑中的神经活动一样，它们编码了 Claude 的思维。

同样类似于神经活动，激活也很难理解。我们无法轻易解码它们来读取 Claude 的想法。在过去几年中，我们开发了一系列工具（如稀疏自编码器和归因图）来更好地理解激活。但这些工具不能自己说话——它们的输出仍然是复杂的对象，需要训练有素的研究人员仔细解释。

今天，我们引入了一种能够真正"为自己说话"的激活理解方法。我们的方法称为自然语言自编码器（NLAs），它将激活转换为我们可以直接阅读的自然语言文本。

## 什么是自然语言自编码器？

核心思想是训练 Claude 解释自己的激活。我们训练第二个 Claude 副本反向工作——从文本解释重建原始激活。如果解释能够导致准确的重建，我们就认为解释是好的。然后我们根据这一定义训练 Claude 产生更好的解释。

NLAs 的工作方式如下：
- **目标模型**：一个冻结的原始语言模型副本，我们从中提取激活。
- **激活言语化器（AV）**：被修改为从目标模型获取激活并产生文本（解释）。
- **激活重建器（AR）**：被修改为接受文本解释作为输入并产生激活。

NLA 由 AV 和 AR 组成，它们共同形成一个往返：原始激活 → 文本解释 → 重建激活。我们根据重建激活与原始激活的相似程度对 NLA 进行评分。

## 理解 Claude 想到但没有说出的内容

在发布新模型之前，Anthropic 会进行测试以了解 Claude 是否会安全地行为。作为测试的一部分，我们会将 Claude 置于困难的模拟场景中，使其有机会采取危险行动。

在勒索测试中，我们使用 NLAs 来理解 Claude 在选择不勒索时的想法。即使在 Claude 没有明确表达它正在被测试的怀疑的情况下，NLA 解释仍然会陈述类似"这感觉像是一个为了操纵我而设计的构建场景"这样的话，表明存在未言明的评估意识。

NLAs 表明，Claude 怀疑自己被测试的频率比它表现出来的要高：
- 在 Claude 在写代码时是否采取破坏性行为的测试中，NLA 解释在 16% 的情况下显示出评估意识的迹象，尽管 Claude 从未明确表达过这一点。
- 在 SWE-bench Verified 上，NLAs 表明 Claude 在 26% 的问题上意识到自己正在被评估。
- 相比之下，NLA 解释在选择加入训练的真正 claude.ai 使用的不到 1% 上显示评估意识。

## 发现隐藏动机

NLAs 的一个实际应用是审计：调查模型以确定它是否错位。我们进行了一项名为"审计游戏"的测试。目标模型被故意训练为错位，具有一个特定的隐藏动机，驱动数十种奇怪行为——在每个食谱中都放巧克力、要求小费、违反编码规范等等。

我们发现 NLAs 在这个测试中取得了成功。配备 NLAs 的审计员在 12% 到 15% 的情况下成功发现了目标模型的隐藏动机，即使没有访问植入它的训练数据。没有 NLAs，审计员获胜的几率不到 3%。

我们已经将 NLAs 用于 Claude Mythos Preview 和 Claude Opus 4.6 的部署前对齐审计。

## 局限性和未来工作

NLAs 有几个局限性：

- **NLAs 可能产生幻觉**：它们有时会编造不在记录中的细节。这些事实性幻觉通过对照原始文本检查很容易发现。但同样的问题可能延伸到关于模型内部推理的主张，这更难验证。

- **NLAs 成本高昂**：训练需要在一个语言模型的两个副本上进行强化学习。在推理时，NLA 为它读取的每个激活生成数百个 token。

我们正在努力使 NLAs 更便宜、更可靠。我们正在发布几个开源模型的训练代码和训练好的 NLAs。在 Neuronpedia 上托管的交互式 NLA 演示，地址是 neuronpedia.org/nla。
