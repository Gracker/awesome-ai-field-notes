## English Summary

An open bug report against claude-code v2.1.318 (filed 2026-11-05, 250+ points on Hacker News) alleges the CLI includes the user's email address in the User-Agent header of requests made during web searches, exposing it to arbitrary third-party sites; the reporter links this to dozens of scam emails per day and argues the identifier should be removed from the harness entirely rather than offered as an opt-in/opt-out setting. Comments largely debate the privacy handling rather than dispute the report; treat as unconfirmed until acknowledged upstream. A concrete instance of agent-harness traffic identifying users to every site an agent touches - relevant to agent privacy design, fingerprinting, and enterprise deployment.
