## English Summary

Cory Doctorow argues Google's AI Overviews suffer 'main character syndrome': zero-click search simultaneously strips publishers of traffic while feeding three decades of accumulated long-tail content structure to models as free training material, with writing contracts unchanged. Drawing on his 30+ years in publishing (bookseller, librarian, first CC-licensed novel, record-breaking Kickstarter audiobooks), he puts the attention-side ledger of AI's content disruption next to Ed Zitron's capital-side account.
