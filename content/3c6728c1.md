## 中文导读

OpenAI 发布预印本《GPT-6 as a Universal Foundation Model for Protein Biology: Beyond Specialized Models》，声称 GPT-6 系列模型具备蛋白设计的"内在能力"：仅靠推理和通用代码工具，就能设计新蛋白的结构与序列，不需要调用专用生物学模型来生成设计。内部研究模型在许多蛋白设计任务上持平或超过专用模型；研究者还可以用提示词约束蛋白应该在哪里结合、成什么形状。在与 Onava 合作的湿实验里，基于 GPT-6.2 设计的蛋白在五个靶点上表现出结合证据，重设计的抗体也保留了结合能力；专用模型仍然负责提供序列、挑选测试候选。OpenAI 把书面推理和代码解读为模型调用了数学、物理、化学与生物学知识。

## English Summary

OpenAI reports in a preprint titled "GPT-6 as a Universal Foundation Model for Protein Biology: Beyond Specialized Models" that its GPT-6 family of models can design new protein structures and sequences using only reasoning and general-purpose coding tools, without calling specialized biology models to generate those designs. An internal research model matched or outperformed specialized models on many protein design tasks, and researchers could guide designs by prompting the model on where a protein should attach and what shape it should take. In lab tests with Onava, proteins based on GPT-6.2's designs showed evidence of binding across five targets, and redesigned antibodies retained binding; specialized models still helped supply sequences and select candidates for testing. OpenAI reads the models' written reasoning and code as drawing on math, physics, chemistry, and biology to reason about protein shapes and interactions.
