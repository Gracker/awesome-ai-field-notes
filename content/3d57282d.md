## English Summary

TabbyML's Weight-Space Playground makes weight-space programming tangible: Qwen3.5-0.8B often rechecks the same reasoning until its token budget runs out (93.0% of runs hit the 32k limit, MATH-500 accuracy 7.9% in the original experiment). An AI agent edited a small set of weights directly - no fine-tuning - subtracting one direction from 64 neurons' ffn_down output weights in layers 15 and 19 (131,072 of ~800M weights, about -0.016%), cutting capped runs to 33.1% and lifting MATH-500 to 48.1%. The page replays base/edited token streams recorded offline with llama.cpp (same prompt, same seed), shows the weight diff and neuron activity, and offers a live WebGPU run in the browser; edited neurons fire 2-4x harder inside loops.

> An LLM edited 0.016% of Qwen3.5-0.8B's weights - no training - and reduced repeated reasoning in the original experiment. See where the edit lands and run both models live in your browser with WebGPU.

- The edit: subtracts one direction from 64 neurons' output weights (ffn_down) in layers 15 and 19 - 131,072 of ~800M weights.
- The result: hit-token-limit 93.0% -> 33.1%; MATH-500 accuracy 7.9% -> 48.1%.
- Replay cases come from a batch of MATH-500 problems recorded with the same settings (llama.cpp, same prompt and seed).
- When the original model repeats itself, edited neurons fire harder: the push is about 2-4x bigger inside loops.
