## 中文导读

cmHC 研究 mHC（Manifold-constrained Hyper-Connections）架构里多残差流如何在 Birkhoff 双随机约束下有效混合。论文揭示一个混合权衡：欠混合时残差矩阵靠近多面体顶点，超连接退化成近似独立的多流；过混合时矩阵聚在均匀重心附近，流之间失去区分度。cmHC 用几何收缩把约束多面体向内部收缩，强制残差矩阵深入探索混合空间，有可证的流混合上界且不引入新参数化，并在浅层施加适度收缩防止过混合，让超大规模预训练的梯度传播保持稳定。dense 与 MoE 多规模实验验证有效性，还在 Ascend NPU 上做了融合算子，超连接执行延迟最高降 44.8%。

## English Summary

cmHC investigates how to effectively mix residual streams in Manifold-constrained Hyper-Connections (mHC), where stream-mixing residual matrices are constrained to the Birkhoff polytope of doubly stochastic matrices. The paper reveals a mixing trade-off: under-mixed matrices staying near the polytope vertices degenerate hyper-connections into nearly independent streams, while over-mixed matrices concentrated around the uniform barycenter make streams indistinguishable from each other, with theoretical bounds on cumulative residual mixing, stream difference and gradient difference across layers. cmHC addresses this by geometrically contracting the Birkhoff polytope toward its interior, forcing residual matrices to explore deeper mixing with a provable stream-mixing bound and no new parameterization, plus a moderate contraction at shallow layers to prevent over-mixing and keep gradient propagation stable in extremely large-scale pretraining. Experiments across dense and MoE models at multiple scales confirm effectiveness, and fused operators on Ascend NPUs cut hyper-connection execution latency by up to 44.8%.
