# Anthropic tells investors quarterly revenue up nearly 50% to $16.8bn ahead of IPO

## 要点 (中文)

FT 报道 Anthropic 上市前向投资人披露季度收入：Q1 约 $4.8bn、Q2 $11.5bn、Q3 $16.8bn（环比 +46%），Q3 每 $1 算力投入对应 $1.70 收入，剔除股权激励后调整经营利润率 11%。蒸馏（其他实验室用 Claude 输出训练自有模型）占收入 5%-7%，Q3 已“实质性”压缩——按 Simon Willison 的 napkin maths，以 Q2 收入反推，匿名实验室单季蒸馏花费超 $5 亿。FT 文中“told investors”出现四次，公司正把沟通从 annualized run rate 转向真实季度披露。

## Key claims (English)

Per FT, Anthropic told investors Q1 revenue was ~$4.8bn, Q2 $11.5bn, Q3 $16.8bn (+46% QoQ), with $1.70 revenue per $1 of compute in Q3 and 11% adjusted operating margins excluding stock-based compensation. Distillation — rival labs training on Claude outputs — was 5-7% of revenue and was cut materially in Q3, implying other labs spent over $500M distilling in Q2 alone.

**[Anthropic tells investors quarterly revenue up nearly 50% to $16.8bn ahead of IPO](https://www.ft.com/content/11632d03-358c-454b-af95-d06d4072932b?syn-25a6b1a6=1)**. The phrase "told investors" appears four times in this FT article, which reports on Anthropic's day of hosting investors on Wednesday. Lots of details in here from "[people with knowledge of the matter](https://simonwillison.net/2023/Nov/22/deciphering-clues/#hints-to-look-out-for)".

Key things I learned from the article:

-   Q1 revenue (Jan/Feb/Mar) was "about $4.8bn"
-   Q2 (Apr/May/Jun) was $11.5bn
-   Q3 (Jul/Aug/Sep) was $16.8bn
-   In Q3: $1.70 in revenue for every $1 spent on "computing power"
-   Adjusted operating margins (excluding stock-based compensation) were 11%

This point about distillation is worth quoting in full (highlight mine):

> The start-up has also clamped down on “distillation” — a controversial practice whereby rival AI labs train their models on the outputs of more advanced ones.
>
> When rival companies “distil” their models from Anthropic’s models, that involves heavy usage and spending.
>
> Anthropic told investors on Wednesday **distillation accounted for 5 per cent to 7 per cent of revenue** and that they had cut it back “materially” in the third quarter of the year, said four people with direct knowledge of the matter.

Napkin maths says that if distillation was 5% of their Q2 revenue (before they cut it back in Q3) then unnamed "other labs" spent over half a billion dollars distilling Anthropic's models in that quarter!

Also notable: Anthropic appear to no longer be leaning on "annualized run-rate revenue" ([previously](https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/29/anthropic/)) in these communications to their investors.
