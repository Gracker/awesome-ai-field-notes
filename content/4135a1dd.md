## English Summary

Auditing failed agent executions requires the responsible actor, the earliest decisive step, and the error mode. RecordRoute couples comparative localization with local diagnosis: it represents each record with word and character features, learns to rank records through within-trajectory comparisons, then lets the selected record supply actor and step while its text and metadata drive mode prediction. On controlled-injection Who&When Pro it outperforms StepFinder and ASCon on all four official metrics; with actor and step fixed, local diagnosis reaches 77.47% pooled joint accuracy versus 69.28% for full-trace diagnosis. On natural failures from TraceElephant and CatchBench POST it beats those baselines and three open LLMs at localization. Failure attribution is converging on structured two-stage methods.

### Abstract (opencli arxiv paper, 2026-11-26)

RecordRoute couples comparative localization with local diagnosis: it represents each record with word and character features and learns to rank records through comparisons within each trajectory. The selected record supplies the actor and step, while its text and metadata drive mode prediction. On controlled-injection Who&When Pro, RecordRoute outperforms StepFinder and ASCon on all four official metrics. With actor and step predictions fixed, local diagnosis reaches 77.47% pooled joint accuracy versus 69.28% for full-trace diagnosis. On natural failures from TraceElephant and CatchBench POST, it outperforms these baselines and three open LLMs in localization.
