# Gumloop：AI 实验工作流编排工具

> 原文：[Product Hunt - AI Workflow Automation](https://www.producthunt.com/categories/ai-workflow-automation) | 评分：5

## 原文 / English

Gumloop is a platform for automating repetitive and complex workflows end-to-end with AI. Builders drag, drop, and connect modular components onto a canvas to build powerful automations.

### Key Features

**Visual Builder with 115+ Pre-made Blocks**
You get a visual builder with over 115 pre-made blocks to create automations quickly. It uses AI for smart decisions and connects to many apps.

**Model-Agnostic Architecture**
Gumloop lets you choose which AI model powers each task. You can route different parts of a workflow to OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, or open-source models depending on what works best. This means you are not locked into one provider, and you can optimize for cost or quality on a per-task basis.

**Chrome Extension**
There's a Chrome extension you can use to help you record browser actions, scrape web data, and automate any web-based tasks.

**Natural Language Agent Building**
Gumloop's "Gummie" feature allows you to create AI agents based on natural language descriptions.

**Enterprise-Ready**
One simple subscription covers everything, and you can build custom team interfaces. Companies like Gusto use Gumloop to power AI agents that drive revenue, reduce churn, and elevate rep performance.

### Recent Funding

Gumloop recently raised $50M from Benchmark to turn every employee into an AI agent builder.

### Example Use Cases

- An agent that automatically briefs you before every meeting with context from across your tools
- An agent that analyzes call recordings to surface objection patterns, coaching insights, and competitive intelligence
- Monitoring and alerting workflows

## 中文

Gumloop 是一个端到端使用 AI 自动化重复性和复杂性工作流的平台。构建者通过在画布上拖放和连接模块化组件来搭建强大的自动化流程。

### 核心功能

**可视化构建器，115+ 预制模块**
拥有可视化构建器，提供超过 115 个预制模块，可快速创建自动化流程。内置 AI 智能决策，并支持连接多种应用。

**模型无关架构**
Gumloop 允许你为每个任务选择 AI 模型。你可以将工作流的不同环节路由至 OpenAI、Anthropic、Google Gemini 或开源模型，根据效果灵活切换。这意味着你不会被单一供应商锁定，可以按任务优化成本或质量。

**Chrome 扩展程序**
提供 Chrome 扩展，可记录浏览器操作、抓取网页数据并自动化任何基于网页的任务。

**自然语言智能体构建**
Gumloop 的"Gummie"功能支持通过自然语言描述创建 AI 智能体。

**企业级支持**
单一订阅方案涵盖全部功能，并可构建自定义团队界面。Gusto 等公司使用 Gumloop 驱动 AI 智能体，提升收入、降低流失率并改善员工绩效。

### 最新融资

Gumloop 近期从 Benchmark 获得 5000 万美元融资，致力于让每位员工都成为 AI 智能体构建者。

### 典型应用场景

- 会前自动简报：根据跨工具信息为你准备会议背景
- 通话分析智能体：分析录音，挖掘异议模式、培训洞察和竞情情报
- 监控与告警工作流
