# Movement Matters: A Turing Test for Robot Interaction

You have one minute to interact with a robot through movement only. At the end, you need to say whether it's tele-operated or not.

That's the React to This (RTT) Test—one of the robot Turing tests presented at the research page.

## How initial tests went down

In preliminary experiments, people tested:

1. **The robot's sensing abilities** — such as whether it could track them visually or respond to poking
2. **The robot's motor abilities** — whether they could copy their movements
3. **The robot's responses to affective stimuli** — smiling, aggression, kicking (pain), and other emotional triggers
4. **The robot's responses to emblems** — waving, heart gestures, and other symbolic gestures
5. **The robot's responses to pointing** — to things in context, like clothing
6. **The robot's responses to roleplay** — especially based on its looks

People change how they interact depending on looks. Tested forms included: robot, human-like character, animals, and objects.
