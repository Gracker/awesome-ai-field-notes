## English Summary

Writing from a month in London, Steve Blank reports European policymakers' reaction to the reported unilateral restriction of Starlink/Starshield service to Ukraine: one CEO's decision degraded an ally's wartime capability, so treating the U.S. as an unreliable security and technology partner is no longer paranoia. The AI turn is economic: AI collapses the cost of building sovereign software exactly as geopolitical risk raises customers' willingness to switch - recreating the features, functions and compatibility of Microsoft Office, Oracle, Salesforce, Palantir and Anduril is now within reach. If every U.S. vendor is treated as a kill-switch risk, the allied market for the whole ecosystem shrinks, potentially costing American companies tens to hundreds of billions in lost revenue.
