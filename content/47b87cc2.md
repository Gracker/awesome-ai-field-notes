## English Summary

Anthropic is partnering with Accenture to embed independent evaluators inside the lab, with each side committing at least USD 1B over five years. The work is led by Accenture Faculty and covers alignment assessments, red-teaming, and safeguard testing. This is the first concrete step on Dario Amodei's commitment to embed evaluators at frontier labs. Unlike conventional external evaluation, the embedded model gives the evaluator access to the model under review and its production context; details of how it operates 'are still being worked out.' Open question: who funds, who evaluates, and who reads the reports is the real measure of independence.
