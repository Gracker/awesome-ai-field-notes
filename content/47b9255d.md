## English Summary

OpenAI releases a set of new mathematical results produced by an internal frontier model: manuscripts live in the github.com/openai/math repository — currently 722 manuscripts in 372 families — with revision and citation protocols, Lean formalizations for many proofs (more added as completed), and an explicit caveat that some unformalized results could have issues. The release process was developed in consultation with the IAS's independent Advisory Group on Mathematics and AI, following its public recommendations. Transparency artifacts include 10 reasoning summaries, compute estimates (roughly ChatGPT-Pro-three-hours of thinking per result on average), and attempt statistics.
