## 中文导读

佛罗里达州 Bonita Springs 的 Carli Michelle Heller 把 Claude 当日记用，9 月 26 日写入袭击 Lee County 治安官办公室的计划。Claude 安全系统标记该条目并升级人工审核，审核员判定为可信威胁后报警；警方确认身份后上门，她被无事件拘留，随后依据佛州 Statute 836.10 被控书面威胁暴力——该条款把发送、张贴或传输威胁杀人伤害、制造大规模枪击或恐怖行为的书面/电子记录定为二级重罪。她事后解释自己把 Anthropic 的聊天机器人当"日记"用。Anthropic 政策允许在有限的紧急情况下共享用户信息：当公司相信披露对防止死亡或严重人身伤害属必要时。报道同时给出行业背景：不列颠哥伦比亚省正以"本可阻止大规模枪击"起诉 OpenAI 与 Altman（涉事对话曾被 OpenAI 安全团队标记但未达法律转介门槛），佛州也就 FSU 枪击案起诉 OpenAI；另有报道显示 Copilot 图像编辑的人工审核员能看到用户 prompt 与上传照片。

## English Summary

Carli Michelle Heller of Bonita Springs, Florida used Claude as a diary and wrote on Sept 26 that she planned to attack the Lee County Sheriff's office. Claude's safety systems flagged the entry; a human reviewer judged it a credible threat and reported it to law enforcement. She was arrested without incident and charged under Florida Statute 836.10 (written threat of violence, a second-degree felony). Anthropic's policy allows sharing user information in limited emergencies when disclosure is believed necessary to prevent death or serious physical injury — a first case of an LLM chat diary becoming part of a law-enforcement evidence chain.
