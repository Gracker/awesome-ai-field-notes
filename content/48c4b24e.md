## 中文导读

Manual do Usuário 报道：iOS/macOS 27 起，Apple Intelligence 从「完全可选」变成**不可整体关闭**。此前 iOS 26 在设置里有一键关闭开关；27 的 beta 里总开关消失，只能逐项关闭（Writing/Math Assistance、Image Creation、Extensions、Messages 摘要、Mail 摘要等），而且走 Screen Time 内容限制关闭会连带把 CarPlay 干掉。

成本侧的具体数字：macOS（Air，8GB RAM / 256GB 存储）上仅 Apple Intelligence 模型就占约 **35GB 磁盘**且关不掉；HN「关掉 Apple Intelligence 拿回磁盘空间」相关讨论 770+ 分、557 条评论（10 月社区持续发酵）。新 Siri AI 还改变了 Spotlight 的系统级行为（The Register）。

同周对照：Android 端 Google Assistant 已于 9/28 全面切换 Gemini。两端同时进入「AI 操作系统化」——系统级 AI 成为默认在场的基础设施，不再是用户主动启用的高级功能。

## 为什么值得关注

对 App 开发者这是基线假设的变化：系统内置 Foundation Models 意味着「用户没装 AI SDK」的 fallback 逻辑可以删掉，但健康/金融/隐私类应用要把「系统级 AI 拦截/重写」纳入安全边界；App Store 审核也要按「系统 AI 必装」做新假设。跨端产品则要把 AI 视为系统能力而非第三方服务。

## English Summary

Manual do Usuário reports (since confirmed by October community discussion): Apple Intelligence can no longer be turned off wholesale in iOS/macOS 27. iOS 26 had a single disable switch; in 27 betas the master switch disappeared — only per-feature toggles remain (Writing/Math Assistance, Image Creation, Extensions, Messages/Mail summaries), and disabling via Screen Time content restrictions also kills CarPlay. On a MacBook Air the models take ~35GB of disk that cannot be reclaimed; the HN thread on turning Apple Intelligence off to recover space hit 770+ points with 557 comments. The same week, Google finished switching Android's Assistant to Gemini — both platforms now treat OS-level AI as default-present infrastructure rather than an opt-in feature.
