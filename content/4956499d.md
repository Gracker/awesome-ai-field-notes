## 原文摘录（thread 1/2/3/5/7）

> 1/ We ran the 475.5 GB Kimi K3.1 Pro pruned75 checkpoint on two DGX Stations, one GB300 each. It fits and generates tokens - but answer quality is unreliable.
>
> 2/ With 40 GiB of Grace weight offload per Station, 64 simultaneous requests reached 295 generated tokens/s aggregate. Rates include reasoning - not completed tasks.
>
> 3/ At 128, it was 272.9 seconds - over 4 minutes. 'Completed successfully' doesn't mean a good interactive experience.
>
> 5/ The big caveat: this checkpoint removes 75% of routed experts. Basic checks produced reasoning loops and wrong arithmetic. JSON and a forced tool call passed. Useful-task quality remains unproven.
>
> 7/ Final two-GB300 context result: 786,561-token inputs completed 3/3 at C1 under a 1M context ceiling. Median first-token wait: 495 seconds.

## English Summary

SJRonanMD's team posted the first self-hosted readings of the Kimi K3.1 Pro pruned75 checkpoint (475.5 GB, 75% of routed experts pruned) on Nov 6, 2026: two DGX Stations with one GB300 each, 40 GiB Grace memory per station for weight offload. Aggregate throughput reached 295 generated tokens/s at 64 concurrent requests (273 at 128), worst first-token waits 6.3s vs 272.9s. Long-input probes: 196,739-token inputs completed 15/15 (median 346s first token at 4-way), ~393K-token inputs 6/12 at 4-way with six timeouts, 786,561-token inputs 3/3 single-stream, median 495s. Basic checks showed reasoning loops and wrong arithmetic; JSON and a forced tool call passed. Throughput and capacity numbers are not task-quality evidence - long inputs were synthetic repeated prose; next step adds two RTX PRO 6000 sidecars.
