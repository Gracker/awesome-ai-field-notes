## English Abstract / Excerpt

Gary Marcus pairs two warnings on September 9. First, Terence Tao's three Mathstodon posts are markedly darker than his 2024 Atlantic interview — separating problem-solving from novel insight, demanding AI training transparency, and calling out the OpenAI/NYU/Anthropic Navier–Stokes dispute where OpenAI rushed a proof (X-tracked cost ~$22.5M) and Mark Chen conceded de-identified user data could flow back into models. Second, Anthropic's Jacob Coxon posted on September 8 that 'people building AI genuinely believe AI could wipe out humanity within a decade' (37.6K views, 12.8K likes in 24h); Alignment lead Evan Hubinger publicly agreed on >10% probability. Marcus's read: internal cognition and external messaging at frontier labs have already diverged sharply.
