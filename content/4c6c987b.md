## 中文导读

Anthropic 官方公告（2026-10-18，周日）：设立 Anthropic Public Benefit Fund。经股东批准后 5% 股份即日拨入；相对设定基线，公司价值每增长 50%，董事会可裁量追加最多 2%。基金设为全资 LLC 而非 501(c)(3)，理由是在不同捐赠形态（向传统非营利的大额捐赠、向特定人群直接发放）之间保持调整空间；LLC 持有的股权不用于美国或境外游说与政治捐赠。叠加联合创始人 80% 持股捐赠承诺与约半数员工参与的股权捐赠匹配，公司口径约 20% 股份面向公共用途。劳动市场影响被点名为优先方向（已有 $2 亿承诺）。

## 为什么值得关注

 Reuters 同日报道 Anthropic 正以 PBC 身份筹备史上最大规模 IPO 之一：上市前把一块股权写进可随估值追加的公共基金，实体与比例规则都进了公告，而估值基线、治理细则和拨款名单都留在"未来数月"的披露窗口。与既有结构并读要分三层：PBC 公司体内的使命工作、LTBT 治理席位（不对外发钱）、本次的对外捐赠基金。

## Announcement Full Text (fetched via curl, 2026-10-19)

> Oct 18, 2026 — Today, we're committing a portion of Anthropic's shares to the Anthropic Public Benefit Fund, a new entity with a structure that increases the stake as the company grows. Subject to stockholder approval, 5% of Anthropic's shares will be committed today, with an additional 2% added at the board's discretion for every 50% increase in the company's value above a set baseline.

> As a public benefit corporation, most of our work in pursuit of our mission takes place inside the company: research relevant to AI safety and interpretability; applied biomedical research; discounts on Claude for nonprofits and scientific institutions.

> The Anthropic Public Benefit Fund will have increased flexibility and scale, enabling us to pursue our mission in new ways: for example, we might explore large donations to traditional nonprofits or direct distributions to specific populations.

> One example is labor market impacts. AI may generate enormous economic value, but it could also cause instability for many workers. Anthropic has already committed $200 million to studying and addressing this issue, but given the pace of AI progress, bolder approaches may be needed.

> We're establishing the Anthropic Public Benefit Fund as a wholly-owned LLC—rather than a 501(c)(3) or other format—to give adjustability between different forms of giving. None of the Anthropic equity held by the LLC will be used for lobbying or political giving, in the US or abroad.

> It is common practice for tech companies to offer escalating compensation awards to their executives. We are instead directing that equity to the public benefit.

> This builds on commitments our co-founders and employees have already made. When Anthropic was founded, our co-founders pledged to donate 80% of their equity holdings, and since founding we have also operated an equity donation match program that allows employees to pledge equity which Anthropic matches. Approximately half of our employees participate in this program. When combined with today's announcement, roughly 20% of Anthropic shares will be dedicated to the public good.

> In the coming months, we will share more about the Anthropic Public Benefit Fund's structure and governance, including the valuation milestones that may add to its stake. We will also report on our progress to date against our mission in an upcoming public benefit report.
