## 中文摘要

论文定义 Autonomous Policy Evolution 作为新的受控评估设置，由 harness-model Agent 在固定交互预算下反复修改可执行策略系统作者基于紧凑的交互式 RL 环境构建 EvoPolicyGym 基准，用于评估 Agent 如何迭代地改进已探索的策略在 16 个环境上 GPT-5.5 取得最强综合排名和 top-two 表现基准还提供轨迹级诊断，区分 Agent 如何分配预算把反馈转化为参数化调优；结论是强大的自主策略进化不仅依赖单点任务胜率，还依赖发现任务适配机制并在有界反馈下持续优化策略

## English Abstract

Defines Autonomous Policy Evolution, a controlled evaluation setting where a harness-model agent repeatedly edits an executable policy system under a fixed interaction budget. Instantiates the setting in EvoPolicyGym, a benchmark of compact interactive RL environments. On the 16-environment suite, GPT-5.5 achieves the strongest aggregate rank and top-two performance on every environment. Beyond leaderboard scores, the benchmark provides trajectory-level diagnostics that distinguish how agents allocate budget and convert feedback into parametric tuning, showing that strong policy evolution depends on discovering task-appropriate mechanisms and refining policies under bounded feedback, not only on isolated wins.
