## Summary (English)

OpenAI's official account tweeted Aug 18: as models grow more capable, the risks of developing and testing them internally grow too, so it paused RL training on its latest deployment-intended models for two weeks while hardening and red-teaming research environments and expanding monitoring coverage; the largest planned frontier RL run remains on hold until smaller-scale training and evaluations validate the safeguards and build more evidence of alignment. The tweet card links to 'Pacing model development in an era of cyber-critical capabilities,' formally tying development pacing to a cyber-critical capability threshold.
