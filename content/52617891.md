## English Summary

Ramp's CEO claims 'Jevons paradox has broken' — token spend growth fell from 42% in July to 1.5% across August and September. The author cools the data (noisy sample, skewed toward funded tech companies) and attributes his own token plateau to running out of wanted projects, feature-refusal discipline, and a compressed shipping cadence. He maps AI adoption onto four tiers with token magnitudes: chat around 50k tokens per person per day; ad-hoc agents around 2.6M tokens per Claude Code session (his own median); engineered agents hundreds of millions per day; autonomous agents billions per day. Ramp's top 1% of spenders account for 80% of spend, so heavy users hitting ceilings shows up in totals. Conclusion: Jevons holds — price cuts buy more volume within a tier, but tier upgrades are organizational problems; the binding constraint is attention and organizational change speed, not price.
