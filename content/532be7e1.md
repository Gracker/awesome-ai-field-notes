## 中文导读

jyn 的思想实验从一个假设开始：如果 AI 真的便宜到不计价，生产力归谁？文章推演五种世界：现状（AI 影响有限，如移动电话）、混乱过渡（NBER 式复职效应创造 UI 设计、销售工程、HCI 研究等新岗位，10 年内大体消化但贫富差距恶化）、复职效应失效（劳动对资本全面失去议价力，财富集中成财阀政治，没有 GPU 工厂与供应链的国家面对经由市场而非武力的后现代殖民主义）、以及随后可能的政治反应——作者借巴特勒圣战与《莱博维茨的赞歌》论证革命更可能是无差别破坏、焚书与知识损失，而非解放。结论：收益一部分给建设者、一部分给在位者、大头给房东与食利者；对策是给土地之外的稀缺资源设计土地价值税式的机制，让租金进入公共财政而非纯粹食利者。

## English Summary

jyn runs a five-scenario experiment on who captures AI productivity once software stops being scarce: status quo, messy transition (the reinstatement effect creates new tasks, displacement absorbed within a decade), reinstatement failure (labor loses pricing power to capital, wealth concentrates into a plutocracy, countries without GPU foundries face market-mediated neo-colonialism), and the political reactions that follow, including Butlerian-Jihad-style indiscriminate destruction of knowledge. Core thesis: gains flow partly to builders and incumbents, mostly to landlords and rent-seekers, hardly any to everyone else. Proposed response: Land-Value-Tax-equivalent mechanisms for other scarce resources, so rents fund the public rather than pure position-holders.

## 原文摘录 (Excerpts)

> I posit that it partly goes to the people building, partly goes to incumbents, mostly goes to landlords and other rent-seekers, and hardly any of it goes to everyone else.
> To avoid that situation, we need an equivalent of Land-Value-Tax that works for scarce resources other than land.
> Not every country has its own GPU foundries; not every country is part of the global supply chain for those foundries. We could see a post-modern version of colonialism, mediated through markets rather than force.
