Source: [petitegeek](https://x.com/petitegeek/status/1953082338029781441)

---

**EN:** You have one minute to interact with a robot through movement only. At the end, you need to say whether it's tele-operated or not.
**ZH:** 你只有一分钟时间，通过动作来与一个机器人互动。结束时，你需要判断它是远程操控的还是完全自主的。

**ZH:** 

**EN:** That's the React to This (RTT) Test - one of the robot Turing tests presented at https://t.co/ze2BnPp1oq
**ZH:** 这就是 React to This（RTT）测试——发表于 https://t.co/ze2BnPp1oq 的机器人图灵测试之一。

**EN:** 
**ZH:** 

**EN:** Here's how initial tests went down 👇
**ZH:** 初步实验的测试方式如下 👇

**EN:** 
**ZH:** 

**EN:** In our preliminary experiments, people tested:
**ZH:** 预实验中，参与者测试了：

**EN:** 
**ZH:** 

**EN:** 1. The robot's sensing abilities, such as whether it could track them visually or respond to poking
**ZH:** 1. 机器人的感知能力——它能否视觉追踪他们，或对戳弄作出反应

**EN:** 2. The robot's motor abilities, or whether they could copy their movements
**ZH:** 2. 机器人的运动能力——它们能否模仿参与者的动作

**EN:** 3. The robot's responses to smiling, aggression,... such as kicking (pain), and other affective stimuli
**ZH:** 3. 机器人对微笑、攻击……如踢腿（疼痛）及其他情感刺激的反应

**EN:** 4. The robot's responses to higher level emblems, eg. waving or heart gestures
**ZH:** 4. 机器人对高阶象征性动作的反应，如挥手或比心手势

**EN:** 5. The robot's responses to pointing to things in context, like clothing
**ZH:** 5. 机器人对指向性动作（如指向衣服）的反应

**EN:** 6. The robot's responses to roleplay, especially based on its looks
**ZH:** 6. 机器人对角色扮演的反应，尤其基于其外观

**ZH:** 测试对象包括机器人形态、类人角色、动物和物体。人们会根据外观改变与它们的互动方式。
