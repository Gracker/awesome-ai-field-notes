## 要点 (中文)

Ed Zitron 拆解 AI 行业对“年度化运行率”（ARR）的滥用：本应只对近 28 天经常性收入做年化的口径，被 OpenAI、Anthropic、CoreWeave、Cognition 用来支撑估值叙事——OpenAI 9 月被报 $70B ARR 实际约 $50B，差额来自云合作方销售的 gross-up；法律 AI Harvey 累计融资 $910M 后毛利率从年初约 50% 跌到 6 月的 -50%；CoreWeave 债券收益率 10 月 13 日达 15.1%。文中引用的数据称 OpenAI 与 Anthropic 约 80% 收入来自 1% 的客户，多数是 AI 应用公司，允许用户在 $20 订阅上烧 $83-118 的 token。与同周 Anthropic 停用 ARR 的动作对照，两边对同一份数据的判断相反。

## Key claims (English)

Ed Zitron dissects the misuse of annualized run rates: OpenAI's reported $70B ARR in September was really ~$50B once cloud-partner gross-ups are removed; Harvey, with $910M raised, saw gross margins fall from ~50% to -50% by June; CoreWeave bond yields hit 15.1% on Oct 13. Cited data says ~80% of OpenAI and Anthropic revenue comes from 1% of customers — mostly AI apps letting users burn $83-118 of tokens on $20 subscriptions.
