# Zapier AI：用自然语言生成跨应用自动化工作流

> 原文：[Zapier AI](https://zapier.com/ai) | 评分：4

## 原文 / English

Zapier has evolved into a unified orchestration platform where you can add AI exactly where you need it — in a workflow, as an agent, or a customer chatbot.

### Core Offerings

**AI Workflows**
Zapier enables you to automate advanced workflows with the full building power of Zapier, connecting over 9,000 apps. You can embed AI decision-making into your existing automations.

**AI Agents**
Deploy AI agents that work alongside your team. Agents can be interacted with like co-workers in Slack, Teams, and email — simply tag @Gumloop (or the agent name) and ask questions or trigger actions.

**AI Chatbots**
Build customer-facing chatbots without code, integrated with your existing app ecosystem.

**Tables & Forms**
Use AI-powered tables and forms for data collection and processing within your workflows.

### Use Cases

- Connecting AI capabilities to 9,000+ apps including Google Sheets, Salesforce, Slack, and more
- Building multi-step automations that incorporate AI reasoning at any step
- Deploying agents that handle customer support, sales inquiries, and internal operations
- Creating chatbots that pull data from your business tools in real time

Zapier's AI Actions feature includes Natural Language API (NLA) and ChatGPT integration, allowing you to automate tasks and boost productivity across your entire app stack.

## 中文

Zapier 已演进为一个统一的编排平台，让你可以按需在任意位置引入 AI——在工作流中、作为智能体，或作为客服聊天机器人。

### 核心产品

**AI 工作流**
Zapier 让你用完整的构建能力自动化高级工作流，连接超过 9,000 个应用。你可以将 AI 决策嵌入现有自动化流程中。

**AI 智能体**
部署与团队协作的 AI 智能体。智能体可以在 Slack、Teams 和电子邮件中像同事一样交互——只需 @ 智能体名称，提出问题或触发操作。

**AI 聊天机器人**
无需编码即可构建面向客户的聊天机器人，并与现有应用生态深度集成。

**表格与表单**
在工作流中使用 AI 驱动的表格和表单进行数据收集与处理。

### 应用场景

- 将 AI 能力与 Google Sheets、Salesforce、Slack 等 9,000+ 应用连接
- 构建多步骤自动化流程，在任意环节引入 AI 推理
- 部署处理客户服务、销售咨询和内部运营的智能体
- 创建实时从业务工具获取数据的聊天机器人

Zapier 的 AI Actions 功能包含自然语言 API（NLA）及 ChatGPT 集成，让你能够自动化任务并在整个应用生态中提升生产力。
