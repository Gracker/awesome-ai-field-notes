## 中文摘要

## English Summary

Mojo 1.0 collapses the several parallel syntaxes accumulated through rapid iteration into unified var declarations, a single Pointer type, and a single lambda definition form, while formally committing to backward compatibility - effectively graduating Mojo into something usable for production code. Since its 2023 debut the community has shipped 1,100 PRs from ~200 contributors and 200K lines of code. The 26.5 release adds Python-style lambdas, a stable LSP, Mojo AI Skills 1.0, reference-invalidation diagnostics, and unified error messages for where clauses. Modular commits to open-sourcing the Mojo compiler and toolchain in 2026 and teases async, pattern matching, and union as upcoming core work. On the MAX side, max serve / max benchmark / max all are split into separate installables and the modular package is deprecated from 26.6.
