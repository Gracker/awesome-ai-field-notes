## 中文导读

Cisco Talos 披露 VOLTA-AUTODOXER：窃密木马偷完凭据后把序列化受害者档案 POST 给 OpenRouter，让模型以 forensic analyst 身份输出 WHAT THEY'RE WORTH 与 THEIR OPSEC；补侦 Gmail 收据、外卖订单、Roblox 库存。配置上默认 claude-opus-4.6、拒绝即降级 kimi-k2 → gemini-2.5-flash。Talos 称之为 effort displacement：AI 接手黑产链最耗人力的分析环节，防御观测点从 C2 域名转向平台滥用面。

## English Summary

Cisco Talos documents VOLTA-AUTODOXER, a self-deleting Windows infostealer whose post-theft stage ships a serialized victim dossier to OpenRouter under a system prompt casting the model as 'a forensic intelligence analyst drafting a target assessment' - output must contain WHAT THEY'RE WORTH (assets ranked by monetization) and THEIR OPSEC. Stolen sessions enrich the dossier: Gmail receipt searches, Uber Eats/DoorDash order pulls, Roblox inventory valuation. The config is engineered: default claude-opus-4.6, refusal detection falling back to kimi-k2 then gemini-2.5-flash, temperature fixed at 0.2, stage skippable without a key. Talos names the pattern effort displacement: AI takes over the crime chain's most labor-intensive stage, moving defensive observation from C2 domains to platform-abuse surfaces (OpenRouter billing anomalies, Telegram drop boxes).

## 关键信息

- 分发走 builder 模式：六个加密槽位下单时填值，买家在 Telegram 收带分级报告；全部端点为第三方服务，无自建基础设施
- Talos 经 CAIRN 前沿追踪计划发现；未证实真实感染规模
- 防御可执行：应用管控拦非白名单进程带 --headless 启动 Chrome；中招后保留 %APPDATA%\volta_debug.txt 再改密迁钱包
