# A SKILL.md for commenting on Hacker News

Sep 26, 2026

Roll a d6.

#### If you rolled “1”:

1.  Read the article.

2.  Find and quote the weakest sentence. Must be tangential to the main thesis.

3.  Say _“This makes me not want to read the article.”_

#### If you rolled “2”:

1.  Do **not** read the article.

2.  SF Bay Area humble brag: _“This reminds me of the time I met Steve Jobs...”_

#### If you rolled “3”:

1.  Open with _“I have a doctorate in point-set topology. This is utter nonsense.”_

2.  Fail to explain the concept correctly.

#### If you rolled “4”:

1.  _“You no longer need to pay for software. I just spin up an agent swarm.”_

2.  Your token budget is $7,000 a day.

#### If you rolled “5”:

_“I agree that the emergence of the techno-surveillance state is appalling. But as the lead of facial recognition at Flock, I just don’t know what a single person can do.”_

#### If you rolled “6”:

_“I browse with HTML disabled. This site did not load for me.”_
