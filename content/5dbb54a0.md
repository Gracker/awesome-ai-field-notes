## English Summary

Anthropic opened a research preview of Claude Duet, a proactive personal agent for Pro and Max plans in the US, powered by Opus 5.5. Instead of waiting in a chat box, it briefs you every morning, flags what needs your attention, takes tasks handed over by message, and keeps working on its own cloud computer until it returns finished docs, sheets or decks. It waits for your ok before sensitive operations such as sending messages or payments, reuses Claude memory, skills and more than 6,000 connectors and plugins, and runs approvals, logins and safety checks inside the user's own cloud computer with full visibility of what it is doing. The page frames the product as a personal AI agent for inbox, calendar and follow-ups that messages you when something needs you, with waitlist access during the research preview.
