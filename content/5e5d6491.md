## English Summary

LG AI Research made the K-EXAONE 2.5 API free (Nov 9, 2026): obtain a key at docs.exaone.ai for service development and research, open to developers, startups, enterprises, and research institutions. The model is a technical-validation version with additional training on the August release, positioned on agentic capability; vendor-internal benchmarks report AutomationBench-Openset 74.6, SWE-Bench Pro 59.2, BrowseComp 78.0, and Claw-Eval 77.3, with claimed parity against Chinese frontier models like DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash and GLM-5.3. All scores are internal evaluations without third-party verification; the free OpenAI-compatible entry is practically useful for trialing agentic workflows at zero cost, but license terms (commercial use, training, key lifetime) need checking.
