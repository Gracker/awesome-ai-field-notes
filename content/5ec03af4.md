## 中文导读

从 macOS 13 起，每台 Mac 都内置一对守护进程（knowledgeconstructiond 与 intelligenceplatformd），交叉分析通讯录、日历、照片、信息、邮件、定位、屏幕使用时间等端上信号，构建并持续更新一张"你是谁、你认识谁、如何交流、去过哪、做过什么"的个人知识图谱。作者逆向了这套系统：数据存于 ~/Library/IntelligencePlatform 下 51 个明文 SQLite 库（位于 TCC 全盘访问保护区内）；核心 graph.db 是三元组存储，词表含 181 个实体类与 278 个谓词，ontology.db 负责解码谓词码；判定家人朋友的 EntityTagging_FamilyAndFriends 读 144 个特征，分三块——20 个身份、40 个按星期/时段条件化的通讯模式、84 个共同出现照片（如 photosPersonPosteriorProbabilityGivenSaturdayNight）。删除语义也值得注意：live 图会"遗忘"，被撤回的行进入 expired_stable_graph 保留置信度与来源，约 48 小时后被清理。作者开源了查看自己图谱的本地仪表盘 iKnow，并做了"隐私单点故障 + 去审查 LLM"的攻击思想实验。

## 为什么值得关注

这是把 Apple Intelligence 个性化底座第一次公开拆开看的分析：官方文档几乎只有一页 man page，forensics 社区（iLEAPP）今年 9 月才学会读 iOS 侧同一存储。对端上个性化 AI 生态，这张图谱是 LLM 检索的底座数据，隐私含义直接；文章把讨论从"收集了什么"推进到"被滥用时会发生什么"（知识库落入攻击者手中、与去审查 LLM 组合的威胁场景），并强调结论适用于任何同类个性化助手系统。对做端上 agent、个性化记忆系统或 macOS 安全的人都是一手参考。

## 关键原文摘录（English）

- man 8 knowledgeconstructiond（macOS 26.6.2）: "knowledgeconstructiond – A daemon that analyzes content on the device to build a general purpose knowledge graph."
- "The two daemons keep their work in ~/Library/IntelligencePlatform, a directory of SQLite databases (fifty-one databases) stored in plaintext but located in a zone protected by TCC Full Disk Access."
- "It is a triple store written in a vocabulary of 181 entity classes and 278 predicates: every row is a subject, a predicate and an object."
- "photosPersonPosteriorProbabilityGivenSaturdayNight is a shipped feature name in a shipped classifier whose job is deciding whether someone is family."
- "Delete a contact and nothing re-derives them; the next run replaces the table wholesale. But the store keeps a second table, expired_stable_graph, beside the live one."

## English Summary

Reverse-engineering write-up of the personal knowledge graph every Mac ships since macOS 13: daemons such as knowledgeconstructiond analyze on-device content — Contacts, Calendar, Photos, Messages, Mail, location, Screen Time, and a long tail of behavioral signals — to build and continuously update a graph of who you are, who you know, and how you communicate, move, and act. The graph lives on device as a queryable store that Apple Intelligence LLMs retrieve from, with little public documentation beyond the man page. The authors classify it under privacy/personalized AI affecting macOS 13+, and release iKnow, an inspection tool for peeking into your own graph.
