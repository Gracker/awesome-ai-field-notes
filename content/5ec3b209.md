## Abstract（节选原文）

> Harness evolution from a common base exhibits Locality, Complementarity, and Nonstationarity: variants repair different failures, and their utility changes as the policy learns. Rather than seeking a universally superior harness, we introduce Harness-S to selectively rescue failures of a base execution system through sibling harnesses and transfer the resulting capabilities into a single policy that remains effective under the original harness. … Complementary Harness Rescue Training invokes this pool only when all initial attempts under the base harness fail. The same policy generates fresh rollouts under selected auxiliary harnesses, and these trajectories replace the failed group for reinforcement learning.

## 中文摘要

Harness-S 观察 harness 变体修复不同失败、且效用随策略学习漂移（Locality / Complementarity / Nonstationarity）。做法：从同一基础运行框架派生多个专修不同失败的 sibling harness，组成固定的 Sibling Harness Pool；仅当基础 harness 下整组尝试全失败时，Adaptive Harness Router 按 Harness Behavior Profiles 与投票选 Top-k 变体重新 rollout，用新轨迹替换失败组进 RL，训练后回到原框架评估。实验覆盖 AppWorld、SWE-Gym、DBBench。价值在把变体诱发的行为学进策略本身，而非推理时挂更多工具。
