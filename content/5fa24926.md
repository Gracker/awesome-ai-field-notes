## English Abstract

AI agents store text as embedding vectors and compress them at scale. A compression is accepted when each item's nearest neighbors survive, but agents also consolidate, delete, and compare memories, tasks that depend on which items lie far apart. We evaluate both ends with two label-free fidelities from our prior work: the local fidelity L at 10 neighbors and the global fidelity G at 30 to 50 percent of the memory. At 96 bytes per passage, rescored binary codes reach an L of 0.97 yet move 10.1 percent of passages to another theme; PCA with 8-bit codes, at an L of 0.77, moves 1.7 percent. Rescoring leaves the stored codes, and thus G, unchanged. We prove that across the G band, 1−ν_k is at most 2 to 2.18 times the square root of the near-far rank-violation rate, and that this rate follows the distance noise of the compression (correlation 0.979). Our locality-aware quantization (LAQ) turns the bound into a hinge objective on the codebooks of an OPQ index, keeping its bytes, rotation, and search. To our knowledge, LAQ is the first quantizer whose objective comes from a proven bound on the far end. Reconstruction alone raises G in 0 of 52 held-out runs; the far hinge raises it in all 52. LAQ raises G in all 52 (p=8.5×10⁻⁴ over 13 memory-encoder groups), including the external FinDER memory, has the highest G of seven equal-size quantizers in all 12 Multi-Doc-2025 runs, scales to a million passages, and keeps evidence recall.

## English Summary

Agent memories stored as embeddings are usually compressed under a nearest-neighbor fidelity criterion, yet consolidation, deletion, and comparison depend on far-apart structure. The paper measures both ends with label-free fidelities: local fidelity L (10 neighbors) and global fidelity G (30-50% of memory). At 96 bytes per passage, rescored binary codes reach L=0.97 but move 10.1% of passages across themes, while PCA with 8-bit codes at L=0.77 moves 1.7%. A proven bound ties 1−ν_k to the near-far rank-violation rate, which correlates 0.979 with compression distance noise. Locality-aware quantization (LAQ) turns the bound into a hinge objective on OPQ codebooks without extra bytes: the far hinge raises G in all 52 held-out runs, and LAQ in all 52 (p=8.5×10⁻⁴), scaling to a million passages while keeping evidence recall.
