## English Summary

openai/codex issue #48195 (opened Sep 25, 2026, still open) asks to make daemon_auto_start opt-in: since 0.157.0, merely opening an interactive codex session starts a persistent background daemon (codex app-server --managed-daemon) with no TTY that reparents to PID 1 after every window closes, until daemon stop or a reboot. It also copies the whole ~374MB platform package into ~/.codex/packages/app-server-daemon/releases/<version>/ and runs from that copy, decoupling the backend version from npm. Amplified on Nov 10, 2026 by an HN front page (~610 points / 294 comments): comments share daemon_auto_start = false in config.toml as the workaround, report old-version disk piles around 12GB, and claim auto-updates have broken permissions.
