## English Abstract / Summary

Across 500 single-error note pairs (298 omission, 202 added-or-altered) the authors show judge discrimination reaches 0.79-0.94 for additions/alterations but only 0.50-0.63 for omissions; on single notes no judge design flags omissions significantly above the perfect-note baseline, and rephrasing, voting, and prompt tweaks only shift the operating point. Recovery requires task restructuring: enumerate facts from the transcript first, then check each one. The per-fact pipeline reaches 36.9% single-call detection at 2.7% false-positive rate, ten times cheaper than the single-call baseline; doctors sided 10/10 with the pipeline on disagreements.
