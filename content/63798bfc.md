## English Abstract / Summary

Sean Goedecke frames "workslop" as an effort-asymmetry DoS: producing AI text is cheap, reading it is not. He recommends (1) authoritative stop-words, (2) treating the AI-text sender as a high-latency Slack interface to Claude Code, (3) using AI on AI to summarize or replace the text, (4) trading asynchronous AI text for synchronous calls so it cannot survive, and (5) actively ignoring external status updates and PR-style notes when they do not matter. He adds a boundary caveat: text someone genuinely iterated on is not workslop, and AI style is not a reliable filter.
