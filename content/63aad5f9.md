## English Summary

ShellBench introduces 100 everyday agent tasks drawn from more than 10 million real user requests, each set in a realistic workspace with messy files and competing data sources, graded for correctness and safety. The evaluation spans 12 models x 5 agent harnesses x 3 reasoning levels for 53,100 rollouts (20% on public-release tasks). Model choice matters more than harness choice, but the three leading models sit within 1.1 percentage points: GPT-6 Astra leads at 62.5% while GPT-6.1 Sol passes 61.4% at about one-sixth of Astra's estimated token cost per attempt. Harness choice barely moves performance or safety - pass@1 spans 42.9 (Claude Code) to 45.2 (OpenClaw) across all five harnesses. Practical read: budget goes to the model, not the harness.

## Source Excerpts

### Abstract (opencli arxiv paper, 2026-11-26)

ShellBench: 100 day-to-day tasks based on more than 10 million user requests, each set in a realistic workspace with messy files and competing data sources, and graded for both correctness and safety. We evaluate 12 models across 5 agent harnesses at 3 reasoning levels for a total of 53,100 rollouts. Model choice impacts performance more than harness choice, but the three leading models differ by only 1.1 percentage points. GPT-6.1 Sol passed 61.4% of attempts at about one-sixth of GPT-6 Astra's estimated token cost per attempt, while GPT-6 Astra led at 62.5%. Harness choice had little effect on either performance or safety with all harnesses scoring between 42.9 (Claude Code) and 45.2 (OpenClaw) for pass@1.
