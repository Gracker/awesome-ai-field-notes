## Abstract（节选原文）

> Agent decisions depend on empirical relationships that deployment observations reveal and task descriptions alone may leave unresolved. We introduce Decoupled Predictive Decision Making (DPDM), an architecture that formulates heterogeneous agent decisions as conditional prediction with a shared pretrained learner. Language models provide semantic representations of current and historical records, while a general-purpose conditional predictor predicts task-defined targets from the represented evidence. Pretraining supplies reusable inference capability; deployment evidence instantiates problem-specific predictions without updating either model's neural parameters. Tool judgments, candidate preferences, and reasoning assessments share an evidence-and-query interface, with predictions mapped to choices by a decision rule.

## 中文摘要

DPDM 把异构 agent 决策统一成条件预测：语言模型只做语义编码，冻结的 LimiX-2 条件预测器结合历史证据预测任务目标，部署时两侧参数都不更新。摘要报告 Qwen3.5-27B 在 50 个基准的均值从 few-shot 78.80% 提到 84.92%，但历史预算不同；相同历史与查询条件下为 82.33%。架构意义：把"语言预训练的语义知识"与"部署证据上的经验推断"拆成互补两路，工具判断、候选偏好、推理评估共用同一证据-查询接口。
