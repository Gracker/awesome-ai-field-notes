## English

### Productive Launches 5.0 With AI Agents That Free Teams From Routine Work

Productive, the project management platform, has released version 5.0 introducing AI Agents and a comprehensive suite of intelligence features.

**What's New in Productive 5.0:**

**AI Agents (Beta):**
- Autonomous agents that handle routine operational tasks without human presence
- Configurable skills that guide how AI Assistant or Agents respond in specific contexts
- Connectors for integrating with existing tools and workflows

**Intelligence Suite:**
- **Time Tracking Intelligence**: AI-powered tracking that categorizes and reports time automatically
- **Reporting Intelligence**: Automated insights and anomaly detection in project reports
- **Meeting Transcription**: AI transcription with summarization and action item extraction

**Key Features:**
- Skills (Beta): Reusable instruction sets for context-specific AI behavior
- Connectors: Integration framework for third-party tool connectivity
- agents that operate on triggers without user presence
- Suite of intelligence features covering operational workflows

**Target:**
Designed to take on routine operational work and free up teams for strategic and client-focused tasks. The launch is informed by Productive's original agency management focus, now expanded to AI-powered automation.

## 中文

### Productive 5.0 发布：AI Agents 让团队从日常工作中解放出来

项目管理平台 Productive 发布 5.0 版本，引入 AI Agents 和全面的智能功能套件。

**Productive 5.0 新增功能：**

**AI Agents（Beta）：**
- 自主 agent，无需人工干预即可处理日常运营任务
- 可配置技能，指导 AI Assistant 或 Agents 在特定场景中的响应方式
- Connectors：与现有工具和工作流集成的连接框架

**智能套件：**
- **时间追踪智能**：AI 驱动的追踪，自动对时间进行分类和报告
- **报告智能**：项目报告中的自动洞察和异常检测
- **会议转录**：AI 转录，支持摘要生成和行动项提取

**核心功能：**
- Skills（Beta）：用于特定上下文 AI 行为的可复用指令集
- Connectors：第三方工具连接的集成框架
- 基于触发器运行、无需用户在线的 agent
- 覆盖运营工作流的智能功能套件

**目标用户：**
旨在承担日常运营工作，让团队专注于战略性和客户服务任务。此次发布基于 Productive 原有的代理商管理定位，扩展至 AI 驱动的自动化领域。
