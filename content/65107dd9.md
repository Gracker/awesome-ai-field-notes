Diallo walks through AI model shopping with real numbers: benchmark scores clustered within 0.9 points (Opus 94.8, Mythos 96.1, Fable 92.7) while prices are not - Sol scored 99.1 versus Terra's 98.2 on an enterprise productivity benchmark but cost $14k more per month, so the bill, not the score, picks Luna. Practical picks: try DeepSeek R1 before V4, stop at Qwen 3.8 MoE, Kimi K paired with Codex, and for coding just use Claude Code or Cursor. His own stack: Jules plus Antigravity, Continue over local Ollama-hosted R1 Flash in VS Code, Qwen-VL for camera feeds. The decision variable that actually matters is the team's budget and fault tolerance.
