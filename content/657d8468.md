## 英文原文

One shift I've found powerful: don't wire a single model straight into your workflow. Run critical questions across multiple models, compare the reasoning, surface disagreements, then synthesize. Tools like ConvergePanel make that process structured instead of ad hoc — which changes how you build.

The builders in 2026 won't just prompt better. They'll design better decision pipelines.

Using AI is asking it for answers.

Building with AI is deciding where it's allowed to be wrong, where it must be verified, and how its output flows into real decisions.

## 中文翻译

我发现一个很有用的转变：不要把单一模型直接接入你的工作流。让关键问题跨多个模型运行，比较推理过程，暴露分歧，然后综合结果。像 ConvergePanel 这样的工具让这个过程变得结构化而非临时性——这改变了你构建产品的方式。

2026 年的构建者不会只是更好地写 prompt。他们会设计更好的决策管道。

使用 AI 是在向它要答案。

用 AI 构建是在决定它在哪里可以被允许犯错，在哪里必须被验证，以及它的输出如何流入真实决策。
