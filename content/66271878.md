## English Summary

Gary Marcus assembles recent Anthropic items into an argument: IPO-adjacent employee surveys floated the possibility of the stock going to zero while the public narrative promotes $30-trillion-scale financial imagination; meanwhile Thomson Reuters announced on Aug 25 it is moving internal AI from Claude to a self-built Qwen-based model, its CTO saying renting frontier intelligence is renting while building on open source is owning. Marcus reads this as the crack between the valuation story and customer reality: enterprises are voting with their feet toward open source and self-building. A pointed opinion piece -- the data points are real, the conclusion carries the author's usual critical stance.
