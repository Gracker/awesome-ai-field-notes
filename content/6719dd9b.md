## English Abstract

I wrote a specification for a new terminal escape sequence: OSC 7501, the Program Status Protocol. It lets any program tell the terminal what it's doing: idle, working, waiting on the user, finished, or failed, and why. The post covers why this protocol needs to exist, why existing approaches aren't good enough (especially for coding agents), and how the protocol works. It is a completely generic, terminal-native specification and protocol.

## English Summary

OSC 7501 is a proposed terminal escape sequence for programs to self-declare structured status (state, kind, app, message), replacing title guessing, screen scraping, and private inbox APIs for observing background agents.
