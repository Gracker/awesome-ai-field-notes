## English Summary

Survey of self-evolving multimodal agents across four scenarios - multimodal reasoning, multimodal generation, GUI/Web navigation, and embodied manipulation - formalizing the agent via four criteria plus a differentia of self-reference and a three-stage loop of experience generation, feedback acquisition, and update consolidation. Because each self-improvement mechanism is designed and evaluated within a single scenario, the survey asks which mechanisms transfer and why transfer is asymmetric: each mechanism is recast as a trade-off that raises a scarce factor by presupposing a task-structural resource such as output equivalence or checkable outcomes, judged portable, conditional, blocked, or redundant. Asymmetry is attributed to divergent combinations of scarce factors and resources across transfer directions; matched-budget measurements are proposed to test the verdicts.
