## English Summary

IEEE Spectrum (Nov 2, 2026) reviews the PHP2Shell episode as a signal about AI and legacy software debt. The out-of-memory bug behind PHP2Shell was reported to the PHP Foundation back in 2024 and left unaddressed because it 'just' crashed; AI made this class of painful, specific reasoning tractable, and the flaw affected NASA, Wikipedia, the White House, and the WordPress/Drupal ecosystems before a patch landed ahead of exploitation. Matt Suiche (AI security research lead at Tolmo): 'all the legacy software that was written by humans over the last 40 years is being exposed... Now it is time for the technical debt to be paid off.' Suman Jana (Columbia) on crash-only reports not taken seriously. Complements the Wiz and AWS MadPot disclosures with an engineering-debt perspective.
