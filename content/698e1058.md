# 神经符号 AI 解 RAG 规模化失效：Ontology 加确定性层加 LLM 解释层

> **EN原文：**  
> "RAG is not broken, naive vector-only RAG fails at scale. What's broken is the idea that meaning = vector similarity. At scale, that collapses. The future is neurosymbolic AI: • ontologies for structure • deterministic layers for truth • LLMs for interpretation (not computation) That's how you eliminate retrieval-driven hallucinations."

## 中文翻译

AI (@DeepLearn007)

RAG 并未坏掉，但朴素的纯向量 RAG 在规模化时会失效。真正坏掉的是"含义 = 向量相似度"这个假设。一旦规模扩大，这个假设就会崩溃。未来的方向是神经符号 AI：

• **Ontology** 用于结构化
• **确定性层** 用于确保真实性
• **LLM** 用于解释（而非计算）

这才是消除检索驱动幻觉的正确方式。

> 引用 Stanford 研究者的发现："RAG 是坏的，没人谈论它。'语义崩溃'正在杀死每一个'能读你文档的 AI 产品'。当你的知识库达到临界规模时，这种情况就会发生。"

## 原始英文

AI (@DeepLearn007)

RAG is not broken, naive vector-only RAG fails at scale. What's broken is the idea that meaning = vector similarity. At scale, that collapses. The future is neurosymbolic AI:

• ontologies for structure
• deterministic layers for truth
• LLMs for interpretation (not computation)

That's how you eliminate retrieval-driven hallucinations.

"Stanford researchers exposed the fatal flaw killing every 'AI that reads your docs' product in existence. It's called 'Semantic Collapse,' and it happens the second your knowledge base hits critical mass."
