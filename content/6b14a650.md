## 中文摘要
macOS 27.3 beta 2 把 AI agent 权限模型做细：27.2 统一的 Device Control and Data Access 拆成 Accessibility（走 entitlement 审批）与新的 System and App Protection Override（控制别的应用/自动化）。用户可按应用授权、要求每次使用重新申请；Full Disk Access 审批界面重做，可只授权特定文件夹，已授权限继续有效。官方理由直白：越来越多'有能力且自主'的 AI agent 需要系统权限；依赖旧式全局授权的自动化脚本要重写授权路径。

## English Summary
macOS 27.3 beta 2 refines the permission model for AI agents: the unified 'Device Control and Data Access' toggle from 27.2 splits into Accessibility (assistive tech, entitlement review) and a new System and App Protection Override for apps that bypass system protections to control other apps or automate tasks. Users can scope the override per app (Mail yes, Messages no), require re-authorization per use instead of one-time grants, and a menu-bar indicator shows when an app controls others in the background. Full Disk Access review is redesigned to state which protections are bypassed and allow folder-scoped grants; existing grants and underlying APIs stay valid. Apple's stated reason: increasingly capable and autonomous AI agents need system permissions, and developers must not obtain them without explicit authorization.

Last month, Apple [announced](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/02/apple-says-its-tightening-macos-privacy-controls-amid-the-rise-of-ai-agents/) its plans to tighten macOS privacy controls around Full Disk Access amid the rise of “increasingly capable and autonomous” AI agents.

With today’s new macOS 27.3 beta, Apple is implementing several changes as part of this.

In a post on its [developer website last month](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=p6zjojqw), Apple said some developers are “using Full Disk Access in ways that could put users at risk, exposing everything on their systems.” As such, it said that it would “introduce additional controls to ensure that users who genuinely wish to grant an app this extraordinary level of access can only do so with very explicit user action.”

## What’s changing in macOS 27.3

In macOS 27.2, “Device Control and Data Access” is a unified toggle in System Settings, where Apple says that apps with that level of access can “view and send email, edit contacts and photos, monitor your keyboard, track websites, record your screen, control any app on your Mac, and more.”

-   Accessibility: Intended specifically for assistive technologies, with permissions handled through an entitlement process.
