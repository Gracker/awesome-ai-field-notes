## 原文摘录

It's impossible to deny that, despite all the slop/data centers/environmental destruction/theft, LLMs are incredibly useful and powerful. It's also impossible to deny that OpenAI and Anthropic suck and you can't trust them with your data. Luckily, you don't need them. You also don't need a prohibitively expensive computer with lots of GPUs and RAM, and you don't need to be an expert on LLMs.

Models can be completely closed and proprietary, like OpenAI's GPT models or Anthropic's Claude models... Or they can be "open weight," which means anyone can download and host them. Unlike closed models, it's possible to run open weight models privately. The most powerful open weight models are from Chinese companies, but some are also from US companies and from around the world.
