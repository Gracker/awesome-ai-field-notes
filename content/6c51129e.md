## 中文导读

Kimi K3.1 Pro 上线 Kimi API，Willison 第一手记录：美元定价每百万 token 输入 2 美元、输出 10 美元——与 GPT-6.1 Sol 同价、为 Claude Opus 5.5 的一半；另有 Highspeed 版快 2–3 倍、价格翻倍，仅输入缓存命中部分按同价计。权重尚未开放，Moonshot 承诺 2026 年 11 月 4 日前放出完整权重。Moonshot 自家 benchmark 显示与 Opus 5.5、GPT-6 Astra 大致持平；Artificial Analysis 打 55 分，压过 Claude Fable 5.1、GPT-6 Astra、Gemini 4 Argon（均 53），仅次 Opus 5.5 的 58 分——Willison 指出这是即将开放权重的模型第一次站到这个位置。他用 llm openai endpoint 接 Moonshot API，以 low/medium/max 三档推理力度跑了 pelican-on-a-bicycle SVG 冒烟测试。

## English Summary

Simon Willison's October 23 hands-on with Kimi K3.1 Pro, live on the Kimi API ahead of the promised full-weight release by November 4, 2026: USD pricing is $2/million input and $10/million output tokens - the same as GPT-6.1 Sol and half of Claude Opus 5.5 - with a Highspeed tier that is 2-3x faster at twice the price (cache-hit input tokens priced the same). Moonshot's own benchmarks put it roughly level with Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Astra, and Artificial Analysis scores it 55, ahead of Claude Fable 5.1, GPT-6 Astra, and Gemini 4 Argon (all 53) and behind only Opus 5.5 (58) - in Willison's reading the first time a soon-to-be open-weight model has scored this close to the frontier. He smoke-tests it through the Moonshot API with his pelican-on-a-bicycle SVG prompt across low/medium/max reasoning efforts.
