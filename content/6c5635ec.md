## 中文导读

Ed Zitron 2026-10-16 的 Premium 通讯（39 分钟阅读）。开篇即定罪：十四年前政府犯了一个错误，让科技公司与风投得以合法地诈骗数百万零售投资者——既剥夺了他们的信息，又虚假抬高了此后所有大型科技 IPO 的估值。机制是 JOBS 法案废掉了 1964 年 Section 12(g) 的披露扳机（500 股东即须按公众公司披露，员工持股计入），把门槛抬到 2000 且不再计算员工持股，公司从此失去上市动力，估值交给一级市场自我循环。

## 为什么值得关注

Anthropic 正在筹备的 $2T IPO 是这篇文章的活体展品：$200B 远期收入承诺、$30T TAM、零收入的 Instinct 被标到 $100 亿——理解这套估值传导机制，比争论"泡沫破不破"更有信息量。

## 关键信息

- JOBS 法案：2012-04-05 奥巴马签署、两党通过；Section 12(g) 500 股东披露门槛 → 2000 股东 + 员工持股不计入
- 12(g) 曾是上市扳机：Facebook 2011 年预期跨越 500 股东、2012 年 IPO；Microsoft/Google 同样被这条规则推入公开市场
- Anthropic：IPO 叫价 $2 万亿（对标 Meta+SpaceX），依据是远期 $200B 年收入与 $30T TAM
- Instinct：零收入、靠发短信干活的 "AI agent"，红杉+Benchmark+Coatue 以 $1B 买 10% → 市场标价 $100 亿
- 对比：Microsoft 上市 $500M、Google $230 亿、Facebook $1000 亿——今天散户再无早期入口
- 结论与主张：当前系统激励有钱人 inflate 私人资产；作者"激进主张改革——如果不直接废除——JOBS 法案"

## English Source (public preview fetched via curl, 2026-10-19)

> On April 5, 2012, President Obama signed the Jumpstart Our Business Startups (JOBS) Act under the auspices of it being "too difficult" for small businesses and startups — though overwhelmingly the latter — to access capital, all while reducing reporting requirements in ways that mostly helped venture capitalists fund and startup founders run businesses in reckless and destructive ways.

> In 1964, Congress implemented a rule to securities law called Section 12(g) ... said that once a company of any real size had 500 shareholders, it had to start reporting like a public company.

> The JOBS act, amongst other changes, made it so that you could have up to 2,000 shareholders (but no more than 499 "unaccredited" investors) and, crucially, changed the rules so that employees were no longer counted in the calculation.

> That's how Instinct, a zero-revenue "AI agent" that you text to do stuff, is somehow worth $10 billion — because investors from Sequoia Capital, Benchmark, and Coatue had been willing to give...

> Anthropic's upcoming IPO has the company demanding a $2 trillion valuation, equivalent to both Meta and SpaceX, based on flimsy promises of $200 billion in annual revenue years in the future and a $30 trillion total addressable market.

> As Casual Finance noted, when Microsoft went public, it had a market cap of around $500 million, Google $23 billion, and Facebook $100 billion.

> Today I am going to aggressively advocate for reforms — if not an outright repeal — of the JOBS act, and call for serious legislative efforts to bring the private markets to heel.
