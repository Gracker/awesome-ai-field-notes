## Key claims (English)

Atria Dawn Preview is the open-weight release from Shanghai AI Lab / InternLM on Hugging Face at internlm/Atria-Dawn-Preview (HF API 200, likes ≈54, created 2026-09-11), with a 744B-parameter MoE, 256K context, and MIT license.
