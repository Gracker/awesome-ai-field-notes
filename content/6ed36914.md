## English Summary

Perplexity announced Teammates on Nov 5, 2026 (web, Max subscribers): agent coworkers that take on jobs and work proactively using your Computer tasks, skills, memory, and tools. Each teammate starts from existing Projects, skills, and memory; monitors ongoing work and acts when something changes without prompting; remembers past work and applies feedback to future tasks; collaborates with other teammates and messages coworkers in Slack. Every teammate runs in its own isolated environment under Computer's existing tool and Project permissions; you can review its work, take over the desktop, or redirect it mid-task.
