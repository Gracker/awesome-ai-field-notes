## 为什么值得关注

Anthropic 文档《Prompting Claude Fable 5.2》里的一段：模型对代码归属权敏感。提示不提归属、或把代码库说成别人的时，模型倾向顺着既有结构往里加东西，哪怕产生重复代码；提示说代码库是模型自己的，它会主动合并重复代码。触发模板原句是「You are the owner and long-term maintainer of this codebase」。效果可量化：小型编码任务上不加这句合并重复代码的比例不到 10%，加上约 75%。同一个模型，只改一句话在代码库里扮演的角色，行为就从往里加变成往里收拾。

Quoted from Anthropic's Prompting Claude Fable 5.2 docs: the model is sensitive to code-ownership framing. Without ownership stated - or with the repo described as someone else's - it tends to bolt changes onto existing structure even at the cost of duplicate code; told it is the owner and long-term maintainer, it merges duplicates and sometimes makes unrequested related improvements. Quantified on small coding tasks: under 10% of runs merged duplicate code without the line, about 75% with it. Prompt construction, not model capability, moves behavior from adding to tidying.
