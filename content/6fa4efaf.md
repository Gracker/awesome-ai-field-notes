## 中文导读

Sean Goedecke 回答「LLM 写完代码后人是否该去做更多胶水活儿」：不，且胶水活儿对公司比 2010 年代更不重要。四条理由：agent 放大效应让单人接下原需小团队的项目，协调型胶水活儿根本不存在了；ZIRP 结束后最难缠的「看门人」型工程师离场或改态度；junior 招得少，新人上手无人可托；证明工作量的 write-only 报告 LLM 秒出且本就没人读。他划了底线：给同事发消息不能外包给 LLM，「让他们跟你的 Claude Opus 谈话」最快毁掉关系。建议维持 2024 年位置：别当专职胶水人，让坏流程失败，时间花在驱动 agent 并对齐公司价值观上。

## English Summary

Sean Goedecke answers whether LLMs writing most code frees engineers for glue work: no - glue work matters less to companies than in the 2010s, for four reasons. AI agents shrink coordination itself, so single engineers run projects that needed teams; the end of ZIRP forced out or mellowed the prickliest gatekeeper engineers; fewer juniors removes onboarding glue; write-only proof-of-work reports are now instant with LLMs (they were never read anyway). Hard limit: communicating with other engineers cannot be delegated - nothing torches relationships faster than making colleagues talk to your Claude Opus instance. Stay where 2024 landed: avoid becoming the glue person, let bad processes fail, spend the time driving agents and aligning them with company values.

## 原文摘录 / Evidence Excerpts

I am not a believer in glue work. Processes that can only be sustained by the unrewarded sacrifice of engineers ought to die.

First, AI decreases the amount of coordination needed to ship. The magnifying effect of AI agents means that any single engineer can now take on projects that would previously have needed a small team.

Write-only reports can be automated with LLMs. ... It doesn't really matter that nobody wants to read LLM text, because the point of these reports was never to be read anyway.

Communicating to other engineers cannot be delegated to an LLM. ... Nothing will torch your relationship with other engineers as quickly as making them talk to your Claude Opus instance.

Most of your time and effort should still be spent on the code: on driving AI agents and aligning them with your company's values.
