## English Summary

Simon Willison's roundup of his AI Engineer Code Summit keynote: three inflection points for LLMs and coding agents in twelve months. First, coding agents in November 2025: Opus 4.5 was the first model where Claude Code's default result was usually correct, and GPT-5.1/Codex models turned reliable the same month. Second, Fable-class models in June-July: give a goal and tools and the model brute-forces the problem — Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.2 localized an annoying beep among boxes to 40cm with five WebAudio microphones, and Fable built its own Quartz screenshot mechanism and logging server to chase a Safari rendering bug. Third, frontier 3D modeling in September-October: told to make a bland RTS 'much more fun', Opus 5.5 spent 12 hours playtesting and rebalancing it into something playable. Whether Fable 5.2 is a fourth inflection is undecided: software you can describe, Fable can write.
