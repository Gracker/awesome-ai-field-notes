## 为什么值得关注

黄仁勋辞职后公司该怎么走，Blank 用愿景型 vs 执行型 CEO 框架分析：黄仁勋把 Nvidia 重做三遍——2006 年几乎无收入时投 CUDA、2012 年 ImageNet 后转向深度学习、2020 年收购 Mellanox 转卖整机架——都是商业模式重写。对照：鲍尔默输掉搜索、手机、媒体、云，纳德拉围绕云重组完成逆转，库克营收涨到 4160 亿却没打开新市场、错过 AI。继任者面对约 60 个直接下属、无接班梯队、加管理层即创意死亡螺旋，而最大客户正变成自研芯片的竞争者——每年新芯片、CUDA 护城河、向 AI 实验室投约 500 亿美元恰恰是最先被砍的赌注。

Blank reads Nvidia post-Huang through the visionary-versus-execution CEO lens: Huang reinvented the company three times (CUDA in 2006 ahead of revenue, deep learning after ImageNet 2012, Mellanox racks in 2020) - business-model rewrites, not product updates. Ballmer's Microsoft shows execution without new markets; Nadella shows recovery by reorganizing around cloud; Cook shows growth without opening new markets, missing AI. The successor inherits concrete hazards: about 60 direct reports, no succession bench, and an innovation death spiral once execution CEOs add management layers - while Nvidia's largest customers build their own AI chips, and the bets an execution CEO cuts first are exactly the ones that matter.
