## 中文导读

Mat Duggan 把每天约 20 亿行混合厂商防火墙 syslog 灌进 ClickHouse（14 天 TTL、2 分片×3 副本、Grafana 25 个面板）的踩坑复盘。防御式 WHERE 写法让同一台机器读取行数从 305 万涨到 2.5764 亿、耗时 115ms→1134ms，答案一样多读 84 倍行；改用 Grafana ad-hoc filter 后回到 467 万行，因为它把过滤条件下发为 additional_table_filters 查询设置、在规划阶段生效。排序键前缀决定主索引裁剪：低基数 observer 列前置让 tail-logs 面板读 2.918 亿行/158GiB/18.9s，重排后 2310 万行/936ms，而 MODIFY ORDER BY 只能在同次 ALTER 里追加列、不能重排，回填代价每分片约 520GiB。projection 路由不做等价推理：toStartOfHour(Timestamp)>=X 能路由到 2022 行，数值相同的 toStartOfInterval 写法静默回落读 1237 万行。他自测 10.8 倍提速后才发现是旧表 88% 分区已分层到 S3 造成的假对照；system.query_log 是这套系统里唯一会告诉你出错的地方。

## 为什么值得关注

OLAP 的错误模式是「不报错只变慢」，这篇把资源护栏、验证习惯与 query_log 定位写成可抄清单。

## 关键信息

- 护栏：max_rows_to_read 10 亿、max_bytes_to_read 100GB、max_execution_time 60s
- 防御式 WHERE：305 万行→2.5764 亿行（115ms→1134ms）；ad-hoc filter 回到 467 万行
- 排序键重排：2.918 亿行/158GiB/18.9s → 2310 万行/936ms；回填约 520GiB/分片
- projection 路由不做等价推理；10.8 倍「提速」来自 S3 分层差异

## English Summary

Mat Duggan's war story feeding ~2 billion rows/day of firewall syslog into ClickHouse (14-day TTL, 2 shards x 3 replicas, 25 Grafana dashboards): a defensive WHERE clause read 257.64M rows instead of 3.05M (115ms to 1134ms) for the same answer; Grafana ad-hoc filters push predicates down as additional_table_filters at planning time. Sort-key prefix mistakes cost 291.8M rows/158GiB/18.9s until reordered (MODIFY ORDER BY can only append within one ALTER; backfill ~520GiB per shard). Projection routing does no equivalence reasoning, and his celebrated 10.8x speedup turned out to be an S3-tiering artifact. system.query_log is the only place that tells you something is wrong.
