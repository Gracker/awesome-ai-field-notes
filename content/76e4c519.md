## Summary (EN)
Sean Goedecke's detailed analysis of historical Luddism based on multiple historical texts. Real Luddites were skilled craftspeople (7-year apprenticeships) whose trade was being devalued by automation making their hard-learned skills obsolete. Their anger was a survival response, not simple technophobia. The 1810s war-related economic deterioration amplified the damage. The author cautions against simplistic analogies to today's anti-AI movement — the mechanisms of skill displacement differ — but historical understanding enriches current debates about AI datacenters.
