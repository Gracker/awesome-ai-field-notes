## English Summary

Moonshot AI has released the full weights of Kimi K3.1 Pro on Hugging Face (repo created Nov 2, 2026; 96 safetensors shards): the first open-weights model of the K3.1 series. Card specs: 2.8T total / 104B activated MoE parameters, 93 layers, 1,048,576-token context, image-text-to-text with vision tower. Weights are quantization-aware trained: MXFP4 weights with MXFP8 activations via compressed-tensors on Linear layers, excluding attention, shared experts, MoE expert projections, lm_head, and the vision tower. Custom kimi-k3.1-pro license. This completes the earlier API/benchmark coverage from Oct 24 with the actual weight drop.
