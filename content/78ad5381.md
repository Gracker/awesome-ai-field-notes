## 中文导读

OpenAI 为回应欧盟 AI Act 的文本可识别要求，公开文本水印方案 textGrain：在模型选词阶段植入统计信号，检测端据此判断一段文本是否带有 OpenAI 水印。技术报告已发布（entropy-calibrated watermarking），并计划开源。官方明确把“水印能告诉人什么、不能告诉什么”写进方案：文本水印与检测仍是早期技术，其局限本身就是发布内容的一部分。

性能与局限同时给出：1% 目标假阳率下，200-token 短文检出率约 80%，更长文本约 95%；对 400 token 心理学科普文本替换 10% 同义词，检出率从 92% 降到 66%，25% 时只剩 17%；数学题等词面自由度低的文本天然更难检测。质量侧，加水印前后在 Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index、DeepSWE v1.1、Terminal-Bench 4.0 等 8 项基准上的差异都在噪声范围内（如 49.57→49.76、72.80→71.68）。落地节奏分三步：API 全球可选开（默认关）、未来几周对欧盟 ChatGPT/Codex 合规输出加隐形水印、检测器先只开放给审核过的研究机构与专家组织；图像/音频的验证工具（openai.com/verify、Content Provenance API）不受影响，继续对组织公开。

## English Summary

OpenAI published its approach to text watermarking for EU AI Act compliance: textGrain embeds a statistical signal in the model's word choices and a detector assesses whether a passage carries an OpenAI watermark; a technical report is out and open-sourcing is planned. Limitations are quantified: at a 1% target false-positive rate, detection is about 80% on 200-token passages versus about 95% on longer text; substituting 10% of words with synonyms cuts detection from about 92% to 66%, and 25% substitution leaves 17%. Benchmark impact stays within noise across 8 evaluations. Rollout: global API opt-in (off by default), invisible watermark on eligible EU ChatGPT/Codex output within weeks, detector limited to approved researchers; image/audio verification tools remain public.

Today, we're sharing our approach to text watermarking in response to the EU AI Act, and how it fits into our broader work. [...] Our text watermarking technology, textGrain, adds an invisible statistical signal to the model's word choices. Our detector looks for that signal to assess whether a passage contains an OpenAI watermark. [...]

**Shorter or more constrained text is harder to detect.** At a target false positive rate of 1%, our detector identified watermarks in about 80% of 200-token passages, compared with about 95% of [longer passages]. [...]
