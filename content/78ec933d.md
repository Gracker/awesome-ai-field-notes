# Introducing Gemini 3.8 Live and 3.8 Live Extended Thinking

> Source: [https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-8-live-gemini-3-8-live-extended-thinking/](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-8-live-gemini-3-8-live-extended-thinking/) · platform: google-blog · authors: Tom Ouyang, Malini Jaganathan (Gemini Audio Team) · date: 2026-09-15

## TL;DR（中文摘要）

Google 发布 Gemini 3.8 Live 与 3.8 Live Extended Thinking 两档实时对话模型：Live 主打流式对话 + 近实时视觉 grounding，97 种语言会话中自动切换，工具调用后台执行不打断对话；ET 档面向高复杂度任务，边推理边说话，拿下 Artificial Analysis Speech to Speech Quality Index 第一（82.6）、tau-Voice agentic 68.6%、tau-Voice-banking 35.1%、Big Bench Audio 97.7%，Live 档居 Speech Agent Arena 第二。两档已通过 Gemini API、Workspace（Docs/Gmail/Keep Live）与 Search Live 提供选型要点：ET High 的 82.6/68.6% 不等于默认 Live 档成绩，实时语音 agent 先改状态机与异步工具再选档。

## Summary (English)

Google launches Gemini 3.8 Live and 3.8 Live Extended Thinking: Live targets fluid conversation with near-real-time visual grounding, 97-language mid-conversation switching, and background tool execution; Extended Thinking takes #1 on Artificial Analysis' Speech to Speech Quality Index (82.6), 68.6% on tau-Voice agentic, 35.1% on tau-Voice-banking, 97.7% on Big Bench Audio, while Live ranks second in the Speech Agent Arena. Available via Gemini API, Workspace, and Search Live.

## 入库依据（同日 digest 交叉验证）

调研卡 2026-09-16-Gemini38Live-选型（价格 $0.005/$0.018 per min 音频、AA/AArena 数据）；官方博客全文抓取核实（82.6/68.6%/35.1%/97.7%/97 languages）。
