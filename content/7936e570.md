## English Summary

JetBrains case study linking five weeks of coding-agent token usage to commits of 404 engineers plus a survey of 412 employees, asking what token spend actually measures. Spend varied mostly between engineers, and the biggest spenders stayed largely the same over five weeks; among weeks with similar commit counts, the 90th-percentile spender used 39-240x the tokens of the 10th. Within an engineer, doubling spend was associated with about 10% more commits; available work metrics explained 38% of between-engineer variance and 13% of week-to-week variance. Nearly nine of ten technical respondents used agents for work beyond coding - analysis, research, planning - so token spend measures how much engineers use agents, not the outcome or value of that work; the authors recommend it only for resource accounting, evaluating agent-assisted work via task and outcome evidence instead.
