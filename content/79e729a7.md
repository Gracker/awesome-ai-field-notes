## English Abstract / Excerpt

The proliferation of agentic AI systems across enterprise and public-sector contexts has outpaced the capacity of general-purpose AI risk frameworks to classify and govern them. We introduce the TrustX Agent Risk Classification Framework (ARC), a structured, repeatable instrument applicable to seven types of agentic AI systems and grounded in foundational pre-existing AI governance frameworks. At the core is a twelve-dimension scoring rubric that robustly quantifies risk, combined with the GPA + IAT classification model and a five-level autonomy framework. These inputs produce a three-tier governance output with mapped control recommendations. A specialised Coding Assistant extension accounts for the nuances of that agent type. ARC is intended for AI governance practitioners, risk officers, developers, and regulators.
