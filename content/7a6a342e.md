## 中文导读

Anthropic Frontier Red Team 记录了 2026 年 10 月的一次内部追查：员工让研究模型在不给任何线索的前提下破解 NIST 椭圆曲线种子来源之谜，Claude 不到半小时确认 P-192、P-224、P-256、P-384、P-521 五个种子全部来自以 Unix 时间戳播种的 C 库 lrand48 伪随机数生成器，而不是传闻中的口令哈希。方法上先滑动扫描 34 种 1990 年代生成器与输出切片组合，认出四个种子高位全零的模式（概率 1/5810），再用 LLL 格基规约从截断输出恢复状态，十个素域种子都符合 lrand48() >> 23，反推的初始化时间全部落在 1997 到 1998 年。这不是密码学破绽，反而让种子含后门的猜测更不可信；六个 1997 年 5 月生成的曲线基点也被溯源到 FreeLIP 的随机数生成器。

## 原文要点

- Claude wrote a scanner that slid along each published seed one bit at a time and tested 34 combinations of generator and output slices from 1990s-vintage C PRNGs.
- Four of the ten seeds had a 0 in the top bit for all 20 words (chance 1/5810) - the signature of lrand48 returning only the top 31 bits of a 48-bit state.
- State recovery: brute-force the 2^17 hidden bits of the first word and verify the next four words; truncated outputs handled as a textbook lattice problem solved with LLL basis reduction over every window.
- Every recovered srand48 initialization was a Unix timestamp between May 1997 and April 1998; the four binary-curve initializations read as February 1984, mapping to April 17, 1998 when doubled.
- Base points of the six curves generated in May 1997 (P-192 plus five ANSI X9.62 example curves) come from the FreeLIP long-integer package RNG, also shipped in early NTL.
- Article quotes Claude's internal reasoning at the moment of discovery, including its self-checks for simulation artifacts and plausibility.
