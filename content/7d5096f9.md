## English Summary

Alibaba's Qwen speech team published the Qwen-Audio-3.1 series page. The ASR line, Qwen-Audio-3.1-ASR, ships in Flash and Next tiers covering 30 languages and 16 Chinese dialects, with non-streaming speaker diarization and general audio understanding in Next. The realtime line, Qwen-Audio-3.1-Realtime, focuses on spoken reasoning and tool use, with a persistent voice agent packaged as a separate runtime extension. The page also aggregates the 3.0 generation (ASR/TTS, Fun-ASR, CosyVoice 3) plus demo and technical-report entry points, forming a full listen-to-speak stack. A first-hand entry point for anyone comparing on-device or server-side ASR or selecting voice-agent building blocks.
