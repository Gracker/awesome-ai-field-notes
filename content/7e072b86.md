## 摘要（中文）

Google 发布面向规模化 agent 的 Flash 组合：3.6 Flash（相对 3.5 Flash 宣称更少 output token、更高 agent/coding 相关指标）、3.5 Flash-Lite（高吞吐子任务）、3.5 Flash Cyber（安全/漏洞相关多 agent 流程）。叙事从通用聊天转向 agent 执行层的成本、延迟与专用模型分工。ClawFeed/厂商文给出 DeepSWE、Terminal-Bench 等对比数字，需以官方页与独立评测交叉；对产品方重点是单位任务成本与工具调用结构，而非单榜分数。

## Summary (English)

Google introduces Gemini 3.6 Flash, 3.5 Flash-Lite, and 3.5 Flash Cyber aimed at building AI agents at scale—efficiency, latency, and reliability. Positioning shifts Flash from general chat toward an agent execution stack: a stronger Flash for complex workflows, Lite for high-throughput subagents, and Cyber for security-oriented multi-agent reporting. Treat vendor benchmark headlines as directional; track unit-task cost, tool-call structure, and refusal/safety boundaries in your own harness.
