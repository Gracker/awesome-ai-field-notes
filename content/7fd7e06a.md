## 原文要点

- Anthropic monorepo changes landing per day went from about 2,500 in May to more than 30,000 in September 2026 (engineering post on redesigning source control).
- Layer 1: work verified before anyone looks - give Claude something runnable (tests, build, screenshot to compare), ask it to show output, then a subagent reviews the diff with fresh context; internal skills hunt for bugs, simplify diffs, and check end-to-end behavior.
- Boris Cherny: giving Claude a way to verify its work is probably the most important thing for good results, estimating it makes the final result 2-3x better.
- REVIEW.md written by code owners says what failure looks like in that repo, caps nits, lists paths to skip, and asks for file-and-line evidence instead of guesses; teams separately check whether the reviewer actually follows it.
- When the automated reviewer keeps flagging the same thing, fix it at the source: in the skill, with a lint rule, or by updating CLAUDE.md/REVIEW.md.
- Goal: most PRs merge with at most one human in the loop (who can be the author); revert and incident rates are the guardrail that human time was actually saved, not moved.
