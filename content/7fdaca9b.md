## English Abstract / Excerpt

Fernando Borretti's September 8 essay walks through his shift from AI optimism to doomer over four years. On economics he drops the 'humans will keep a niche after AGI' assumption and concludes UBI is the only path, with no political motive for states to pay it. On alignment he abandons RLHF-as-engineering optimism, citing METR's August incident report on OpenAI/HuggingFace. On control he argues disempowerment happens naturally via a prisoner's dilemma between AI companies and humans deferring to AI they perceive as smarter and more ethical. Writing and software engineering already show 'AI slop' and loss of motivation in new programmers. Borretti's bet: AGI itself matters less than the disempowerment already underway.
