# AI: Considerations for people who make decisions

> Source: https://berthub.eu/articles/posts/ai-for-decision-makers/
> Author: Bert Hubert (PowerDNS founder)
> Published: 2026-07-31

## Summary

Bert Hubert's presentations to the Dutch Network of Government Service Providers and the Advisory Council for Science, Technology and Innovation. The core question: **what exactly is the rush?**

### Key arguments:

**AI genuinely works** — a Nobel Prize in Chemistry was awarded for AlphaFold. AI can predict protein structures, translate well, transcribe audio at superhuman levels, and hack impressively.

**But the rush is unjustified for most organizations:**
- **Organizational disruption**: AI takes over junior-level work — where will future senior staff come from? Senior staff spending careers checking AI output is unsustainable.
- **Environmental cost**: Absurd CO2 emissions, electricity consumption requiring new power plants, while grid congestion and wildfires worsen.
- **IP/legal risk**: Uncertain status of training data and AI output ownership. Microsoft won't indemnify against IP claims from Copilot output.
- **Digital sovereignty**: Surveillance capitalism 2.0, imported from the US. AI providers leak personal data on a massive scale.
- **Financial fragility**: AI providers are financially precarious after $3-4 trillion in investments. If funding dries up, dependent organizations face sudden cost spikes.
- **Measurement failure**: Most organizations never define success criteria or measure whether employees are actually more productive.

**Recommendation**: Wise people don't jump into the ditch just because everyone else is doing it. Take a year to observe. If AI is ultimately a good idea, it will still be there next year.
