## English

# What 81,000 people want from AI

**Source:** Anthropic Features
**URL:** https://www.anthropic.com/features/81k-interviews
**Date:** May 2026 (study conducted December 2025)

## Scale

80,508 participants across 159 countries and 70 languages — largest and most multilingual qualitative study ever conducted.

## What People Want (9 categories)

1. **Professional excellence** (18.8%) — AI handles routine so humans focus on strategic work
2. **Personal transformation** (13.7%) — AI as guide for growth, wellbeing, behavior change
3. **Life management** (13.5%) — AI as cognitive scaffolding for schedules and mental burden reduction
4. **Time freedom** (11.1%) — Reclaim time for family, hobbies, rest
5. **Financial independence** (9.7%) — Income generation, business building, investments
6. **Societal transformation** (9.4%) — Solve poverty, disease, climate via AI
7. **Entrepreneurship** (8.7%) — Build and scale businesses with AI as force multiplier
8. **Learning & growth** (8.4%) — AI as personalized teacher and learning accelerator
9. **Creative expression** (5.6%) — Overcome barriers between imagination and execution

## Where AI Has Delivered

1. **Productivity** (32%) — Dramatically sped up work, automated repetitive tasks
2. **Cognitive partnership** (17.2%) — AI as thinking partner and creative collaborator
3. **Learning** (9.9%) — Adaptive explanations, patient tutoring, on-demand expertise
4. **Technical accessibility** (8.7%) — Non-developers shipping apps, solo creators doing team-scale work
5. **Research synthesis** (7.2%) — Literature review, distilling sources, making sense of complex material
6. **Emotional support** (6.1%) — Judgment-free space to talk, processing difficult situations

## Key Insights

- 81% of people say AI has taken a step toward their stated vision
- 11% see productivity benefits as ultimately a way to free time for personal relationships
- Many with executive function challenges describe AI as especially helpful for focus and organization
- Ukraine users discussed AI as emotional support throughout the war
- Quote: "Relaxing while my AI gets the work done, builds the wealth. It's a shadow of me, just a very, very long one." — Entrepreneur, Honduras


## 中文

# 8.1 万人想要 AI 做什么

**来源：** Anthropic Features
**链接：** https://www.anthropic.com/features/81k-interviews
**日期：** 2026年5月（研究于2025年12月进行）

## 规模

80,508 名参与者，覆盖 159 个国家和地区、70 种语言 — 史上规模最大、多语言程度最高的定性研究。

## 人们想要什么（9 大类）

1. **职业卓越**（18.8%）— AI 处理日常事务，让人类专注于战略性工作
2. **个人蜕变**（13.7%）— AI 作为成长、健康、行为改变的向导
3. **生活管理**（13.5%）— AI 作为认知脚手架，处理日程和减轻心理负担
4. **时间自由**（11.1%）— 夺回时间陪伴家人、发展爱好、休息
5. **经济独立**（9.7%）— 通过 AI 实现收入增长、创业、投资
6. **社会变革**（9.4%）— 用 AI 解决贫困、疾病、气候问题
7. **创业**（8.7%）— 用 AI 作为力量倍增器来建立和扩展业务
8. **学习与成长**（8.4%）— AI 作为个性化教师和学习加速器
9. **创意表达**（5.6%）— 克服想象力与执行之间的障碍

## AI 已经做到了什么

1. **生产力**（32%）— 大幅加速工作、自动化重复任务
2. **认知伙伴**（17.2%）— AI 作为思维伙伴和创意协作伙伴
3. **学习**（9.9%）— 自适应解释、耐心辅导、按需专业知识
4. **技术可及性**（8.7%）— 非开发者上架应用，个人创作者完成团队规模的工作
5. **研究综合**（7.2%）— 文献综述、提炼来源、理清复杂材料
6. **情感支持**（6.1%）— 无评判空间的对话、处理困难情境

## 关键洞察

- 81% 的人表示 AI 已经朝着他们描述的愿景迈出了一步
- 11% 将生产力收益视为最终能腾出时间用于人际关系的手段
- 有执行功能障碍的人特别描述 AI 对专注和组织方面的帮助
- 乌克兰用户讨论了战争中 AI 作为情感支持的作用
- 引言："让 AI 完成工作、积累财富，我在放松。这是我的影子，只是一个非常非常长的影子。" — 洪都拉斯创业者
