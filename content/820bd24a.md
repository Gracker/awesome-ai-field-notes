## 中文导读

Mistral 发布 Large 4 公开预览：非官方代号 ML4，正式名 le Chonk。1T 总参数、49B 激活的原生多模态模型，Mistral 史上最大最强，仍在快速迭代。宣称编码、agentic 工作流、多模态理解上与全球最强开源模型竞争，显著超过美欧开发的任何 open-weight 模型；网络安全、金融、法律等关键企业负载上是开源模型里的 SOTA；视觉定位等部分领域甚至超过前沿闭源模型。权重月底放出；放出前与网络安全厂商、审核过的伙伴和政府一起做真实环境红队——他们拿到的是同一个模型，但审核更低、网络能力更强。训练在 Mistral 欧洲自建数据中心的 3,800 块 NVIDIA Grace Blackwell GPU 上从零完成，预览 API 也跑在同一套设施上；将提供 Mistral 端到端运营、独立于其他数字服务商并受欧洲法律管辖的欧洲部署；训练数据大量多语言，覆盖 160+ 语言，包括欧盟全部官方语言；训练/定制/RL 环境与 Mistral Forge 客户所用相同。基准图覆盖 DeepSWE、Terminal Bench 4.0、Artificial Analysis Cyber Index、agentic 行为、Finance Agent、Harvey Legal Agent 与视觉定位。

## English Summary

Mistral Large 4 enters public preview: a 1T-parameter, 49B-active natively multimodal model, Mistral's largest yet. Claimed competitive with the strongest open-source models globally on coding, agentic workflows, and multimodal understanding, significantly ahead of any US/European open-weight model, open SOTA on enterprise workloads (cybersecurity, finance, law), and beyond frontier closed models in visual grounding. Weights drop end of month; until then, red-teaming runs in real-world settings with cybersecurity leaders, vetted partners, and state authorities, who access the same model with reduced moderation and expanded cyber capabilities. Trained from scratch on 3,800 NVIDIA Grace Blackwell GPUs in Mistral's own European datacenters; a Mistral-operated European deployment under European law is planned; training data spanned 160+ languages.
