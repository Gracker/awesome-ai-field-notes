## English Source Notes

OpenAI's recent release of Astra 6.2 and Sol 6.2 was widely panned. Based on the author's battle-system data for real work tasks, both models appear tuned for cost savings: per-task output tokens and reasoning tokens dropped significantly (output drop p=0.0055 Sol, p=0.022 Astra; reasoning drop p=0.00013 Sol, p=0.0064 Astra). Plotted against the same opponents, Astra 6 and Sol 6.1 look like normal models, but the 6.2 pair becomes an underthinking outlier. The author's conclusion: Astra and Sol appear to be cost tuned compared to their predecessors, to a degree that makes them underthinking outliers.
