Alibaba shipped Qwen4-Max on November 16 with hosted-only API access on Alibaba Cloud - 2.4T parameters, 1M-token context, vision support, priced at $1.5/$4.5 per million input/output tokens (below Qwen3.8-Max; cache $0.075/M, about 70% cheaper). Benchmarks are all self-reported: coding claimed above Opus 5.5 and GPT-6.1-Sol, WebDev Arena 1674 (+35), per-task cost cut from $4.43 to $1.67 (-62%). The hard facts are the price curve and context length; the rankings live in Alibaba's own harness and need same-task reproduction, including failure rates, before any switch decision. Note: the qwen.ai page is a JS shell; facts were cross-checked across multiple secondary sources.
