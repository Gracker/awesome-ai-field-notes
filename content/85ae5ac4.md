## 中文摘要

论文聚焦 LLM 在部署时仍然会输出不安全内容的现实，论证仅靠离线对齐训练不够，需要在线监测与告警作者给出一个极简方案：把外部 verifier 的信号作为监控输入，用一个由 risk control 校准出的阈值直接决定是否告警，避免复杂的多步序贯检验在数学推理与 red-teaming 数据集上的实验显示，这种阈值告警方案在性能上能与基于序贯假设检验的更复杂监控器相当，对实时部署更友好

## English Abstract

Addresses the gap that alignment training alone cannot prevent unsafe LLM outputs at deployment, and argues for real-time online monitoring. The authors propose a minimal monitor that turns an external verifier's signal into an alarm decision by thresholding, where the threshold is calibrated via risk control. Experiments on mathematical reasoning and red-teaming datasets show this simple thresholded design is competitive with more advanced monitors based on sequential hypothesis testing, while being cheaper to run in real-time deployments.
