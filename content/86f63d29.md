## English Summary

Thibault Sottiaux, OpenAI Codex engineering lead, reported on X that GPT-6.2 launched with a billing-accounting error that underestimated Astra and Sol usage; now fixed, the same workload exhausts plan quota about 40% (Astra) or 25% (Sol) sooner than last week, though both remain more durable than 6 Astra / 6.1 Sol. Other models unaffected, no retroactive billing; Go/Plus/Pro/Business each receive one compensating banked reset. Quota budgets sized at old burn rates need re-basing (multiply remaining headroom by ~1.4x/1.25x before re-routing). In the same window OpenAI's status page listed Codex PR review/merge errors, downstream of GitHub's 2026-11-25 critical incident.
