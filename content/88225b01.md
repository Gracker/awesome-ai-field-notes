## 中文导读

开发者常在训练时扣留数据，阻止模型学到可能被滥用的能力，但扣留能延迟多久、事后补回要多少数据，此前没有答案。作者在棋类、Othello、分子串、括号语言和网页文本上扣留单项技能的训练信号做对照，发现模型在其余数据上的通用进步可以替代被扣留的数据：从未被显式训练过应对将军的棋模型，对合法应对的排序几乎不输训练过的模型；大模型里被扣留技能的延迟时长可以从小模型预测；重新引入被扣留数据后，找回技能只需从零训练所需数据量的一小部分，且模型越进步需要的越少。同等难度的控制规则学得慢得多，说明替代来自通用进步而非难度。一个被扣留编程语言的 70 亿参数模型也以同样方式找回该语言。结论：数据过滤只是延迟能力，而非阻止能力。

## 为什么值得关注

数据过滤是各实验室防止滥用能力的默认做法，这篇把这个做法的实际效果第一次量化了：扣留只是延迟，且延迟时长可由小模型外推，补课成本随模型通用进步持续下降。对安全策略来说，"扣数据"不是一劳永逸的闸门，而是需要按时间尺度重新评估的延迟手段；对训练效率来说，它暗示模型已有的通用能力本身就是一种可复用的资产。

## English Abstract

Language models acquire their capabilities from training data, and developers withhold data during training to keep models from learning capabilities that could be misused. How long this delays a capability, and how much data is needed to acquire it later, is unknown. Here we show that the general progress a model makes on the rest of its training data substitutes for the data that was withheld. We withheld the training signal for individual skills in models of chess, Othello, molecular strings, bracket languages and web text. The delay of a withheld skill in larger models was predicted from smaller ones. A chess model never explicitly trained to respond to check ranked the legal responses almost as well as a model that was. When models were then trained on the withheld data, they acquired the skill with a small fraction of the data needed from initialization, and this fraction decreased as the models progressed. A control rule of equal difficulty was learned much more slowly. A programming language withheld from the training text of a 7-billion-parameter model was regained in the same way. Data filtering therefore delays a capability, and the data required to regain it decreases as models improve.

## English Summary

Developers withhold training data to keep models from acquiring dangerous capabilities, but how long this delays a capability and how much data is needed to regain it was unknown. Training models on chess, Othello, and web text while withholding the signal for individual skills, the authors show that general progress on the rest of the data substitutes for the withheld data: a chess model never explicitly trained to respond to check ranked legal responses nearly as well as a trained one; the delay of a withheld skill in larger models is predictable from smaller ones; and when withheld data is reintroduced, the skill is regained with a small fraction of the data needed from initialization, a fraction that shrinks as models progress. A programming language withheld from a 7B model's training text was regained the same way. Data filtering delays capabilities rather than preventing them.
