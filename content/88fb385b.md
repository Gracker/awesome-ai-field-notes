# 中文原文

## Karpathy 力荐：用 LLM 打造个人知识库，科研工作流新范式

这条推文非常火爆！Karpathy 分享了他所谓 **"想法文件"（idea file）** 的改进版。在这个 LLM Agent 时代，共享具体代码/应用的意义下降了——你只需分享想法，然后让别人的 Agent 根据自身需求定制。他把这个想法称为 **LLM Wiki**。

### 核心思路

传统 RAG 的问题：每次提问，LLM 都从零开始「重新发现」知识。没有积累，没有整合。

LLM Wiki 的不同之处：LLM 不是只在查询时从原始文档检索，而是 **递增地构建和维护一个持久的 Wiki**——一个结构化的、互相链接的 Markdown 文件集合，夹在你和原始资料之间。

当你添加新资料时，LLM 会阅读它、提取关键信息，将其整合进现有 Wiki——更新实体页面、修订主题摘要、标注新数据与旧主张的矛盾点、强化或挑战不断演进的综合知识。知识被编译一次，然后 **保持最新**，而不是每次查询都重新推导。

**关键差异：Wiki 是一个持久的、不断累积的产物。** 跨引用已经存在，矛盾已经被标记，综合已经反映了你所阅读的一切。Wiki 随你添加的每个来源和你提出的每个问题而变得更加丰富。

你几乎不需要自己写 Wiki——LLM 来撰写和维护所有内容。你负责资料来源、探索和提出正确的问题。LLM 做所有苦活——总结、交叉引用、归档和记账，让知识库真正长期有用。

### 典型应用场景

- **个人**：追踪自己的目标、健康、心理、自我提升——归档日记条目、文章、播客笔记，随着时间建立关于自己的结构化图景。
- **研究**：在数周或数月内深入一个主题——阅读论文、文章、报告，逐步建立一个包含演进论点的综合 Wiki。
- **读书**：边读边归档每章，为角色、主题、情节线索及其关联方式构建页面。读完时你拥有一本丰富的伴侣 Wiki。

# English Original

## Karpathy's Endorsement: Building Personal Knowledge Bases with LLMs, A New Paradigm for Research Workflows

This tweet went very viral! Karpathy shared an improved version of what he calls an **"idea file"**. In this era of LLM agents, there's less point/need of sharing the specific code/app — you just share the idea, then the other person's agent customizes & builds it for your specific needs. He calls this concept **LLM Wiki**.

### Core Idea

The problem with traditional RAG: every question, the LLM has to "rediscover" knowledge from scratch. No accumulation, no integration.

The difference of LLM Wiki: instead of just retrieving from raw documents at query time, the LLM **incrementally builds and maintains a persistent wiki** — a structured, interlinked collection of markdown files that sits between you and the raw sources.

When you add a new source, the LLM doesn't just index it for later retrieval. It reads it, extracts the key information, and integrates it into the existing wiki — updating entity pages, revising topic summaries, noting where new data contradicts old claims, strengthening or challenging the evolving synthesis.

**Key difference: the wiki is a persistent, compounding artifact.** The cross-references are already there. The contradictions have already been flagged. The synthesis already reflects everything you've read. The wiki keeps getting richer with every source you add and every question you ask.

You never (or rarely) write the wiki yourself — the LLM writes and maintains all of it. You're in charge of sourcing, exploration, and asking the right questions. The LLM does all the grunt work — summarizing, cross-referencing, filing, and bookkeeping.

### Typical Use Cases

- **Personal**: tracking your own goals, health, psychology, self-improvement — filing journal entries, articles, podcast notes, and building up a structured picture of yourself over time.
- **Research**: going deep on a topic over weeks or months — reading papers, articles, reports, and incrementally building a comprehensive wiki with an evolving thesis.
- **Reading a book**: filing each chapter as you go, building out pages for characters, themes, plot threads, and how they connect. By the end you have a rich companion wiki.
