## English Original

**MiniMax M2.7 Release: SWE-Bench 56.22% / Terminal Bench 57.0%, Weights Live on Hugging Face**

MiniMax has released the M2.7 model with weights now open-sourced on Hugging Face, achieving 56.22% on SWE-Bench (software engineering evaluation) and 57.0% on Terminal Bench — top-tier performance for its parameter class. However, note that the model uses a special license that prohibits unauthorized commercial use and does not meet OSI open-source definition. For research purposes it can be used directly; commercial deployment requires licensing authorization.
