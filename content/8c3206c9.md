## English Summary

Stitches AI-overview hallucinations, link rot, Reddit-driven AI poisoning that contaminates Wikipedia sources, FiveThirtyEight being deleted wholesale by Disney, and news outlets blocking scrapers after the Internet Archive publisher lawsuit into a single causal chain describing a collapsing public-memory infrastructure. Examples include AI overview fabricating an already-passed sunset time for a Colorado user, 404 Media reporting firms seeding Reddit to game AI answers, Wikipedia traffic being scraped directly so donations dry up, Internet Archive losing the digital-lending case and then being blocked by news sites to prevent AI training, Disney deleting FiveThirtyEight entirely, and a German court holding Google editorially liable for AI-overview-attributed scams....
