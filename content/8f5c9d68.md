A couple hours ago Jensen Huang declared that the race to AGI is over.

[

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!vbW1!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F608d4701-6c7b-40ee-9842-aecc96614901_1226x584.png)

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!vbW1!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F608d4701-6c7b-40ee-9842-aecc96614901_1226x584.png)

Unfortunately, Huang gave no evidence and no definitions, which feels to me like an effort at a takeover of a scientific question by corporate fiat.

I would urge him to read [agidefinition.AI](https://www.agidefinition.ai/) by Hendrycks, Yoshua Bengio & many others (including myself), and to consider how Astra is doing on the kinds of examples I laid out in my 10 -item [bet with Brundage](https://garymarcus.substack.com/p/where-will-ai-be-at-the-end-of-2027?r=8tdk6&utm_medium=ios). Autoformalization may finally be in reach, and maybe (?) reliable coding; I doubt that Astra will have hit any of the other eight.

[

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!8mW4!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe28f4029-948d-4978-9774-9fb94129c8f9_1318x1587.png)

](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!8mW4!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe28f4029-948d-4978-9774-9fb94129c8f9_1318x1587.png)

By conventional definitions, Astra still falls short.

Declaring victory without a definition simply muddies the waters.
