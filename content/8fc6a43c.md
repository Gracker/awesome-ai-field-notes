## English Abstract

Gary Marcus's September 19 post threads two stories together. A same-day NYT piece reports Trump downplaying AI risk and resisting regulation for economic reasons; the same day CNN follows up with a US military AI-assisted intel report that flagged a Chinese vessel supposedly carrying nuclear-weapons components, prompting an actual intercept turned out to be a hallucination, the ship was a regular commercial vessel. Marcus warned the Senate back in 2023 that AI hallucinations could mis-trigger war; this is nearly his prediction come true. He writes 'we won't get that lucky next time.' Short but the article pairs White House political motive with a verifiable near-fire incident, which beats abstract AI-risk discourse.
