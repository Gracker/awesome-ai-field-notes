## English Summary

Google launches Gemini 4 Hydrogen (Nov 12, 2026), the fast everyday tier of the Gemini 4 family: while frontier Gemini 4 Carbon and Argon handle deep, long-horizon reasoning, Hydrogen is built for rapid execution across diverse agentic workflows and everyday coding. It surpasses Argon on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at its default settings, and ships to all tiers of Google AI Studio, the Gemini API, Gemini for business, the Gemini app, Google Search, and Google Antigravity for paid AI Pro/Ultra subscribers. The announcement highlights efficiencies for multi-model workloads (routing work between Carbon/Argon/Hydrogen), adaptable speed-versus-intelligence across thinking levels, and safe execution at scale; HN discussion reports it rolling out immediately as the Gemini app replacement for 3.8 Flash at roughly 2.5x its speed.
