## English Abstract / Summary

lcamtuf documents a negative-result TSCM experiment on the long-running "fluorescent lamps can be used for passive eavesdropping" folklore. He heats a 9-inch tube with DC current to avoid AC flicker, drives a 200W speaker from a signal generator, and captures high-speed 1/8000s exposures, finding no detectable modulation. He walks through the physics that disfavors the effect: tube pressure is ~1/200 atm, UV must traverse a phosphor layer with non-trivial afterglow. The piece models how negative results in security research should be reported.
