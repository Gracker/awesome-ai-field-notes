## 中文导读

Brian Potter 把技术进步重述为搜索过程：新技术是设计空间里的一个点，272 个命名方程的组合（2^272）已超宇宙原子数。他用 PTOP 模型把对象、属性、现象串成层级：最简模型约 100 亿组合，随机生成整份设计平均试 10 亿次，模块化搜索——每步只加一个物体并锁死已验证子设计——平均 1300 次即可，差近六个数量级。模块度比空间大小更要命：100 级现象的 10^200 空间靠 100 个可锁定台阶一万次就够；level 2 复现有性繁殖约 100 代摸到最高层级、无性 2000 代不行；level 3 出现性能取舍。结论：研究搜索过程本身。

## English Summary

Brian Potter reframes technological progress as search: a technology is a point in a design space so large that choices over Wikipedia's 272 named equations alone (2^272) exceed the observable universe's atom count. His PTOP model chains objects, properties and phenomena into levels (one phenomenon's triggered properties are prerequisites for the next). In the simplest model (~10 billion combinations), generating whole designs at random needs ~1 billion tries, but modular search - adding one object per step while freezing validated sub-designs - succeeds in ~1,300, nearly six orders of magnitude less. Modularity matters more than space size; sexual recombination reaches the top level in ~100 generations where asexual fails at 2,000; performance tuning reintroduces design trade-offs. Study the search process itself.
