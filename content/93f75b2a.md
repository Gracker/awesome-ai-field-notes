## English Summary

Gruber's commentary on Zuckerberg's 6,500-word AI manifesto highlights the otherwise-easily-missed detail: Meta's Richland Parish, Mississippi data center pays public-school teachers $50K bonuses. The piece reads Zuckerberg's essay as standard 'personal empowerment vs centralization' PR accompanying Meta's Workforce Academy, and frames the Mississippi teacher salaries as the only 2026 data-center-vs-community-relations card that could turn public opinion around - while noting it hasn't yet.
