## English Summary

Dwarkesh Patel and Max Farrens respond to objections to his 'superintelligence by 2030' thesis: median 2028 for automating top AI researchers, superintelligence the year after; he gives ~25% for next year and concedes about half his examples were hasty intuition pumps. Empirical line one: research with Jerry Han shows 2019-2026 data corpora explain pretraining progress better than architectures, and data gains come mostly from filtering and synthetic-data methods needing ML researchers, not domain experts; AI has solved over 10% of Erdos's thousand problems within six months to a year of frontier math ability. Line two is compute: supply grows 2.6x per year while labs need quadrupling to sustain pace; the experiment bottleneck compresses years of progress into one. By end of next year he expects 'competent colleagues who never sleep', with internal research models far ahead of external.
