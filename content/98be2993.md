## 中文导读

HN 讨论串 49548452 摘出 Antigravity 现网 ToS 关键句：'Using third party software, tools, or services to access the Service (e.g. using OpenClaw with Antigravity OAuth) is a breach of this Agreement.' 后跟 'grounds for suspension or termination of your Antigravity and/or Gemini CLI accounts'。这意味着第三方 harness 通过 Antigravity OAuth 访问仍属明确禁止，处罚对象为 Antigravity 与/或 Gemini CLI 账号。研究包另提供官方 headless 路径：`https://antigravity.google/docs/cli/headless/` 与 `https://antigravity.google/docs/ide/extensions/`，仍以 `-p` / stream-json / stdin 等官方脚本形态打开。研究包明确：整号 Gmail 必然封的解读超出 ToS 文本，community-precedent 中的误判叙事被标记为 anecdote。

## 为什么值得关注

条款文本把违规主体和处罚主体都写在同一句里，对工程团队意味着两件事同时成立：(1) 通过 Antigravity OAuth 的三方 harness 调用仍是违规，封禁范围限定在 Antigravity 与 Gemini CLI 账号，整号 Gmail 必然被吊销的解读超出文本；(2) 官方 headless 路径仍然可用，CI 与批处理可以走 `-p`、stream-json、stdin。研究包也明确自写封装是否构成 third party 条款无判定标准。

## English Summary

HN thread 49548452 (325 points, author tosh, 2026-09-03) extracts the current Antigravity ToS clause: third-party software, tools, or services to access the Service (e.g. OpenClaw with Antigravity OAuth) is a breach of the Agreement, and may be grounds for suspension or termination of the Antigravity and/or Gemini CLI accounts. The sanction is scoped to Antigravity and Gemini CLI accounts, not the underlying Google account. The research pack separately documents that the official headless path on `https://antigravity.google/docs/cli/headless/` and `https://antigravity.google/docs/ide/extensions/` remains open via `-p`, stream-json and stdin. It also notes the 'whole Google account must be banned' reading exceeds the ToS text and treats the notatoad community-precedent narrative as anecdote.
