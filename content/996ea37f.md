## 中文导读

Turan（2606.08919，2026-06-08，cs.AI/cs.CR/cs.LG）把 agent 审批门建模为资源分配问题：125 个对抗加权的人工标注动作上，评审对「危不危险」仅中等一致（Fleiss κ=0.52），不存在客观 ground truth；把 guard 形式化为非对称代价下的选择性分类后，硬输入上无法安全自动决策。将评审人建为会疲劳的内生变量后，实际安全性对升级率呈倒 U 形——人审拉满反而更不安全，最优策略停在中档升级；负载感知策略还能抵御向疲劳评审人塞恶意动作的 flooding 攻击。开源实现 headroom 把 guard 质量从猜测变成一条曲线。

## English Abstract（节选）

> We argue the gate is the easy part; the hard part is the judgment - which actions to stop - which the field evaluates against two false assumptions: that there is a ground-truth notion of 'risky,' and that the human reviewer is a perfect, infinitely-available oracle. ... realized safety becomes an inverted-U in the escalation rate: more human oversight can make a system less safe, and the safety-optimal guard escalates below full escalation.

## English Summary

Turan (arXiv 2606.08919, Jun 8, 2026, cs.AI/cs.CR/cs.LG) models the human-approval gate for LLM agents as a resource-allocation problem, not just classification. On 125 hand-labeled adversarially-weighted agent actions, reviewers agree only moderately on what is risky (Fleiss kappa = 0.52), so no ground-truth label exists; framing the guard as selective classification under asymmetric cost makes its operating limits measurable, and on hard inputs it cannot safely auto-decide. Modeling the reviewer as endogenous and fatiguing makes realized safety an inverted-U in escalation rate: full human escalation can be less safe than partial, and a load-aware policy resists a flooding attack that slips a malicious action past a tired reviewer. Open-source implementation (headroom) turns 'is my guard good?' into a curve.
