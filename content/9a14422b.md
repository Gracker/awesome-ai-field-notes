## 中文摘要

Tejas Kumar 基于 Aleph Alpha 189 页技术报告写的 Kolibri 开源 MoE 深度解读。Kolibri 78B 总参、每 token 激活约 3.5B，80% 训练 token 为德语；UniBPE 训出的 128k tokenizer 针对德语复合词，作者实测在德国《基本法》上比 GPT-5 的 o200k_base 省 15%（官方数据 11.2%）；40/50 层用 512 窗口的 sliding-window attention 且仅这些层带 RoPE，262k 训练上下文实测外推到 1M，RULER 得 63.2；用 Merlin-Arthur 三角色博弈训练弃答，Omniscience 测试上答「不知道」占 44%。短板同样直白：Terminal-Bench 27.7、SWE-bench Verified 66.4，78GB FP8 权重与德英双语定位。10 月 3 日发布，Apache 2.0。

## English Summary

A deep technical walkthrough of Kolibri, Aleph Alpha's open-weight 78B-parameter MoE (about 3.5B active per token) for German and English, released under Apache 2.0 on Oct 3, 2026. Drawing on the 189-page technical report plus the author's own tokenizer experiment: the 128k-token UniBPE vocabulary needs 15% fewer tokens than GPT-5's tokenizer on legal German (official figure 11.2%); 40 of 50 layers use 512-token sliding-window attention with RoPE only on those layers, so the 262k training context extrapolates to 1M tokens (RULER 63.2); abstention is trained via the three-role Merlin-Arthur protocol, answering 'I don't know' 44% of the time on the Omniscience test. Weaknesses are stated plainly: Terminal-Bench 27.7, SWE-bench Verified 66.4, 78GB of FP8 weights, German/English only.
