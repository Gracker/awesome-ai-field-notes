## 摘要 (Summary)

仓库运营受标准作业流程（SOP）约束，SOP 编码跨系统的复杂决策逻辑，需在严格时延下可靠执行现有 LLM 智能体缺乏强制 SOP 合规的机制，在加载完整 SOP 时又因上下文过载而性能下降论文提出生产环境部署的 Eluna 系统，它把 SOP 编码为有向无环图（DAG），配合渐进披露，并把独立任务分派给可并行执行的子智能体，每个子智能体拥有持久化代码执行与实时数据访问为兼顾生产时延与准确率，Eluna 采用非对称情景蒸馏：强教师模型通过情景错误记忆改进自身，蒸馏出一个能在毫秒级响应的小学生模型，已落地于真实仓库运营并报告了显著的可靠性与效率收益

## English Abstract / Excerpt

Warehouse operations are governed by Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) that encode complex, multi-system decision logic, which must be executed reliably under strict time constraints. LLM agents lack mechanisms to enforce procedural compliance and degrade under the context overload that full SOP specifications introduce. We present Eluna, a production-deployed agentic system for reliable SOP execution. Eluna is a graph-guided, multi-agent framework that encodes SOPs as directed acyclic graphs with progressive disclosure and delegates independent tasks to parallel sub-agents, each with persistent code execution and live data access. To meet production latency and accuracy needs, we use asymmetric episodic distillation where a strong teacher is improved through episodic error memories, distilling a small student model that serves sub-second decisions....
