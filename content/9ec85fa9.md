## English Summary

B3 (Blurring the Block Boundaries) is a training-free, hyperparameter-free acceleration method for block-diffusion language models with self-editing: instead of waiting for the slowest token in the current block before starting the next, two blocks are refined in parallel within a shared forward pass, and B3 calibrates itself on the fly, opening the next block only once the model is ready to generate it. Evaluated on state-of-the-art DLMs across 10 diverse datasets spanning varied tasks and difficulty levels, B3 keeps generation quality on par with the base model while delivering 11.5-14.5% speedup, pushing the Pareto frontier of the latency-quality trade-off in DLM inference.
