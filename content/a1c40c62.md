## 中文摘要

Google 11-19 官宣 Gemini Mac 应用的 computer use 在美英推出：Gemini 在隔离后台窗口里操作 Mac 应用与网页，点击、打字、菜单、表单等 multi-step 任务自动完成，带实时预览。门槛很硬：AI Plus/Pro/Ultra 订阅、Apple Silicon、macOS Sequoia 15.0+、美区英语、个人账号 18+。桌面 computer use 从 demo 走进付费订阅产品门，与同日 Tool Retrieval 预览并看，Google 在 agent 产品面是桌面操作与大工具库 token 结构两条线并进。

## English Summary

Google shipped computer use for the Gemini Mac app in the US and UK on Nov 19: Gemini can navigate Mac apps and websites in an isolated background window to finish complex multi-step tasks - clicks, typing, menus, forms - with live preview. The eligibility gates are explicit: Google AI Plus/Pro/Ultra subscription, Apple Silicon, macOS Sequoia 15.0+, US English, personal accounts 18+. The launch post frames it as automating repetitive chores on the Mac. Together with the same-day Tool Retrieval preview, Google is pushing desktop computer use as a paid-subscriber product while restructuring how large tool libraries enter the prompt.
