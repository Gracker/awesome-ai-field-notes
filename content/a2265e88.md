# WeatherNext Helps NHC Predict Hurricane Melissa Cat-5 Landfall 5 Days Out

> Source: https://deepmind.google/blog/how-weathernext-helped-the-national-hurricane-center-better-predict-hurricane-melissas-historic-landfall-in-jamaica
> Author: WeatherNext team (Google DeepMind + Google Research)
> Published: 2026-05-19

## Summary

When Hurricane Melissa was first identified as a weak tropical depression, traditional numerical models wavered on whether it would strike Haiti as a weak system or intensify toward Jamaica. **WeatherNext**, an AI weather model developed by Google DeepMind and Google Research, predicted a Category 5 landfall in Jamaica **five days in advance** with 80% confidence — rising to near-100% confidence three days before impact.

This was the first time the National Hurricane Center (NHC) successfully forecast a storm to reach Category 5 intensity starting from Category 1 wind speed. The NHC combined WeatherNext output with traditional models (HAFS), satellite, and hurricane-hunter data to give the Meteorological Service Jamaica unprecedented lead time, supporting evacuations and resource staging.

For the following hurricane season the model runs 1,000 ensemble futures every six hours (up from 50). NHC senior hurricane specialist Philippe Papin called the increase “more stable and consistent guidance.”

## Background

- **Training:** Decades of global weather patterns plus specialised extreme-tropical-cyclone datasets, under Google's Earth AI initiative.
- **Why it works:** Predicts both *track* and *intensity* in one model, where traditionally agencies maintained two separate model pipelines — coarse global models for track, high-resolution regional models for intensity.
- **Operational baseline:** One day of additional useful lead time per decade of operational development; WeatherNext reportedly delivers that decade of progress in a single model.

## 中文概要

Google DeepMind 的 WeatherNext 模型在 Hurricane Melissa 近以后达到 Category 5 在 Jamaica 登陆二者预测上交出了史无前例的成绩。传统数值模型初期在是否转向希特作为弱系统还是向 Jamaica 增强上云完全不确定，WeatherNext 在风暴仍为弱热带气旋时就预测 5 天后以 Category 5 登陆 Jamaica，初期置信度 80%，接近登陆前提升至接近 100%。这是首次从 Category 1 起点预测出 Category 5 强度。NHC 使用该预测叠加 HAFS 、卫星与风探驾驶资料，为 Jamaica 气象局交出空前的预警领窗，支持撤离与应急调度。项目为后续风暴季将执行频率提升至每 6 小时 1000 个集合预报。
