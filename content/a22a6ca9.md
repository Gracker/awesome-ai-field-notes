## 中文导读

Anthropic 宣布扩展 Cyber Verification Program（CVP）：面向合格安全专业人员提供高级 cyber 能力与放宽的拦截分类器，现在分三档准入，安全团队按工作性质申请。Defense Access 面向防御工作（SOC、应急响应）；Red Team Access 在防御用途之上叠加授权渗透测试与红队（面向内部红队、政府红队、安全渗透公司）；每档都包含 Opus 5.5、Sonnet 5.5、Mythos 5.1 及后续新模型。能力边界直接用 CyScenarioBench 数据摆出：无 CVP 的 GA 模型 50 个任务在首个 prompt 就全被拦；Defense Access 档 46/50 在挑战中某一步被拦、4 个任务全程未被拦；Red Team Access 档进一步放开。这是 Project Glasswing 以来的路线延续：把『要不要放开前沿 cyber 能力』的二元问题，改造成『谁来、用什么身份、拿到哪一档』的产品化分层。

## English Abstract

We're launching a new, expanded version of our Cyber Verification Program (CVP), which makes advanced cyber capabilities and reduced blocking classifiers available to qualifying security professionals. The program now consists of three access tiers, which allow security teams to apply for the level of access that best suits their work. Each tier includes access to our most capable models, including Claude Opus 5.5, Claude Sonnet 5.5, Claude Mythos 5.1, and new models moving forward. On CyScenarioBench, our safeguards blocked 46 of 50 tasks on Claude Opus 5.5 in the Defense Access tier.

## English Summary

Anthropic's expanded CVP formalizes tiered access to frontier cyber capabilities: defense and red-team tiers with reduced classifiers, benchmarked on CyScenarioBench where GA models block everything and Defense Access blocks 46/50 tasks.
