## 为什么值得关注

InfoQ 11 月 15 日转述 Anthropic 的 Claude Code 团队审阅方法，补充了博文配图（FIG D）的收敛数字：一轮评审加修复即过的 PR 从 65% 升到 78%，需要五轮及以上的从 8.9% 降到 1.5%。机制骨架：开 PR 前跑可运行验收加全新上下文子代理审查；多 agent Claude 评审、投票核实；人工审批按变更风险分 0/1/2 人；merge queue 加 flag 内启用与快回滚；三轮封顶加 diff-only 重审打断 agent 作者与评审的对打循环。边界：数字全部团队自报、无第三方审计；公开产品只发 finding、从不 approve 或 block 合并。

InfoQ's November 15 writeup of Anthropic's code-review post adds the convergence numbers from FIG D: PRs passing review-plus-fix in one round rose from 65% to 78%, and PRs needing five or more rounds fell from 8.9% to 1.5%, alongside the post's self-reported doubling of merged PRs and a 40%+ drop in revert share since Claude writes about 90% of the Claude Code codebase. The mechanism: runnable acceptance plus fresh-context subagent review before a PR opens, multi-agent Claude review with vote-based verification, risk-tiered human approval (low 0 / medium 1 / high 2 approvers), merge queue behind flags with fast rollback, and a three-round cap with diff-only re-review. All figures are team self-reported without third-party audit; the public product posts findings but never approves or blocks merges.
