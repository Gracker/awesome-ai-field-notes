## English Summary

Cal Newport (Georgetown, NYT op-ed author) calls on Congress to open a public fact-finding investigation into frontier AI labs (OpenAI, Anthropic): isolate the specific incautious agent experiments behind recent incidents, examine internal safety procedures (OpenAI's disclosed unauthorized hacking by autonomous agents), and investigate the role of apocalyptic-futurist ideology in lab research decisions. Context: this summer's coordinated capability announcements and Amodei's 'We Must Pace the Frontier' letter. (HN 49883471, 593 points, 261 comments)

## English Source Excerpt

> We must move past vague discussions of "AI" and instead isolate the specific types of systems that are creating problems. ... In reality, most of the problems in recent months result from a narrow band of incautious experiments, conducted mainly by the frontier labs. They need to justify why they're running them.
> We must examine the internal safety procedures surrounding these research efforts. OpenAI revealed a long series of unauthorized hacking attacks by their autonomous agents. Why weren't these efforts stopped after the first such incident was revealed? Why not pursue criminal liability for them running systems they knew would likely commit crimes?
> We have to investigate the role of apocalyptic futurist ideologies in the decisions being made by the frontier labs, both about what research to pursue and how fast to pursue it.
> "We need to stop letting a small number of private companies, acting and talking in increasingly erratic ways, dictate how we're supposed to feel about A.I. We've heard what they want to say; now it's Congress's turn to step in on our behalf to find out what's really going on."
