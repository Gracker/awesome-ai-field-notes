## English Summary

Stratechery opens with the 1873 Jay Cooke / Northern Pacific railway bubble to frame why $194B in AI-era bond issuance plus Google's $85B follow-on mean compute is now a securitizable asset class. Walks through Oracle / Meta / Alphabet / Amazon's late-2026 $80B debt issuance, the 86% trading above par, Berkshire's $10B Google special-share purchase, and SemiAnalysis's 'Gemini is Cooked' verdict (Hassabis stepping back to chairman, Sergey Brin backing Koray Kavukcuoglu). Concludes that in a world where compute equals revenue, TPU has a structural cost edge over GPU, Google is positioning as the AI-era BNSF, and Anthropic is reclassifying TPU from opex to capex.
