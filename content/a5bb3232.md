## 中文导读

HybridLink 把最后一层 hidden state 映回 input embedding 空间进入潜空间推理模式，需要调工具或对外输出时切回文本模式，切换点与每段长度由模型自己学出而非配置预设；潜模式不解码但可读（每个 latent step 映回最近 token embedding 可见语义）。训练分两段：HybridSFT 先让 backbone 学会与 HybridLink 协调，HybridRL 再学何时潜行、何时文本行动，奖励里没有针对模式使用的显式项。Qwen3-Base 4B/8B/14B 在 11 个基准（数学、科学、医学、检索、agentic reasoning，含 GAIA、WebWalker、HLE、SealQA、DSQA）平均比前沿 agent 与各类 agentic 框架高 10.4%，token 用量降 35.5-54.7%，推理快 1.8-2.6 倍、成本降 2.7-3.7 倍；5 次独立运行准确率标准差 ±0.014。成本按 $2.50/H100-hour、BF16 峰值 10% 利用率估算，单题解码算力从 51.2-66.9 TFLOP 降到 13.9-25.1 TFLOP。边界：成本估算排除 prefill、上下文相关 attention 与工具执行；实验只做到 14B。代码与数据在 togethercomputer/HybridAgent。

## English Summary

Hybrid Reasoning Agents let an agent think partly in latent space and act in text: HybridLink maps the last-layer hidden state back into input-embedding space for latent mode, switching back to text for tool calls or user-facing output, with switch points learned rather than configured. Training is two-stage (HybridSFT then HybridRL, no explicit mode-usage reward). On Qwen3-Base 4B/8B/14B across 11 benchmarks (incl. GAIA, WebWalker, HLE), it averages +10.4% over frontier agents and agentic frameworks while cutting tokens 35.5-54.7%, speeding inference 1.8-2.6x and cutting cost 2.7-3.7x. Boundaries stated: cost model excludes prefill and tool execution; experiments stop at 14B. Code/data: togethercomputer/HybridAgent.
