A one-page community etiquette site (spiritual cousin of nohello.net and dontasktoask.com) that hit HN front page with 985 points and 535 comments. Core argument: when someone asks you an honest question and you dump it into a chatbot then paste the answer back, you are being a middleman; the asker has the same tools and wanted your context, taste, and judgment. Do-this-instead list: use AI for drafting but read what it gave you and write your own version; extract only the part that answers the question; quote genuinely useful bits and explain why; or say 'no strong opinion here'. The site offers a copyable link and a spicier 'burn bridges' variant for DMs, Slack, and code review.
