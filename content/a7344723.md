# TailSteer: Trajectory-Tail Steering for Drift-Resistant Multi-turn Image Editing

- **ID**: a7344723
- **来源**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06741
- **作者**: Ruikang Li, Zixuan Chen, Jiarui Wu, et al.
- **发表**: 2026-11-04 · cs.CV, cs.AI · 16 pages
- **热度**: HF daily papers 39 upvotes（2026-11-08 抓取）

## Abstract（节选原文）

> Strong single-turn editing performance does not guarantee reliable multi-turn interaction: as edited outputs are repeatedly reused, small unintended changes can accumulate into severe visual drift. … TailSteer follows a simple principle: keep early denoising dynamics close to those of the base model and concentrate preservation corrections near the trajectory tail … These corrections are learned from the editor's own on-policy denoising rollouts, using unchanged source content as a preservation reference without requiring curated ground-truth editing pairs.

## 中文摘要

多轮图像编辑漂移问题（cs.CV，HF 39 热度）：编辑输出被反复复用时，微小意外改动累积成严重视觉漂移。TailSteer 是末段训练框架：早期去噪动力学贴近基模型，把保持性修正集中到轨迹尾部压制细粒度偏差；修正从编辑器自身 on-policy 去噪 rollout 学习，以未修改内容为保持参照，无需人工标注的编辑对，也不改动预训练先验。FLUX/SenseNova/Qwen 系列上，15-20 轮编辑的累积漂移显著下降，同时文生图与单图编辑基准性能基本保留。结论：长程编辑鲁棒可以在基础模型训练完成之后解锁，而非重学已有能力。

## 边界

- 属训练方法而非免训练推理插件；漂移减少在 15-20 轮窗口内测量；项目页 lyricccco.github.io/tailsteer
