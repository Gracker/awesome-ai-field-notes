## 原文摘录

> Goldman Sachs has restricted Anthropic's Claude Code and Microsoft's GitHub to its engineers, Bloomberg News reported, following a review of productivity and risk concerns. Internal guidance updated in October cuts off most traders and bankers; strats across trading, quantitative research and software engineering still have access. The bank barred its Hong Kong bankers from Claude in April and is now a lead underwriter on Anthropic's IPO. As recently as July, chief information officer Marco Argenti was praising the tools traders built with AI coding agents.
