## Summary

PHP2Shell 漏洞真实且重要，但作者认为它远没有传播说的那么好打，与 Log4Shell/Heartbleed/RoR YAML 完全不同级——那些是单请求触发，他逐条对照。观察：GitHub 上「字面意义上几千个」PoC 仓库，没有一个能打穿 vanilla PHP 实例；他邀请读者自起 Docker 亲手验证（别让 LLM 代跑）。归因：五年前大家能分清可利用性，现在「人被自己的 LLM 骗了——LLM 说你有 exploit，其实没有」。置顶编辑强调该打补丁还是要打。与 Wiz 一周利用链、AWS 真实遥测并读才完整：可利用性是分层分布的，顶级研究员的成果不等于每个攻击者手里都有现成武器，假 PoC 海还会让按 PoC 数量评估风险的防守方系统性失真。

The PHP2Shell vulnerability is real and matters, but Elias Taylor argues it is nowhere near as easy to exploit as the coverage suggests - unlike Log4Shell, Heartbleed or RoR YAML, which need no more than a single request. He observes literally thousands of PoC repos on GitHub, none of which defeat a vanilla PHP instance, and invites readers to verify by hand against their own Docker setup rather than letting an LLM run it. His diagnosis: people are being fooled by their own LLMs, which deliver 'you have an exploit' when they do not. Read alongside Wiz's week-long chain and AWS's telemetry, exploitability is layered - and the fake-PoC flood systematically distorts risk assessment by PoC count.
