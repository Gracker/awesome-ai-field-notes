## 摘要（中文）

CACM 观点文：AI 编程助手像外部记忆，卸下语法/样板/API 回忆，但把难点推向推理、架构理解、判断与结构意识。编程并未变简单，而是 differently difficult；教育要从「写出代码」转向验证、整合、解释与长期维护。四条转移：场域开放、工作难度换位、教育变革、角色从知识容器变为编排者。适合作为团队能力建设与培训叙事底稿。

## Summary (English)

CACM opinion piece: AI coding assistants act as external memory systems that offload syntax, boilerplate, and API recall, relocating difficulty toward reasoning, architectural comprehension, judgment, and structural awareness. Programming is not easier—it is differently difficult. Education and roles should shift from knowledge vessel to orchestrating agent: verify, integrate, explain, and maintain generated code. Frames four shifts: broader entry, relocated difficulty, education change, and evolving programmer identity.

## One-liner
