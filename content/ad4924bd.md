## English Abstract

An analysis of the AI industry's debt-funded buildout: with 10-year Treasury yields at 5.33%, hyperscalers expect to issue $400 billion in bonds in 2027 alone; Oracle's $18 billion September data-center bond sale would carry over $7 billion in added interest priced at current rates; CoreWeave trades at 672-882bps spreads with short-end effective yields in the teens; and loss-making AI labs' compute commitments require at least $50 billion in annual debt refinancing at likely junk-grade credit ratings.

## English Summary

Zitron assembles the debt-side evidence chain of the AI bubble: bond issuance forecasts, repricing losses on Oracle's data-center debt, neocloud spreads, and the annual refinancing gap behind AI labs' compute commitments.
