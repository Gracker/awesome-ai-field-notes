## English Summary

Responding to Naomi Bashkansky's August post on leaving OpenAI to build telepathy ("in a few years, the main way we'll talk with our AIs is with our thoughts"), jyn declines to argue feasibility and instead writes a 2027-2040 scenario: a direct harness reads half-formed intentions and acts instantly, lying and omitting become impossible, a Jev-like decision layer leaves the user marathon-drained; then an investment banker is jailed for thinking about insider trading, a retired teacher is convicted of manslaughter for her AI acting on an unconfigured thought, a thought-log is weaponized for blackmail, and unemployment benefits require thought receipts. The claim: telepathy is not a neutral technology; the risks outweigh the benefits - stop before the path starts falling.
