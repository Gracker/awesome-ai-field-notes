## 中文导读

LLM 越来越多地当 judge 和 grader 替代人类标注，什么时候这个替代在统计上站得住？此前的 Alt-Test 只适用于所有条目由同一批标注员处理的稠密场景，而大规模标注里条目往往由不同子集标注。AltRA-Test 把检验推广到无共享标注员场景：bootstrap 重采样条目，每条抽 k 个人类标注，把其中一个换成 LLM 标注，得到配对的全人类面板和混合面板；当混合面板的标注者间一致性统计上不劣于全人类面板时，支持替代。论文证明该检验是渐近水平 alpha 的一致检验，并在 18 个 LLM、5 种提示技术、20 个跨文本/图像/音频/视频数据集上评估：约 100 条标注常常就够用；仿真中比基线做出更多正确的替代决策；真实数据里 LLM 约在半数场景通过——替代并不总是成立，建议按任务先测。

## 为什么值得关注

"LLM judge 能不能替人"现在多靠榜单分数拍脑袋，AltRA-Test 给出的是可执行统计检验：约 100 条标注就能出结论，且真实数据里约半数场景不通过——这个失败率本身就是重要信息。任何用 LLM 做自动评测/标注替代的团队都值得把这套检验跑一遍自己的任务。

## English Summary

LLMs are increasingly used as judges and graders to replace human annotators, raising the question of when such replacement is statistically justified. The earlier Alt-Test only applies to dense settings where all items are annotated by the same annotators; in large-scale annotation, items are often annotated by different subsets. AltRA-Test extends the test to settings without shared annotators: a bootstrap resamples items, draws k human annotations per item, and substitutes one with an LLM annotation, producing paired all-human and mixed human-LLM panels; replacement is supported when the mixed panel's inter-annotator agreement is statistically non-inferior to the all-human panel's. The paper establishes AltRA-Test as a consistent test of asymptotic level alpha and evaluates it with 18 LLMs and 5 prompting techniques on 20 datasets across text, image, audio, and video: about 100 items often suffice; in simulations it makes more correct replacement decisions than baselines; on real-world data LLMs pass in about half of setups, so replacement is not always justified and should be tested per task.
