## 中文摘要

Cory Doctorow 10 月 3 日长文，把战后「和平红利」三要素（工业产能、政治自由、压抑需求）叠在 Piketty 300 年资本流向研究上。关键机制：当财富向 1% 集中达到破裂点，寡头统治引发的崩溃会不成比例地摧毁寡头自己的资本——90% 财富在 1% 手里时，灾难消灭的大部分正是他们的财富，二战后的「辉煌三十年」由此而来。他判断美国正按同一节奏走向破裂，叠加气候冲击与对美元、石油、电信基础设施的攻击，「后美国世界」已在拉锯；AI 在文中被定位为寡头的最后赌注——把世界猜测机器喂大到足够造出神来管理，全栈因此在抢电力、铜、光纤与信贷。十年级别的结构性叙事，可与 AI IPO 季与数据中心债重定价的报道对照读。

## One-liner

Doctorow：寡头资本集中与破裂的周期叙事，AI 是寡头的最后赌注

## English Summary

Doctorow layers the post-war 'peace dividend' (industrial capacity, political freedom, pent-up demand) on Piketty's 300-year capital-flow record: when wealth concentrates to a breaking point, an oligarchy-triggered collapse disproportionately destroys the oligarchs' own capital - if 90% of wealth belongs to 1% of people, a disaster wiping out most capital mostly destroys theirs - which is how the 'trente glorieuses' became possible. He argues the US is steaming into the same situation, compounded by climate shocks and attacks on dollar, oil and telecom infrastructure, and frames AI as the oligarchs' last bet - feeding the world-guessing machine until it wakes up a god - driving the scramble for power, copper, fiber and credit. A decade-scale structural narrative pairing well with AI-IPO-season and data-center-debt coverage.

## 原文摘录

> If 90% of the wealth is in the hands of 1% of the people, a war or disaster that wipes out most capital will mostly destroy the wealth of the 1%.
