# Questions

-   **Do we reach ASI?** Do we reach the point where AI is massively better than humans at almost all cognitive tasks, or does the intelligence explosion fizzle out, and produce minds that have very jagged capabilities, and are worse than humans at some things?
-   **Do we solve alignment?** Can we instill our own values into the AI, or does it have its own evolved, ahuman value system?
-   **What kind of alignment?** Intent alignment means an AI that reliably follows the intent of its operator (that is: only takes actions the operator would approve of), value alignment means aligning to an impersonal system of values.
-   **Takeoff severity?** If the AI is unaligned, how fatal is that for humans? Do we get an out-of-control paperclip maximizer, or a more benign AI that is unaligned but which cares enough to stay out of our way?

## English Summary

Borretti's Nov 11 essay maps AI futures as separately scorable sub-questions: does ASI arrive, does alignment solve intent or values, how lethal is misalignment, and does a singleton or an ecosystem remain. Scenarios come with mechanisms: Misuse Doom keeps control intact while AI merely accelerates dangerous research; Enduring Complementarity admits the G was never real, humans move to management as junior roles shrink; its shadow, Permanent Underclass, zeroes labor income into capital ownership and welfare; The Singleton pairs successful alignment with a pivotal act like permanent surveillance before a ten-trillion-year starfaring era; MAD World distributes aligned ASIs whose mutual destruction keeps the peace, noting wrecking the planet is far easier than building a survival-capable civilization.
