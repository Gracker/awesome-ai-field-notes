A widely shared Chinese-language field note (1.1k likes) on running Claude Opus 5.5 through Google's stack instead of Anthropic billing: buy Google AI Ultra ($199/month, payable with domestic Chinese cards), set the Google account's associated country to US to unlock Antigravity, then log into the Antigravity CLI with Google OAuth and select Opus 5.5 - a US VPS IP required throughout. The author claims it is the most stable route he has tested (no foreign payment, clean residential IP, or KYC), with Gemini and Claude quotas billed separately; replies broadly report the bundled quota is too small to be useful. A snapshot of grey-market access routes around regional payment and subscription walls.
