## English Summary
Google released Gemini 4 Neon on Nov 18 as its 'fast frontier model': Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index 61 (Xhigh), #3 overall; Vals Index 70.9% on the Pareto frontier; Agent Arena Index 1336, #2 globally; steerability #2; lowest fabrication rate in internal Antigravity testing. Pricing is $3/M input ($0.15/M cached, 95% discount) and $15/M output with 1M context, available in the Gemini API, AI Studio, Antigravity and the Gemini app. Strong enough to enter the backup-lane candidate pool - long context and low fabrication are useful for automated pipelines - but the #3 overall rank means the pure reasoning ceiling still yields to larger, slower models; benchmark on your own workflow before switching the main lane.

## 原文/证据摘录
· Gemini 4 Neon 发布：$3/$15 定价卡进代理主力档

Google 11 月 18 日发布 Gemini 4 Neon，定位是"快的前沿模型"。公开榜单：Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index 61 分、xhigh 档总榜第 3（排在更大更慢的模型之后），Vals Index 第 2（70.9%）且落在 Pareto 前沿，新的 Agent Arena Index 上 1336 分、全球第 2，steerability（任务中途被纠正后的适应）单项第 2，内部 Antigravity 测试里 fabrication 率是其历史最低。价格 $3/M 输入（缓存命中 $0.15/M，95% 折扣）、$15/M 输出，1M token 上下文，已在 Gemini API、AI Studio、Antigravity 和 Gemini app 可用。

判断：这个价格加这组 agent 榜单成绩，够格进备用 lane 的候选池，尤其长上下文和低 fabrication 两项对自动化流水线有用。但总榜第 3 说明它在纯推理上限上仍让位于更大更慢的模型，换主干之前先在自己的实际工作流上跑一轮对比，别按榜单直接切。
