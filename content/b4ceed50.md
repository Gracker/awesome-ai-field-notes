# Anthropic 发布 81,000 用户经济展望与担忧研究

## English

Last month, we published our look into what 81,000 people told us they want from AI.

In new research, we've investigated the economic hopes and worries referenced in their responses.

Read more: https://www.anthropic.com/research/81k-economics

---

We invited Claude users to share how they use AI, what they dream it could make possible, and what they fear it might do.

Nearly 81,000 people responded in one week—the largest qualitative study of its kind.

Read more: https://anthropic.com/features/81k-interviews

## 中文

上个月，我们发布了关于 81,000 人对 AI 期望的研究。

在新研究中，我们深入分析了他们在回答中提到的经济展望与担忧。

阅读完整研究：https://www.anthropic.com/research/81k-economics

---

我们邀请 Claude 用户分享他们如何使用 AI，他们期望 AI 能实现什么，以及他们担忧 AI 可能带来什么。

近 81,000 人在一周内做出了回应——这是同类研究中规模最大的定性研究。

阅读完整报告：https://anthropic.com/features/81k-interviews
