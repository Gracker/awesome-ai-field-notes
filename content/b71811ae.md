## 中文正文

OpenAI 于 2026 年 4 月公布了 **GPT-5** 的部分技术细节，重点展现了多模态能力的显著提升：

- **图像输入代码生成精度达 85%**：GPT-5 能根据图像输入生成代码，精度大幅提升，表明视觉-语言-代码的跨模态能力取得突破。
- **语音识别精度大幅提升**：在语音理解和生成方面有质的飞跃。
- **引入视频理解能力**：GPT-5 新增长视频内容的理解能力，支持对视频帧序列的语义分析。

GPT-5 编程能力的提升在教育领域具有巨大潜力，有望成为编程教学的强大辅助工具。

## English Original

OpenAI released partial technical details of GPT-5 in April 2026, highlighting significant improvements in multimodal capabilities:

- **Code generation accuracy from image input reaches 85%**: GPT-5 can generate code from image inputs with greatly improved accuracy, indicating a breakthrough in cross-modal vision-language-code capabilities.
- **Significantly improved speech recognition accuracy**: Major qualitative leap in speech understanding and generation.
- **Introduction of video understanding capabilities**: GPT-5 newly adds the ability to understand long video content, supporting semantic analysis of video frame sequences.

The improved programming capabilities of GPT-5 hold great potential in education, poised to become a powerful assistant for programming education.
