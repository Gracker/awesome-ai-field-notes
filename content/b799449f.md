## English Summary

Citing an Axios report drawing on twenty-plus sources close to internal discussions: unreleased models inside OpenAI and Anthropic are powerful enough that many employees believe they can no longer be safely released, with concern reaching the top of both companies. Marcus allows that PR motives may be mixed in but sees real fear; his core argument is that capability progress has not been matched by comparable progress in explainability and control, and turning models into internet-connected agents changes the risk profile. He recalls his 2022 'bull in a china shop' framing, notes the absence of an independent review body, and proposes companies proactively pull internet-accessed agents until adequate safety measures exist, and never release models lacking internal consensus on safety.
