## 中文导读

NVIDIA 董事会宣布（2026-11-12）：Jensen Huang 即刻辞去总裁、CEO 及董事职务；CFO Colette Kress 出任临时 CEO（同时保留 CFO 职务，直至选定正式继任者）；Harvey C. Jones 出任公司史上首位董事长。董事会已启动正式 CEO 搜寻，内外部候选人皆在考虑。Kress 自 2013 年 9 月起任 NVIDIA CFO，此前在 Cisco、Microsoft 等公司有近 25 年财务履历；Jones 有 30 余年创业、科技高管与风险投资经历，曾任 Synopsys CEO 兼执行董事长。声明称公司运营、客户承诺与产品路线图预期「不间断」继续，并感谢 Huang 33 年来对公司的贡献；新闻稿本身未说明辞任原因。Q3 FY2027 财报仍按原计划 11-17 发布。

- NVIDIA 是 AI 算力供应链的中心公司，掌门人更替对每一个依赖其路线图的实验室与创业公司都是实质信号

- 财报按原定时间发布（11-17），公司明确传递「业务不中断」预期

## 关键信息

> NVIDIA (NASDAQ: NVDA) Board of Directors announced today that Jensen Huang has resigned as President, Chief Executive Officer, and a member of the Board, effective immediately.

> Kress will continue to serve as Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer while serving as Interim Chief Executive Officer until a permanent successor has been named.

> "We expect the company's operations, customer commitments and product roadmap to continue without interruption." — Harvey Jones, Chair of the NVIDIA Board of Directors

## English Summary

NVIDIA's board announced (Nov 12, 2026) that Jensen Huang has resigned as President, CEO, and board member, effective immediately. CFO Colette Kress was appointed Interim CEO while retaining her CFO role until a permanent successor is named; Harvey C. Jones became the company's first-ever Chair of the Board. The board is initiating a search for a permanent CEO, considering internal and external candidates. Kress has been NVIDIA's CFO since September 2013, with ~25 years of finance roles at Cisco and Microsoft before that; Jones served as CEO and Executive Chairman of Synopsys. The release gives no reason for the resignation, states that operations, customer commitments and the product roadmap are expected to continue without interruption, and confirms Q3 FY2027 results remain scheduled for November 17. A leadership change at the center of the AI compute supply chain is a material signal for every lab and startup dependent on NVIDIA's roadmap.
