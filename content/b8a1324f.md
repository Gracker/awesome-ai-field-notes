## 中文正文

Gary Marcus 汇总了四项新研究，结果一致警示：**LLM 医疗建议准确率低，不应作为临床决策依据**。

**BMJ 研究**：近 50% 的 ChatGPT 回复在癌症、疫苗、营养相关问题中存在严重问题，且以自信语气呈现错误信息。

**Nature Medicine 研究**：模拟参与者判断"处置方案"的准确率为 57.3%±5.6%，识别相关条件的准确率为 60.7%±5.5%——虽然高于普通人类参与者，但远未达到临床应用标准。

**MIT 研究**：LLM 在推荐医疗方案时会受到患者消息中非临床信息的干扰，如拼写错误、空格、性别标记缺失、或不确定/夸张/非正式语言的使用，都会影响判断。

这些研究共同表明：尽管 LLM 在特定医学问答任务上表现不错，但在实际临床场景中尚不可靠，不应替代专业医疗人员的判断。

## English Original

Gary Marcus compiles four new studies with converging warnings: **LLM medical advice has low accuracy and should not guide clinical decisions**.

**BMJ Study**: Nearly 50% of ChatGPT responses on cancer, vaccines, and nutrition had serious problems, delivered with confident-sounding but incorrect information.

**Nature Medicine Study**: Simulated participants achieved 57.3%±5.6% accuracy in determining disposition and 60.7%±5.5% accuracy in identifying relevant conditions — above average human participants, but far below clinical application standards.

**MIT Study**: LLMs can be tripped up by non-clinical information in patient messages — typos, extra whitespace, missing gender markers, or the use of uncertain, dramatic, and informal language — all of which affect judgments.

Together, these studies show that while LLMs perform well on specific medical QA tasks, they remain unreliable in real clinical settings and should not replace professional medical judgment.
