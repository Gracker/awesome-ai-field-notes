## English Version

Public conversation about AI often centers on abstract projections of its risks and benefits. What's largely missing is a vision for what "AI going well" means, grounded in the concrete aspirations of people around the world who already use AI.

Over one week in December, we invited everyone with a Claude.ai account to sit down with an AI interviewer to tell us about how they view AI. **80,508 people**, across 159 countries and 70 languages, took the interview. We believe this is the largest and most multilingual qualitative study ever conducted.

### What People Want from AI

**01. Professional excellence (18.8%)**  
Improve effectiveness by having AI handle routine tasks so they can focus on higher-value strategic work.

**02. Personal transformation (13.7%)**  
Achieve personal growth, emotional wellbeing, or life transformation with AI as guide, coach, or support.

**03. Life management (13.5%)**  
AI as comprehensive organizational support and cognitive scaffolding—managing schedules, reducing mental burden.

**04. Time freedom (11.1%)**  
Reclaim time from work and chores to be present with family or friends, pursue hobbies, travel, rest.

**05. Financial independence (9.7%)**  
Achieve financial freedom through AI—income generation, business building, investments, passive income.

**06. Societal transformation (9.4%)**  
Solve major societal challenges—poverty, disease, climate, inequality—using AI for broad human flourishing.

**07. Entrepreneurship (8.7%)**  
Build, launch, and scale businesses with AI as force multiplier.

**08. Learning & growth (8.4%)**  
Use AI as learning accelerator and personalized teacher.

**09. Creative expression (5.6%)**  
Use AI to help bring creative visions to life—art, games, music, films, books.

### Are People Getting What They Want?

When asked if AI had ever taken a step towards their stated vision, **81% of people said yes**.

Where AI has delivered: **Productivity (32.0%)**, **Cognitive partnership (17.2%)**, **Learning (9.9%)**.

Where AI fell short: **AI hasn't delivered (18.9%)**—inaccurate or unreliable outputs, or not yet capable of what they envision.

## 中文版

关于 AI 的公开讨论通常集中在对其风险和收益的抽象预测上。更缺少的是对"AI 发展顺利"意味着什么的愿景——这一愿景应扎根于世界各地已经在使用 AI 的人们的具体愿望中。

去年12月的一周内，我们邀请所有 Claude.ai 账户持有者与 AI 面试官坐下来，分享他们如何使用 AI、梦想 AI 能实现什么，以及担心 AI 可能会做什么。**80,508 人**，来自 159 个国家和地区，使用 70 种语言，接受了这次采访。我们相信这是迄今为止规模最大、多语言最多的定性研究。

###人们对 AI 的期待

**01. 职业卓越（18.8%）**  
通过 AI 处理日常任务来提高效率，从而专注于更高价值的战略性工作。

**02. 个人转变（13.7%）**  
以 AI 为指导、教练或支持，实现个人成长、情绪健康或生活转变。

**03. 生活管理（13.5%）**  
AI 作为全面的组织支持和认知脚手架——管理日程、减少精神负担。

**04. 时间自由（11.1%）**  
从工作和家务中夺回时间，与家人和朋友相处，追求爱好、旅行和休息。

**05. 财务独立（9.7%）**  
通过 AI 实现财务自由或经济安全——收入生成、创业、投资或被动收入。

**06. 社会变革（9.4%）**  
解决重大社会挑战——贫困、疾病、气候、不平等——利用 AI 实现人类广泛福祉。

**07. 创业（8.7%）**  
以 AI 为力量倍增器来建立、启动和扩展业务。

**08. 学习与成长（8.4%）**  
使用 AI 作为学习加速器和个性化教师。

**09. 创意表达（5.6%）**  
使用 AI 帮助将创意愿景变为现实——艺术、游戏、音乐、电影、书籍。

### 人们是否得到了想要的？

当被问及 AI 是否曾为其所述愿景迈出一步时，**81% 的人表示是的**。

AI 已交付的领域：**生产力（32.0%）**、**认知伙伴关系（17.2%）**、**学习（9.9%）**。

AI 不足的领域：**AI 尚未交付（18.9%）**——输出不准确或不可靠，或尚不具备他们期望的能力。
