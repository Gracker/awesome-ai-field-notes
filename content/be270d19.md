## English Abstract

Alex Kladov's September 19 fuzzing case study starts with a regex::Regex::new(".abb|b") plus "zabb" regression: an old version returned "b" instead of the full "zabb". He wrote a 100-line fuzzer using regex and regex_lite as a cross-implementation oracle, plus a swarm-testing twist pick a random subset of the alphabet first, then build strings from that subset, which beats feeding long random strings; regex generation follows the same recipe. The fuzzer not only reproduces the target bug but also surfaces a second one.
