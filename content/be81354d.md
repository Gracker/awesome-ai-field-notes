## English Summary

Google Research reports results from Antigravity's /teamwork multiagent systems paired with Gemini 4 models (Argon, Carbon, Hydrogen) on open problems in mathematics, chemistry, systems software, and ML engineering, all in collaboration with human experts. The design targets two model limits: unpredictable single-attempt generation is bridged by pairing parallel exploration with dedicated falsifiers (Lean 4 proof checker, unmodified test suites, execution simulators, web search, hardware counters), and attention dispersal on multi-day tasks is addressed by partitioning context by construction — each specialized agent sees only its needed files and tools, coordinators hand off to fresh successors, and critics/auditors verify from clean contexts. Headline results: full proof of the 2013 Courtade–Kumar conjecture with a 50M-line Lean 4 formalization; a machine-checked end-to-end formalization of the Weil conjectures (~1.8M lines of Lean across 28,800 subagent invocations); 95% top-1 NMR molecular-structure identification with 30 specialized agents; a libgav1 Rust rewrite that ended 20–30% faster than the original C++; and a 24% reduction in TPU workload preemption from Borg agentic scheduling. The tools are available externally, with six months of free top-tier access for 10,000 academic researchers.
