## English Summary

Daniel Lemire documents arXiv's new rate-limit policy: from October 1, 2026, submissions are capped at two per person per month. arXiv received over 40,000 submissions in September, roughly double the ~20,000 of two years ago, and volunteer moderation cannot sustain the growth. He notes Google also stopped accepting new bug reports in its open-source bounty program due to the influx, reading both as signs of research feedback infrastructure approaching overload.
