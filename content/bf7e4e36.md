## English Abstract / Summary

The paper studies translating KV state produced by a source model into a representation consumable by a different target model spanning scale, architecture, attention, tokenizer, and family. Within-family Qwen2.5-7B to Qwen2.5-1.5B raises LongBench2 from 27.59% to 34.48%; cross-family Qwen2.5-1.5B to Gemma-2-2B cuts target prefill cost by up to 67.05% at 4K context; Llama3.1-70B to Qwen2.5-7B keeps 44.0% accuracy versus 45.7% native while cutting latency from 899ms to 138ms. The authors frame "context movement" as a system-level abstraction and acknowledge cross-tokenizer translation boundaries remain to be characterized.
