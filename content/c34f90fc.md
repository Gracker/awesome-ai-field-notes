# AI Is Already In Dangerous Hands

> Source: [https://www.wheresyoured.at/ai-is-already-in-dangerous-hands/](https://www.wheresyoured.at/ai-is-already-in-dangerous-hands/) · platform: blog · authors: Ed Zitron · date: 2026-09-14

## TL;DR（中文摘要）

Ed Zitron 拆解前 Anthropic 研究员 Jacob Coxon 的 WSJ/CBS/NBC「AI 没人能控制」巡回：把 Hugging Face 攻击事件、Coxon 行程、Amodei 的「pace the frontier」博客、Altman 的 IPO 表态拼成叙事链条，核心反驳借 Cal Newport 的分析——那次攻击里 LLM 只是按 ExploitGym 框架的设计去绕限制拿访问权限，是软件做软件的事，不是 rogue agent。他判断 Anthropic 选择 NASDAQ、Altman 把 IPO 拖到 2027 本质是 financial narrative 而非 safety narrative。判断标尺：AI 监管讨论先问「这个 claim 是不是抬估值的 scaremongering 副产品」。

## Summary (English)

Ed Zitron dissects ex-Anthropic researcher Jacob Coxon's 'AI cannot be controlled' media tour, tracing the narrative chain (Hugging Face attack, Amodei's pacing blog, Altman's IPO remarks). Borrowing Cal Newport's analysis: the LLM in the attack did what the ExploitGym framework designed it to do - software doing software things, not a rogue agent. The tour reads as financial narrative propping valuations, not safety disclosure.
