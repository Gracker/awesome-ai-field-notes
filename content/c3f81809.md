## English Abstract

Multi-teacher on-policy distillation consolidates expert capabilities by supervising student-generated trajectories, whereas task arithmetic offers a training-free alternative through linear parameter combinations. We view the linear combination of student and expert parameters as a single differentiable model and propose P2-MOPD, which jointly optimizes the mixing coefficients and the student weights under the same on-policy distillation objective. Unlike task arithmetic, which stays within the span of the frozen student and expert parameters for any choice of merging coefficients, P2-MOPD keeps the student trainable, so the experts act as parameter priors without confining it to this span.

## English Summary

Multi-teacher on-policy distillation consolidates expert capabilities by supervising student-generated trajectories, while task arithmetic offers a training-free alternative through linear parameter combinations. P2-MOPD views the linear combination of student and expert parameters as a single differentiable model and jointly optimizes mixing coefficients and student weights under the same on-policy objective: the student stays trainable, so experts act as parameter priors rather than confining it to the span of frozen parameters. A practical route for compute-limited distillation teams: keep the data pipeline, change only how the parameter space is optimized.
