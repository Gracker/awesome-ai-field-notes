## English Summary

Max Woolf's timeline of June-October 2026 OpenAI/Anthropic announcements explains frontier LLM price cuts of up to 80%: GPT-5.6 Sol launched at $5/$30/$0.50 with Luna at $1/$6/$0.10; on July 30 Luna was cut 80% to $0.20/$1.20/$0.02 (OpenAI's own chart showed it would otherwise lose to DeepSeek V4 Pro on cost-per-task); Claude Sonnet 5's temporary $2/$10 launch price became permanent in August; Sol got a ~20% three-month cut to $4/$20/$0.40; Fable 5.1 kept $10/$50 but cut cache reads 75% ($1 to $0.25). The mechanism is agentic-coding economics - cache reads are 96-99% of total token expenditure - plus external pressure from open-weight models: DeepSeek V4 Flash's updated 1017 variant is hosted by 29 distinct providers on OpenRouter, leaving little moat and forcing prices down.
