## English Summary

Fingerprinting of agentic cyberattacks: inferring the attacker's LLM version and harness from telemetry. Shibboleth traps are modular honeypot services that make informative interaction a precondition for obtaining resources the agent seeks, eliciting model declarations and characteristic writing without prompt injection. Harness fingerprinting probes execution peculiarities - how a harness masks secrets in tool output, enforces time limits, and schedules concurrent calls - using only effects visible in subsequent requests. Across 15 candidate models, combined declarative and behavioral inference reaches about 90% top-1 accuracy (behavioral classifiers evaluated on held-out harnesses and task families); fixed probe rules identify the correct harness among four with 92% accuracy. When agents are told to lie about identity, declarations collapse but behavioral evidence persists.

## Source Excerpts

### Abstract (opencli arxiv paper, 2026-11-26)

We study the fingerprinting of agentic cyberattacks... we introduce shibboleth traps: modular honeypot services designed to elicit discriminative behaviors from agentic attackers. The traps make informative interactions a condition for obtaining resources the agent seeks, eliciting model declarations and characteristic writing without requiring a successful prompt injection. Combined declarative and behavioral inference distinguishes among 15 candidate models with an average 90% top-1 accuracy; in a separate experiment across four harnesses, fixed probe rules identify the correct harness with 92% accuracy.
