## 摘要 (Summary)

Anthropic 承诺 10M CAD 给加拿大研究机构：分别与 Amii（Edmonton）/ Mila（Montral）/ Vector（Toronto）三大区域 AI 研究所和 CHEO / CAMH / Universit Laval / University of Toronto / Saskatchewan 达成合作，资金用于 RL / AI 安全 / 卫生 / 心理健康 / 多 agent 系统 / 机器人 / 低资源语言研究，同步发布首份加拿大国家 Anthropic Economic Index 简报加拿大占全球 Claude.ai 消费用 2.6% 排名第八，按 AUI 排第二，加拿大政府因双语需求在 NB / NS / QC 翻译请求最密集；并提到加拿大上周发布新国家 AI 战略 AI for All 以及 Alberta 政府用 Claude Code 在 20 小时审完 466M 行代码的政府 side 应用

## English Abstract / Excerpt

Anthropic commits $10M CAD to Canadian research institutions through partnerships with Amii (Edmonton), Mila (Montral), the Vector Institute (Toronto), CHEO, CAMH, Universit Laval, University of Toronto Data Sciences Institute, and University of Saskatchewan funding research into beneficial / responsible AI including RL, safety, health, mental health, multi-agent systems, robotics, and low-resource languages. Concurrently publishes the first Canada brief from the Anthropic Economic Index (Canada = 2.6% of global Claude.ai consumer use, ranks 8th globally and 2nd on AUI per-capita). Also notes Canada's June 2026 AI strategy 'AI for All' and the Government of Alberta case study using Claude Code to review 466M lines of provincial systems in ~20 hours.
