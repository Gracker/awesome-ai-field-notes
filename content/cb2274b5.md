## 摘要 (Summary)

软件工程（SE）的复用范式正从源码库组件服务向 AI 智能体中的可复用 skill（技能）迁移，AnthropicOpenAI 等平台已上线智能体 skill 仓库与市场，使开发者能把 SE 专业知识打包为可复用 skill论文开展首个大规模实证研究，对 14 个主流 skill 仓库与市场（涵盖 12K+ skill）系统分析 SE skill 的实际覆盖面生命周期分布设计与质量核心发现：现有 SE skill 严重偏向编码与调试任务，对需求设计测试运维等 SE 关键活动覆盖不足；skill 文档普遍缺乏与 SE 任务的明确映射，导致检索与组合效率低下研究提出了一个 SE-skill 分类体系与质量评估框架，并给出对智能体 skill 市场的设计建议

## English Abstract / Excerpt

Software engineering has continuously evolved through increasingly powerful forms of reuse, from source code and libraries to components and services. Recent advances in AI agents have introduced a potentially new reusable artifact: skills. Emerging agent skill repositories and marketplaces enable developers to package, share, and reuse SE expertise as reusable skills. This trend raises a fundamental question: what SE activities are being encapsulated into reusable skills? Existing studies primarily focus on a broad range of skills acquisition, safety, or benchmarking, while lacking a systematic understanding of SE-specific skills and their coverage across the software development lifecycle. To address this gap, we conduct the first large-scale empirical study of SE skills in 14 major skill repositories and marketplaces (12K+ skills)....
