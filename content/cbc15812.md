## Summary (English)

Mistral and Mozilla announce a partnership: Firefox's AI browsing assistant Smart Window (beta) is now powered by Mistral models, starting in France and North America with the UK and Germany to follow later in the year. The deal is structured around four arguments: (1) open technology needs open distribution — Mozilla's two-decade advocacy for an open web meets Mistral's open-weight frontier releases; (2) AI optimized for local countries and cultures, not exported to them — models are fine-tuned on regional languages and dialects; (3) user control and privacy first — Firefox Smart Window does not save conversations on Mozilla's servers by default and partners like Mistral agree to zero data retention; (4) sovereign AI in everyone's hands — the deal extends Mistral's enterprise reach to Firefox's consumer base. Joint sign-off: Anthony Enzor-DeMeo (Mozilla CEO) and Arthur Mensch (Mistral co-founder/CEO).
