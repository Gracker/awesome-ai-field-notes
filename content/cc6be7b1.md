Nielsen compares what AI site builders choose for you when a one-off page needs real dependencies: Lovable's fullstack React app silently wired comments to Supabase ('and a whole lot more' for analytics, commerce, AI), while Vercel's v0 asked after 11 seconds, then opened a Neon account on the user's behalf - the welcome email arrived days later. He asks why Supabase and not Firebase or Neon: model inference, statistical likelihood, or paid placement - and notes financial incentives make an already-uncertain model less predictable. Generating a website is easier than ever; understanding what you generated, who you now depend on, and why is not.
