## 中文导读

OpenAI 官方说明 banked Codex resets 的机制:仅在 GPT-6 Astra 推出期间,9-3 PT22:00 与 9-4 PT20:00 之前创建的 Plus/Pro/Business 新账户(以及现有账户在 9-3 与 9-4 当天)各获一次可留存重置;9-7 已为全球 Plus/Pro/Business 用户统一发放一次立即应用的常规重置(不留存、也不显示为 banked)。banked 重置在桌面/CLI/网页端「设置 → 用量」中可见,使用后会刷新 Codex 5 小时与每周用量周期并改写周重置日期;只有真正刷至少一个用量周期后才会消耗,无可重置周期时保持可用。

## Why it matters

- 这篇是 9-9 Codex「banked reset 点用未完整生效」事故的官方背景文档,给出了银行重置在 9-3 / 9-4 两轮的发放口径和 9-7 那轮常规重置(立即应用、不显示为 banked)的区别。
- 把「消耗规则 = 至少触发一个用量周期刷新」写明白,意味着 OpenAI 在事故里要补发的是触发失败的 banked,而不是 9-7 那轮常规重置。

- 关键事实:banked reset 在「设置 → 用量」中显示为「1 次可用重置 / 完全重置」;使用后改写每周重置日期

## 英文原文摘录(节选)

> Banked resets are one-time Codex usage-limit refreshes saved to your account, held until you use them or they expire. They aren't purchased credits and they don't permanently raise the limits on your plan.
>
> New accounts received one banked reset under the following conditions:
> **September 3, 2026:** Plus, Pro, and Business new accounts (including newly added Standard and Premium seats) created before 10:00 PM Pacific Time.
> **September 4, 2026:** Plus, Pro, and Business new accounts (including newly added Standard and Premium seats) created before 8:00 PM Pacific Time.
>
> Eligible existing Plus, Pro, and Business users in good standing (Standard and Premium seats) received a banked reset on both September 3 and September 4.
>
> Note: We issued a global reset on September 7, 2026 to Plus, Pro, and Business users worldwide — that reset applied to your account immediately, cannot be saved for later, and does not appear as a banked reset.
>
> A banked reset is only consumed when at least one eligible usage window actually refreshes. If no usage window can be reset, it remains available.
