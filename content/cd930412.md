## English Abstract / Excerpt

Process Reward Models (PRMs) are highly effective for test-time scaling (TTS), but existing PRMs are text-based and re-encode the entire trajectory from scratch; the O(L) scoring cost is a severe bottleneck for long multi-agent rollouts. KV-PRM is a highly efficient process reward model that eliminates heavy text re-encoding by directly reading the KV cache produced during the LLM's generation phase. By processing a single 'verify token' against the pre-existing KV cache, KV-PRM reduces the scoring cost from O(L) to O(L). We formally prove that the KV cache contains strictly greater information capacity than text for downstream reward modeling. Across MATH, GSM8K, and AIME benchmarks, KV-PRM matches or strictly outperforms text-PRMs under Beam Search, MCTS, and Weighted Voting, with up to 5,000x reduction in scoring FLOPs, 37x latency reduction, and 34x memory reduction.
