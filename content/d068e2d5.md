## English Summary

Gary Marcus pairs two same-day items: NVIDIA's Open Agent Safety Platform announced with over 100 industry partners (combining OpenShell and Sentry; Andrew Ng welcomed handing critical controls to deterministic symbolic code), and Florida seeking an injunction to pause ChatGPT development, citing Axios's reporting on top labs handling tens of thousands of security incidents a month. Marcus is skeptical NVIDIA software announcements move the needle (pointing to NemoClaw's fade) and treats the state-level injunction as the bigger signal: when regulators reach for injunctions rather than hearings, labs' voluntary commitments are no longer accepted as sufficient.
