## English Summary

Wiz Research details PHP2Shell: memory-safety flaws in how core PHP handles fatal errors (upload-progress unserializer use-after-free, fatal-error backtrace use-after-free and OPcache type confusion, grouped under CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, up to CVSS 9.2) affecting every PHP release since 5.4 and enabling unauthenticated RCE on WordPress, Drupal and Joomla plus Heartbleed-style process-memory disclosure. The flaws existed for decades, but the exploit chain was at the edge of what human researchers could practically achieve; frontier AI models cut that barrier, enabling working exploits adapted across multiple targets in hours. Wiz frames this as a turning point for what counts as vulnerable in the AI era, with discovery and patch verification assisted by Calif and OpenAI.
