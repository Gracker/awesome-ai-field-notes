## English Summary

Ed Zitron rolls up two weeks of public data: Fidelity's Jurrien Timmer calls the AI trade 'dead money for more than three months' with token expenditures and H100/A100 lease rates flat to down; Morgan Stanley estimates more than half the GPU servers sold between 2026 and 2028 might have nowhere to plug in, Jefferies extends the bottleneck to people, transformers and cooling, and AI-related debt issuance should run around $570B in 2026. The core framing: OpenAI and Anthropic make up 70-80% of AI revenues and compute demand; over $400B of 2027 AI investment is debt-funded and hyperscalers remain $124.2B short of break-even even counting both labs' revenues. Oracle's Project Jupiter triggered a force majeure notice; CoreWeave's 2025/26 bonds would be 35.8% more expensive if issued today, with 11-13% yields in the best case.
