## English Summary

claude.dev published a model-selection playbook by Addy Osmani (Nov 9, 2026): the generally available lineup is Haiku 5.5, Sonnet 5.5, Opus 5.5, and Fable 5.2, while Mythos 5.2 targets cybersecurity and life-sciences research via verification programs. It frames picking by task: Fable 5.2 as the 1M-context heavy-work tier ($8/$40 per Mtok), Opus 5.5 as the daily default, Sonnet/Haiku for cost control. It recommends measuring tokens and wall-clock per task across effort levels, checking compatibility before migrating, and trusting your own workload measurements over benchmarks or sentiment. Aligns with the ClaudeDevs guidance: keep daily work on Opus, move heavy tasks to Fable with effort tuned first.
