## 中文导读

要解释残差流里的一个方向，常规做法是扫语料库找强激活文本，但解释质量被语料覆盖面封顶论文提出 MAEM（Maximally-Activating Example Metamodels）：把"生成解释样例"本身变成训练任务——训练一个语言模型将主体模型的残差流方向映射为能强激活该方向的文本，目标是无标签 RL，即最大化生成文本激活与给定方向的余弦相似度。Qwen3.6-27B 的 MAEM 在 held-out 激活反演上胜过语料检索与自然语言自编码器（NLA），在多数 held-out 语言与领域上胜过 10M token 规模的语料检索，并激活了 89% 的 held-out SAE 特征；识别 steering vector 背后概念的能力与 NLA 相当，恢复 LoRA 后门载荷的频率高于语料检索，甚至能给模型内部 workspace 的独白生成言语化样例。

## 为什么值得关注

语料扫描的解释上限是可解释性规模化的老瓶颈：方向来自哪里，解释就受限于哪里。MAEM 把这个闭环改成"可训练、可前向验证"——样例好不好，在主体模型上跑一次前向传播即可核对，不需要人工判读，这是它区别于 NLA 等元模型的关键。论文覆盖 SAE 特征、steering vector、LoRA 后门、模型内部独白四类方向来源，说明该目标能跨方向类型扩展；对机制可解释性研究与后门检测都是直接可用的工具路线。

## English Abstract

To interpret a direction in a language model's residual stream, we can look for text that strongly activates it. But when such text is found by scanning a corpus, it is limited to what that corpus contains. We develop Maximally-Activating Example Metamodels (MAEMs), language models which map residual stream directions from a subject model to text exemplars that strongly activate those directions. Our objective is to maximise the cosine similarity of a supplied residual stream direction with the activations of the text generated by the MAEM. This label-free RL objective helps to scale MAEMs on diverse sources of residual stream directions. Empirically, we find the resultant metamodel produces informative exemplars: a Qwen3.6-27B MAEM inverts held-out activations better than corpus search or a natural-language autoencoder (NLA), beats corpus search (at 10M tokens) on a majority of held-out languages/domains, and activates 89% of held-out SAE features. It also identifies concepts behind steering vectors about as well as NLAs, recovers the payloads of LoRA backdoors more often than corpus search, and can even verbalise some model workspace inner monologue. Unlike explanations produced by other metamodels (e.g. NLAs), MAEM exemplars can be verified with a forward pass on the subject model, making the MAEM objective a grounded alternative for scaling the training of metamodels.

## English Summary

Maximally-Activating Example Metamodels (MAEMs): language models trained with a label-free RL objective to map a subject model's residual stream directions to text exemplars that strongly activate them, removing the corpus ceiling of scan-based interpretation. A Qwen3.6-27B MAEM inverts held-out activations better than corpus search or natural-language autoencoders, beats 10M-token corpus search on a majority of held-out languages and domains, and activates 89% of held-out SAE features; it identifies steering-vector concepts about as well as NLAs and recovers LoRA backdoor payloads more often than corpus search. Exemplars are verified with a forward pass on the subject model, giving a grounded alternative for scaling interpretability metamodels.
