## English Summary

Simon Willison merges the FT report with the Ramp AI Index (billing data from 70,000 card-spending companies): Anthropic's annualized revenue reached $65bn in July (up from $47bn in May) with 6,000 customers spending $100k+/yr and Q3 profitability expected; OpenAI's annualized revenue jumped 35% in the quarter to over $40bn after GPT 5.6's July launch. But Ramp's July spend breakdown flips the capability story: Opus 4.8 leads at 28.0% while the July-24-released Opus 5 holds only 3.5% and Fable 5 8.0% — price, not capability, sets the default model.
