## English Summary

Anthropic's S-1 registration statement applies to list Class A common stock on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under symbol "ANT", filed with the SEC on October 26, 2026. Prospectus financials: revenue of $41M (2023), $386M (2024) and $4.6B (2025) - YoY growth of 841% and 1,088% - and $16.3B for the six months ended June 30, 2026, up 1,300% YoY. Net losses since founding: $1.5B (2023), $8.3B (2024), $42.0B (2025) and $80.7B in H1 2026. Compute and infrastructure expense reached $7.3B in 2025 (+190%, 160% of revenue); TTM revenue (to Sep 30, 2026) rose ~1,200% while headcount grew ~170%. The filing quantifies internal AI leverage: over 90% of September 2026 codebase contributions were written by Claude, and average daily code volume per engineer in Q3 2026 exceeded 45x the 2024 average. Frontier model Mythos was released in April 2026 as a gated research preview via Project Glasswing for cybersecurity applications; products include Claude Code and Claude Cowork. Anthropic is incorporated as a Delaware public benefit corporation and qualifies as a "controlled company" under Nasdaq governance rules, with a four-class stock structure (A/N/T/F).
