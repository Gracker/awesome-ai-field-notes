## 中文导读

Anthropic 于 2026 年 10 月 8 日发布 **Anthropic Cyber Mission**，定位为「长期投入守护所有人依赖的系统」的防守方计划，起步拆成两条线：

- **关键基础设施（Critical Infrastructure Defense Program, CIDP）**：面向电网、水务、交通背后的运营技术（OT）与政府系统。OT 设备专有、改动风险高、常常无法停机打补丁，已知漏洞可能多年不修。CIDP 把前沿 Claude 模型、驻场工程师与威胁研究交给运营商信任的安全服务商，创始伙伴 11 家：Accenture、Booz Allen、CrowdStrike、Deloitte、Dragos、Hitachi、Insane Cyber、Nozomi Networks、Palo Alto Networks、PwC、Rockwell Automation。
- **开源软件（OSS Scanner）**：对开源生态依赖的自由共享代码找漏洞、提补丁、加固底层代码。OSS Scanner 为开源项目提供最强模型的免费定期安全扫描。

官博明确本周早些时候已把 Project Glasswing 并入扩版 Cyber Verification Program（CVP）；Cyber Mission 后续还会在新领域发布工具、研究与资源。

## 为什么值得关注

这是 Anthropic 把「前沿模型攻防能力」从研究项目转成常设组织形态的信号：Glasswing（研究）→ CVP（按身份分档放能力）→ Cyber Mission（按防守场景分产品线）。对安全厂商，CIDP 意味着 Anthropic 直接进入 OT 安全供应链的模型层；对开源维护者，OSS Scanner 意味着模型生成的漏洞报告将成为新的上游输入源。

## English Summary

Anthropic launched the Anthropic Cyber Mission (Oct 8, 2026), a long-term commitment to supporting defenders, starting with two areas: the Critical Infrastructure Defense Program (CIDP), bringing frontier Claude models, on-site engineers, and threat research to the trusted providers that secure operational technology (power grids, water systems, transportation) and government systems, with 11 founding partners (Accenture, Booz Allen, CrowdStrike, Deloitte, Dragos, Hitachi, Insane Cyber, Nozomi Networks, Palo Alto Networks, PwC, Rockwell Automation); and OSS Scanner, free periodic security scans of open-source projects by Anthropic's strongest models. Project Glasswing was merged into the expanded Cyber Verification Program earlier this week.

## 原文摘录

> Today we're launching the Anthropic Cyber Mission, a long-term commitment to securing the systems everyone depends on. The Cyber Mission is a new effort to support defenders with tools, research, and resources to secure their software and systems.

> These systems are known as "operational technology," and they often cannot be taken offline to patch, so known vulnerabilities can stay unresolved for years.
