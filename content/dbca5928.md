## 关键信息

- Atlas 机制：汇集数百个数据点（Reddit 帖子、内部反馈调查、职场信息、既往劳资行动），给美加英仓库打 100–900 工会活动风险分；对领导层而言分数越高越好，低分意味着更高工会化风险
- 分级应用：仓库分五档，Tier 1「活动迫在眉睫」到 Tier 5「无可感知风险」；文件称用于 staffing 决策、资源规划与标准动作
- 文件来源：Amazon 与加拿大工会 Unifor 诉讼中披露，经美国非营利媒体 Truthout 公开；BC Labour Board 裁定 Amazon 违反多项当地劳动法
- 英国 Tier 1 仓库：伯明翰 Sutton Coldfield 的 5 亿英镑机器人仓（EMA4）与考文垂附近 BHX4（爆料工人 Hylton 所在仓）；全网络共 16 个 Tier 1
- 背景事件：GMB 2024 年 7 月考文垂工会承认投票以 28 票之差失利；2023 年 1 月该仓工人成为英国首个对 Amazon 正式罢工的群体
- Amazon 回应：否认监控个别员工，称仅为 site/building 层面活动、不追踪个人意见或工会会员身份；并称 Atlas「目前未在英国使用」，但未回应是否以其他名字运行类似系统
- 先例与法律动向：2022 年法国 CNIL 因「过度监控」对 Amazon 罚款 3200 万欧元；Leigh Day 与 Foxglove 正在审查文件，考虑并入现有非法诱导诉讼

## English Source

Key excerpts from the openDemocracy article (fetched via `opencli web read`):

- "Atlas amasses 'hundreds of data points' – including posts on Reddit forums and employees' responses to internal feedback surveys, as well as wider information on the workplace and any previous industrial action – to award warehouses a risk score of between 100 and 900 for union activity. The lower the score, the more likely Atlas believes workers ... in that warehouse are to unionise."
- "Warehouses appear to be categorised into five tiers based on their Atlas score, where Tier 5 is 'no immediate perceived risk' and Tier 1 is 'future activity is imminent'. The document suggests this scale can be used to 'inform staffing decisions, resource planning, standard actions, and ownership to mitigate risks to Amazon labour structure'."
- "In total, 16 Amazon warehouses across the US, Canada and the UK are 'Tier 1', according to the document. Two are in the UK: a £500m robotic-fulfilment centre in Sutton Coldfield, north-east Birmingham (referred to by Amazon as EMA4), and the warehouse where Hylton works near Coventry (BHX4)."
- Amazon spokesperson: "This is site/building level activity only." Amazon also said Atlas "is not currently used in the UK".
- Michael Newman, partner at Leigh Day: the documents tell "a very different story" from Amazon's previous assertions that it does not seek to prevent workers from joining a union.
- The documents were disclosed in the legal challenge by Canadian union Unifor and shared with US non-profit publication Truthout; the British Columbia Labour Board ultimately ruled that Amazon had violated multiple local labour laws.

## English Summary

openDemocracy reports on internal documents disclosed in a court battle between Amazon and Canadian union Unifor: Amazon's in-house AI forecasting tool Atlas amasses hundreds of data points (Reddit posts, internal survey responses, workplace information, previous industrial action) to score warehouses in the US, Canada and the UK between 100 and 900 for likely union activity, with lower scores meaning higher perceived risk. Sites are tiered one to five, and the documents describe the scale as informing staffing decisions, resource planning and standard actions. Sixteen warehouses are Tier 1, including two in the UK (Sutton Coldfield EMA4 and Coventry BHX4). Amazon denies Atlas monitors individual employees, calling it site-level only; a partner at Leigh Day says the documents tell "a very different story" from Amazon's public stance on union joining.
