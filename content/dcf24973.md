## English

### Anthropic Increases Claude Code Weekly Limits by 50% Through July 13, 2026

On May 13, 2026 at 2:19 PM San Francisco time, the official @ClaudeDevs account announced a 50% increase in Claude Code weekly limits for all Pro, Max, Team, and seat-based Enterprise users.

**What changed:**
- Pro users: 50% more conversations and messages per week
- Max users: Increased limits for extended coding sessions
- Team users: Expanded quotas for team-wide usage
- Enterprise: Additional capacity for seat-based deployments
- All increases valid through July 13, 2026

**Context — The Anti-Codex Move:**
This appears to be a defensive move against OpenAI Codex, which launched with competitive pricing and capabilities. By temporarily boosting limits, Anthropic aims to retain and attract developers who might otherwise try competing products.

**What didn't change:**
- Session limits during off-peak hours (through May 28, 2026) are a separate promotion that doubles five-hour limits — this does NOT increase weekly limits
- The temporary promotion is distinct from the standard quota structure

**Strategic Implications:**
- Anthropic is signaling aggressive competition in the AI coding assistant market
- The July 13 end date suggests this is a promotional period, not a permanent change
- Developers should take advantage of the increased limits while available

## 中文

### Anthropic 将 Claude Code 周额度提升 50%，有效期至 2026 年 7 月 13 日

2026 年 5 月 13 日下午 2:19 分（旧金山时间），Anthropic 官方 @ClaudeDevs 账号宣布：所有 Pro、Max、Team 和按席位计费的 Enterprise 用户的 Claude Code 周额度提升 50%。

**变动内容：**
- Pro 用户：每周对话和消息数增加 50%
- Max 用户：延长编码会话的额度提升
- Team 用户：团队范围使用的额度扩展
- Enterprise：按席位部署的额外容量
- 所有增加额度有效期至 2026 年 7 月 13 日

**背景——反击 Codex：**
这被视为对 OpenAI Codex 的防御性举措。Codex 以有竞争力的定价和能力推出。通过临时提升额度，Anthropic 旨在留住和吸引可能尝试竞争产品的开发者。

**未变内容：**
- 非高峰期时段的会话限制（至 2026 年 5 月 28 日）是另一项单独促销，将五小时限制翻倍——这**不会**增加周额度
- 临时促销与标准配额结构是分开的

**战略影响：**
- Anthropic 在 AI 编程助手市场竞争中发出强烈进攻信号
- 7 月 13 日的截止日期表明这是促销期，非永久性变化
- 开发者应趁额度增加时充分利用
