## Summary (English)

Krebs and Bitsight investigation: registering an expired domain gave control of telemetry from ~38,000 H96 Android TV sticks. Devices act as residential proxies when TV is on, and spoof as Samsung/Huawei/Xiaomi phones to click ads on AI-generated websites operated by Fengwo Group when TV is off. Estimated $50K/day fraud revenue.
