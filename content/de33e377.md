## English Abstract

Chen Zili's 2026-09-03 KAIYUANSHE essay argues three points on competitive advantage in the Agentic Coding era: (1) The value of AI coding hinges on whether the model covers your daily baseline; he reports Codex/Sol Max shipping Cronexpr's winnow removal, asyncband's singleflight, and HawkEye v7 rewrite all with passing tests. (2) The core competency is the ability to articulate a wish plus the ability to build its delivery path i.e. System-1 quality and decision-point recognition while pure implementation is largely absorbed by agents. (3) Open-source libraries will be remodeled: many 'convenience dependencies' can now be inlined by AI, and Rust's tool-call feedback into training has largely flattened its readability gap.
