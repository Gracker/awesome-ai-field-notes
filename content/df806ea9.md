## English Summary

geohot grounds American long-term strategy in electricity: sell as many AI chips into China as possible, use the money to buy solar panel and battery factories, and build out generation capacity fast - compute is ultimately a function of electricity. Keep in mind his often-posted trend graph: if trends continue, China will have 10x the electricity of the US in 10 years. What actually scares him is the short-term zero-sum thinking of the "AI race": today's capabilities will eventually be matched, unless you believe ASI enables a decapitating first strike - which probably doesn't work (can you outthink steel and drone production?), but if it does, that may be the end. His concrete number: the nationalization of NVIDIA raised his estimate of imminent WWIII a lot.
