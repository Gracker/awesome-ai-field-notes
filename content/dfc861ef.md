## English Summary

Set theorist Asaf Karagila responds to OpenAI's announcement that the Partition Principle does not imply the Axiom of Choice: without touching the enormous Lean code, he rates the preprint desk-reject quality - unclear, muddled writing, strange structure, off terminology, theorems stated in ways nobody would expect (Lemmas 7.4 and 8.1), and at least three unpublished, unrefereed, non-arXiv lecture notes among the references (including his own, cited for a proposition he is certain appears in published work). The press release says progress; the media hears solutions - so mathematicians must either drop everything to verify, a Denial of Service on expert attention in his words, or watch the public and funding bodies conclude mathematicians are replaceable by bots. No whisky for Sam Altman, and no free copy-editing for OpenAI. He stresses he is not against AI as a tool; the community must first decide how to judge AI-assisted contributions before adopting them.
