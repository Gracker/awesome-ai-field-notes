## 中文摘要

直接挑明 cloud 大厂的 AI 营收几乎全部来自少数 AI 实验室互相倒手，是这一轮关于 AI 泡沫最克制也最有出处的复盘UBS / Wells Fargo / Barclays / Deutsche Bank 的具体估算指向同一件事70%-90% 的世界算力收入，最终依赖 VC 不断为 Anthropic 和 OpenAI 续命；2027 年他们要花掉 2000 亿美元以上算力预算，必须先融到 2500-3000 亿美元

## English Summary

Ed Zitron's most restrained yet best-sourced AI-bubble piece to date: UBS / Wells Fargo / Barclays / Deutsche Bank estimates converge on a single fact - 70-90% of global compute revenue ultimately depends on VCs continuously recapitalizing Anthropic and OpenAI. To spend their projected $200B+ compute budget in 2027 the labs must first raise another $250-300B.
