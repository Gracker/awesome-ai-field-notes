## 中文导读

TurnWise（2611.06728，2026-11-04，HF daily papers 榜首约 124 赞）针对流式视频理解的 GRPO 训练：既有方法在多个决策回合间共享轨迹级 advantage，早期成功后强化冗余回复、早期失败后抑制有价值的追问。TurnWise 改为回合级信用分配——用 silence-completed prefix scoring 从每个决策的剩余回报构造组内相对优势；turn-mean 策略优化在回合内平均 token 损失再跨回合平均，让「回复」与「等待」不因输出长度失衡。无需 critic 或额外 rollout，实验优于既有方法。

## 为什么值得关注

把 GRPO 的轨迹级优势拆到回合级，流式视频「何时开口」的信用分配有了干净解法

流式视频的「响应还是继续看」本质是一串序贯决策，轨迹级奖励把两个方向的错误都放大；这 paper 的 silence-completed prefix scoring 是对这个问题最直接的对症设计。

## English Abstract（节选）

> Turn-level credit assignment uses silence-completed prefix scoring to construct group-relative advantages from each decision's remaining return, separating its learning signal from earlier rewards. Turn-mean policy optimization averages token losses within each turn and then across turns, giving response and waiting decisions equal weight regardless of output length. These complementary designs improve streaming policy learning without a learned critic or additional rollouts.

## English Summary

TurnWise (arXiv 2611.06728, Nov 4, 2026, cs.CV/cs.CL/cs.LG; top of HF daily papers with ~124 upvotes) targets streaming video understanding, where a model repeatedly decides whether to respond or keep watching. Existing GRPO approaches keep a single trajectory-level advantage across turns, reinforcing redundant replies after earlier successes and discouraging useful follow-ups after failures. TurnWise assigns credit per turn: silence-completed prefix scoring builds group-relative advantages from each decision's remaining return, decoupling its learning signal from earlier rewards, while turn-mean policy optimization averages token losses within then across turns so respond and wait decisions weigh equally regardless of output length. No learned critic or extra rollouts; experiments show gains over prior streaming-video RL approaches.
