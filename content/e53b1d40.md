## English Summary

Idiallo pushes back on the centralized-AI narrative: Anthropic and OpenAI sell a future of vetted, safety-reviewed, per-token-billed API fiefdoms, yet developers already drift between models in IDE auto mode for a full day without noticing — the walled garden is loose underfoot. Local inference keeps getting cheaper; he runs Deepseek on a $400 machine without stress. The most informative example is schlarp.com's "everything I own, owned": one developer used an AI agent to reverse every peripheral within reach — plaintext shell on the microphone, a camera whose activity LED can be disabled, a light panel granting write access to anyone on the WiFi. Closed ecosystems are selling foot guns, and developers will use dark fiber to subvert them.
