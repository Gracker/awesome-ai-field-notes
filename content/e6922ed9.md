## English Summary

World Labs introduces Atlas, a multimodal autoregressive diffusion transformer pretrained from scratch on text, images, video and 3D. Atlas maintains 3D consistency across the inputs it has seen, can extrapolate beyond the visible context, and supports camera-controlled generation, scene reconstruction, and simulation. Performance is reported to keep scaling with training compute.
