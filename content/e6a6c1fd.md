## 中文摘要

把 Lauren Leek 的长文读成模型崩溃 = performativity = placelessness同一个机制，让 model collapse 这个纯 ML 概念跨出技术圈，是非 AI 读者也能拿走判断的那种科普Leek 给出三组硬数据：伦敦 640 条购物街上 18,000 家餐饮任意两条街 50% 重合；38,000 部电影中 2% 头部拿走 40% 评分；与平均酒吧越像的酒吧越不容易倒闭证明被推荐 = 长成平均这件事不是比喻

## English Summary

Cory Doctorow reframes Lauren Leek's essay by reading 'model collapse = performativity = placelessness' as one mechanism, lifting the concept out of pure-ML and into a frame non-AI readers can also act on. Leek backs it with three hard datasets: among 18,000 restaurants across 640 London shopping streets any two streets share 50% of venues; the top 2% of 38,000 films capture 40% of ratings; bars that look more like the average bar are less likely to close - empirical proof that 'recommended = average' isn't a metaphor.
