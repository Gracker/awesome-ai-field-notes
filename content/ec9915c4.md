Grounded in the full article text fetched via opencli: the agents ran DFT at two tiers (PBE+U fast, HSE06 accurate; band gaps and spin windows quoted from HSE06). Candidate 1, YBaMnFeO5, was designed from scratch: 2.35 eV gap, spin windows 1.0 eV (holes) / 1.4 eV (electrons), ordering ~420 K raw / ~490 K calibrated - but the required Mn/Fe checkerboard destabilizes near 950 K, so standard synthesis would likely scramble it. ## English Summary

A vals.ai researcher with a team of AI agents hunted antiferromagnetic semiconductor candidates using DFT simulations at two levels (PBE+U fast, HSE accurate). The agents designed YBaMnFeO5 (Luttinger compensated, ~420K raw / ~490K calibrated ordering temperature, but its required Mn/Fe checkerboard is thermodynamically unstable - likely hard to synthesize) and identified KV[Cr(CN)6], first reported 1999, which stays magnetically ordered to 376K (103C) with near-zero net moment (0.125 mu_B residual in the only, water-containing powder sample). Two candidates, one designed and one discovered, each with a complete computational evidence chain.
