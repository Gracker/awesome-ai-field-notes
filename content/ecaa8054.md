## 中文导读

Anthropic Frontier Red Team 发布 **OSS Scanner**：开源项目 opt-in 后，由最强模型（含 Claude Mythos）免费定期扫描漏洞。关键取舍写得很直白——**报告完全由模型生成，无人工 review/triage**，换取更快更频的扫描，代价是可能出现错误或无效报告。

支撑数字：

- 约半年内在全球重要开源项目里发现 **超过 29,000 个候选漏洞**，人工只来得及 review/triage 约 6,000 个；
- 越来越多维护者主动要求**批量接收全部未验证报告**——至今已按此方式直发近 5,000 份（附候选补丁）；
- CyberGym 基准上 LLM 找洞率从去年初的不足 20% 升至今年的 **超过 85%%**。

早期管道验证：请复核 CVD 报告的专家渗透测试员抽检 48 个项目的 97 个 critical/high，**85 个（88%%）达到 CVD 流程标准**；其余 12 个中 11 个真实但重复、仅 1 个误报。具名反馈：wolfSSL 收到 74 份报告仅 2 份无效、5 个成 CVE；PostgreSQL 称多份报告的修复「几乎可以直接用」；curl 的 Daniel Stenberg 称其中一个是 curl 近年最严重的漏洞之一。部分维护者反馈严重度可能偏高、或扫描器对项目威胁模型理解有偏差。

每份报告包含：self-contained reproducer、漏洞说明（尽量附 bisection 定位引入时间）、候选补丁（如有）。报名方式：核心维护者向 GitHub 仓库提交 PR 按模板登记；准入标准与 OSS-Fuzz 类似（对基础设施与用户安全有「关键影响」），个案决定。验证阶段已出现可串联成未认证 RCE 的漏洞链。

## 为什么值得关注

两条线都值得记：其一，「模型直出、无人工预审」的漏洞报告第一次成为面向整个开源生态的常设服务——人工验证 bottleneck（29k 候选 vs 6k 人工）被制度化地转嫁给维护者，这是漏洞披露流程二十年未见的结构变化；其二，88%% 抽检达标率给了「LLM 找洞已到生产可用」一个可引用的量化锚点。对做 agent 自主工作流的人，这是「高风险输出不加人工闸门直接对外发布」的罕见官方样本。

## English Summary

Anthropic's Frontier Red Team launched OSS Scanner: an opt-in service where the strongest models (including Claude Mythos) periodically scan enrolled open-source projects for free; reports are fully model-generated without human review or triage, trading some accuracy for faster and more frequent scanning. Over six months the team found 29,000+ candidate vulnerabilities but could manually triage only ~6,000; nearly 5,000 unverified reports went straight to maintainers on request. LLM vuln-finding on CyberGym rose from under 20%% in early 2025 to over 85%% in 2026. Expert pen-testers sampled 97 critical/high findings across 48 projects: 85 (88%%) met the CVD bar, 11 were real but duplicates, one invalid; wolfSSL got 74 reports with only 2 invalid and 5 CVEs. Each report carries a self-contained reproducer, an explanation with bisection where possible, and a candidate patch. Enrollment via PR to the GitHub repo; eligibility criteria similar to OSS-Fuzz.

## 原文摘录

> The outputs of this opt-in vulnerability scanner will be fully model-generated, without human review or triage. This will enable faster and more frequent scanning, but means that it is possible reports will be incorrect or invalid.

> We have discovered over 29,000 candidate vulnerabilities, but have only been able to manually review and triage approximately 6,000 of these.

> Of these, 85 (88%%) met the bar for our CVD process. Of the remaining 12, 11 were real but duplicated known issues or other findings from the scan, and only one was invalid.
