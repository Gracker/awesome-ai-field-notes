## 摘要 (Summary)

自动程序验证在工业实践中仍然困难：即便多个程序在任务语义上等价，其实现结构的差异也会显著影响可验证性论文提出 Diversify2Verify，一个面向 Why3 的分阶段 LLM 流水线：首先推导表示相关的契约（contract），然后生成并测试多种递归与命令式数组/列表实现，再使用有界 verifier-guided 注解修复尝试形式化验证团队同时构建了一个面向验证的基准：73 个整数数组列表任务，共 292 个实现变体实验显示：初始验证 96 个工件；经过两轮修复后通过 154 个；实现多样化策略显著提升整体可验证性结果表明，对 LLM 生成的等价实现主动引入结构多样性是提升自动验证覆盖度的有效路径，对自动程序合成与审计都有借鉴价值

## English Abstract / Excerpt

Program verification is crucial for software correctness, but producing fully verified programs remains difficult in practice. This paper studies whether implementation structure affects automated verifiability when multiple generated programs are intended to satisfy the same task-level semantics. We present Diversify2Verify, a staged LLM-based pipeline for Why3 that infers representation-specific contracts, generates and tests diverse recursive and imperative array/list implementations, and attempts verification with bounded verifier-guided annotation repair. We also construct a verification-oriented benchmark of 73 tasks over integers, arrays, and lists, yielding 292 implementation variants. Diversify2Verify verifies 96 artifacts initially and 154 after two repair passes, improving artifact-level verifiability....
