## English

> Source: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/ai/full-stack-ai-explainer (Google Blog, June 29, 2026)

If you've spent any time lately reading about AI or using AI tools, you've probably heard about "full-stack" AI and app development. Google expert Richard Seroter (who leads developer experience at Google Cloud) explains what it means to take a full-stack approach to AI — and why it enables Google to bring helpful AI to billions of people.

**What does "full-stack" actually mean?**
When the term "full-stack" originally came out in software development a decade or so ago, people were usually thinking about applications. Building an app required multiple specialized teams: a front-end developer to build beautiful UIs, a back-end developer to handle server-side logic, a dedicated database team. A "full-stack engineer" could work across all of these functions independently — taking an idea from a rough concept to a fully running piece of software without handing it off.

**So it started with apps, and now it's on to AI?**
Right. We've taken that exact same end-to-end principle and applied it to AI. If you're trying to deliver value with AI, you can either buy a bunch of disparate parts from different vendors and try to stitch them together yourself, or you can look for an integrated system where everything you need is already connected.

**What disparate parts can someone stitch together to make a full AI stack?**
An intentional AI stack needs a cohesive combination of layers: compute infrastructure, an AI model, an orchestration platform, and the user interfaces. At Google, we've deliberately invested in every single layer: TPUs as hardware, frontier models like the Gemini family, the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform, and interfaces people use daily like Maps and Gmail. We've essentially done the hunting for you and put all the necessary components right inside the box.

**Was it a deliberate, decades-long strategy?**
Yes. The bet on custom TPUs is already over 10 years old. Owning that thread throughout the entire stack lets Google deliver a level of service, performance and reliability that's very hard to achieve if you're at the mercy of multiple parties.

**Doesn't a full-stack platform lock people in?**
Google describes its AI platform as "opinionated but extensible" and "batteries included" — everything you need is ready out of the box. But you can plug in another model instead of Gemini, or different software instead of Google Workspace. The goal is for users to choose Google because of the completeness of the platform, not because they're forced into a closed choice.

**What are the benefits beyond simplicity?**
System reliability — when a technical failure happens at one layer, ownership of the platform lets it be caught and handled at another layer rather than waiting for an external provider. And an economic advantage — not paying third-party vendors means remarkably competitive pricing for customers.

**Best starting points?**
1. **Google AI Studio** — build and deploy prototype web apps in minutes, ship to Cloud Run with one click.
2. **Gemini Enterprise Platform** — low-code automation for inbox workflows, spreadsheet parsing, etc.
3. **Antigravity platform** — orchestrate more complex application or agent builds without advanced programming knowledge.

## 中文

> 来源：https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/ai/full-stack-ai-explainer（Google Blog, 2026-06-29）

如果你最近在读 AI 资讯或使用 AI 工具，大概已经听过"全栈（full-stack）" AI 与应用开发这样的说法。Google 专家、Google Cloud 开发者体验负责人 Richard Seroter 解释了"全栈"路径在 AI 中的真正含义——以及它如何帮助 Google 把有用的 AI 送达数十亿人。

**"全栈"到底是什么意思？**
"全栈"这个说法大约十年前在软件开发圈出现时，通常指应用开发。搭建一个 App 需要多个专门团队——前端开发做漂亮的 UI，后端开发处理服务端逻辑，再加一个独立的数据库团队。"全栈工程师"则能独立贯穿所有这些职能，把一个想法从粗略概念推进到一个完整可运行的软件，无需在中途反复交接。

**那 AI 怎么就"全栈"了？**
对。我们把这套端到端的原则原封不动搬到了 AI 上。要用 AI 交付价值，你可以走"东拼西凑"的路子：买一堆不同厂商的零件再自己组装；也可以直接选用一个已经把所有部件连通的集成系统。

**一个完整的 AI 栈需要哪些层？**
有意识的 AI 栈需要四层协同：计算基础设施、AI 模型、编排平台、以及用户界面。Google 在每一层都做了投入——TPU 作为自研硬件、像 Gemini 系列这样的前沿模型、Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform，以及 Maps、Gmail 这些用户每天都用的界面。把所有必要的部件都装进了同一个盒子。

**这是不是多年之前就有的战略意图？**
是的。对自研 TPU 的赌注已经超过 10 年。贯穿整个栈的"自持"让 Google 能交付一种很难靠多方依赖达成的服务水平、性能与可靠性。

**全栈平台会不会把人锁死？**
Google 把自己的 AI 平台形容为"有主张但可扩展（opinionated but extensible）"加"电池内置（batteries included）"——开箱即用。但你可以插一个不是 Gemini 的模型，或者把 Google Workspace 换掉。目标是用户因为平台的完整性而选择 Google，而不是因为被迫才留下。

**除了简单，还有什么好处？**
一是系统级可靠性——当某一层出现故障时，因为平台归自己所有，可以在另一层就抓住并处理，而不必坐等外部供应商修复。二是经济优势——不用付第三方供应商费用，可以给到更有竞争力的定价。

**三个最值得作为入口的起点：**
1. **Google AI Studio**：几分钟搭出原型 Web 应用，一键部署到 Cloud Run；
2. **Gemini Enterprise Platform**：低代码自动化收件箱与电子表格解析等工作流；
3. **Antigravity 平台**：编排更复杂的应用或 Agent 构建，而无需高深的编程背景。
