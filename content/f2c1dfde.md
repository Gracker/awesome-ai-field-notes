## English Summary

Dynomight's Nov 9 essay on Google's upcoming Android sideloading restrictions finds the debate under-theorized: even supporters concede the analysis is thin. Any credible account must grant that scams are real, the change helps, and 95% of users are barely affected. Scale: de-Googled forks serve ~3-5M users (0.1% of Android); 300-500M install outside the Play Store; fewer than 100k run Linux phones daily. If Google fully blocked independent installs, a share of those users would migrate to Lineage/Graphene, vendors would adapt, and EU-level policy intervention could follow - a downside far larger than the upside. His testable prediction: this tightening will not escalate into a full lockdown.
