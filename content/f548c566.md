## 摘要

Zitron 的 AI 债务指南第二部：第一部讲债务的形式（项目融资、可转债、债券，数万亿美元数据中心债务藏在表外），这一部讲债务的功能——钱花出去之后到底在发生什么。核心论点（付费墙预览版内容）：AI 数据中心债务按完美预期定价，投的却是成千上万个史上最宏大、最精密也最脆弱的基础设施项目，需要专业人才、材料和空前规模的电力接入。回顾部分确认：超大规模厂商未来两年需要数千亿美元债务且不得不保持完美盈利增长，整个行业在功能上依赖 OpenAI 与 Anthropic 的算力支出。这是评估本轮建设周期可持续性的关键参考。

## English Summary

Part two of Zitron's AI debt guide moves from the form of AI datacenter debt (project financing, convertible notes, bonds, trillions held off-balance-sheet) to its function. Core claim from the premium preview: the debt is priced for perfection yet funds thousands of the most ambitious, intricate, and fragile infrastructure projects in history, requiring specialist talent, materials, and power at unprecedented scale. The part-one recap reiterates that hyperscalers need hundreds of billions in debt while maintaining pristine earnings growth, and that the industry is functionally dependent on OpenAI and Anthropic's compute spend.
