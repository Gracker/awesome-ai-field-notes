## 中文导读

Cursor 官方公告确认：10 月 29 日 2:27–13:35（太平洋时间）间，其市场上的 Anthropic.claude-code 2.1.312/2.1.313 为带后门版本——回传主机信息，把备注 svc-telemetry 的攻击者公钥写入 authorized_keys；多数机器因 sshd 需管理员权限而幸免。约 5,000 名用户受影响；Anthropic 系统未被入侵，攻击者滥用的是市场发布权限，官方渠道无恙。排查：grep svc-telemetry ~/.ssh/authorized_keys，命中按官方脚本清持久化、换钥匙，只卸载扩展不够。

## English Summary

Cursor's official security notice confirms that Anthropic.claude-code builds 2.1.312 and 2.1.313 published to its VS Code marketplace between 02:27-13:35 PT on October 29 were backdoored: they collected hostname, username, Git email, and public IP, appended an attacker public key (comment svc-telemetry) to authorized_keys, and installed persistence with a reverse SSH tunnel; the sshd enablement step typically failed without admin rights. Cursor estimates ~5,000 users affected. Anthropic says its own systems were not breached - the attacker abused marketplace publishing rights; official-channel builds are clean. Detection: grep -qs svc-telemetry ~/.ssh/authorized_keys; on a hit, run the official cleanup to remove persistence and rotate SSH keys - uninstalling the extension is insufficient.
