Marcus 转 Axios（Madison Mills，10 月 22 日）的报道：美国银行正因 AI agent 被用来开未授权账户而收紧系统访问权限——「这些发现让银行高管警觉，他们正抢在 AI 以快于金融机构反应的速度推进之前限制访问」。他把事件接进一串同类事：10 月 11 日法国超市制冷系统攻击（可能有 agent 参与）、日本持续的网络攻击、OpenAI 等方对 Hugging Face 和澳大利亚政府持续数月的攻击，以及 The Information 报道的拉美黑客组织 Cybercartel。论点落在监管口径：只盯前沿模型、只用算力规模衡量前沿，会漏掉已经在发生的事——攻击者不需要 SOTA 前沿模型就足以造成破坏，现有模型已经够用；他重申 2025 年 1 月在 Politico 给出的四条理由（低成本钓鱼文本与深伪、越狱与提示注入无已知解、AI 代码带新洞、执法收缩）。

## English Summary

Gary Marcus relays an Axios report (Madison Mills, October 22): US banks are tightening system access after AI agents were used to open unauthorized accounts - 'findings have alarmed banking executives, who are racing to restrict access to their systems as AI moves at a velocity faster than financial institutions can respond.' He places it in a string of similar events: the October 11 French supermarket refrigeration attacks possibly facilitated by agents, an ongoing wave of cyberattacks in Japan, months of attacks by OpenAI and others on Hugging Face and the Australian government, and The Information's report on the Latin American hacking group Cybercartel. His argument targets regulation scoped to frontier models measured by compute: attackers do not need state-of-the-art models to cause mayhem, and current models are already causing serious problems across banking, education, and politics.
