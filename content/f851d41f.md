# AI 学习五级路线图（Level 1-5）

> 原文：[Miles Deutscher (@milesdeutscher)](https://x.com/milesdeutscher/status/2021666056826486845) | 评分：4

## 原文 / English

If you haven't started learning AI automation, you're starting to fall behind. But don't worry — here's the EXACT roadmap you should use to catch up and increase your chances of staying ahead of the curve. Levels 1-5 (with exact tools included).

### Level 1: Basic Prompt Engineering / AI Skills / Chatbot Tasks

Think: ChatGPT, Claude Web, Gemini

Start here. Learn how to communicate effectively with AI. Master prompting techniques to get better outputs from language models. Understand the basics of how AI chatbots work and how to integrate them into your daily workflows.

### Level 2: Plug-and-Play Agentic Tasks

Think: ManusAI, Runable, LindyAI

Once you know the basics, it's time to level up. Start using ready-made AI agents that can handle multi-step tasks autonomously. These tools can browse the web, write code, analyze data, and execute tasks on your behalf.

**Tip**: Start with using a simple plug-and-play AI agent for 20-30 minutes per day before moving on to more advanced tools. This is how you slowly learn the basics of AI skills (prompt engineering, project management, instructions, etc.) without aimlessly exploring.

### Level 3: Builder Level

You know the basics. Now it's time to start building.

At this level, you start creating your own AI agents and automations. You understand how to chain multiple AI capabilities together, set up triggers, and build workflows that solve your specific problems.

**Tools**: Zapier, Make.com, n8n, custom LLM agent frameworks

### Level 4: Advanced Agentic Systems

Deep understanding of AI agent architectures, memory management, tool use, and multi-agent orchestration. You can build complex systems where multiple AI agents collaborate to solve sophisticated problems.

**Skills**: LangChain, AutoGen, prompt chaining, vector databases, RAG systems

### Level 5: Frontier / Research Level

Working at the cutting edge of AI — contributing to open-source AI projects, fine-tuning models, building novel architectures, and pushing the boundaries of what AI can do.

**Note**: Most people should NOT start here. The five levels above represent the practical path for most learners.

### Key Principle

Fight for your life. The AI landscape changes fast — pick a level that matches where you are today and commit to mastery before moving up.

## 中文

如果你还没有开始学习 AI 自动化，说实话，你已经开始落后了。但别担心——以下是你追赶并保持领先的具体路线图。一至五级（含具体工具推荐）。

### 第一级：基础提示工程 / AI 技能 / 聊天机器人任务

代表工具：ChatGPT、Claude Web、Gemini

从这里开始。学习如何与 AI 高效沟通，掌握提示工程技术以从语言模型获得更好的输出。理解 AI 聊天机器人的基本原理，以及如何将其整合到日常工作流程中。

### 第二级：即插即用型智能体任务

代表工具：ManusAI、Runable、LindyAI

掌握基础后，是时候升级了。开始使用现成的 AI 智能体来处理多步骤自主任务。这些工具可以浏览网页、编写代码、分析数据并代替你执行任务。

**建议**：每天先用简单的即插即用型 AI 智能体操作 20-30 分钟，再过渡到更高级的工具。这样可以循序渐进地学习 AI 技能（提示工程、项目管理、指令编写等），避免漫无目的地摸索。

### 第三级：构建者级别

你已经掌握了基础。现在是时候开始构建了。

在这个级别，你开始创建自己的 AI 智能体和自动化流程。你懂得如何将多种 AI 能力串联起来、设置触发条件，并构建解决特定问题的工作流。

**工具**：Zapier、Make.com、n8n、自定义 LLM 智能体框架

### 第四级：高级智能体系统

深入理解 AI 智能体架构、记忆管理、工具使用和多智能体编排。你能够构建多个 AI 智能体协作解决复杂问题的复杂系统。

**技能**：LangChain、AutoGen、提示链、向量数据库、RAG 系统

### 第五级：前沿 / 研究级别

工作在 AI 的最前沿——为开源 AI 项目贡献代码、微调模型、构建新架构，并拓展 AI 的能力边界。

**注意**：大多数人不应该从这里开始。以上五个级别代表了大多数学习者的实践路径。

### 核心原则

拼命追赶。AI 领域变化极快——选择一个与你当前水平匹配的级别，在升级之前先做到精通。
