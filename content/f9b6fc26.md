## 中文导读

模型聚合网关 OpenRouter 官宣"joining" Stripe。官方披露的运营数据：日处理 **10+ 万亿 token**、接入 **400+ AI 模型**、服务 **1000 万+开发者与公司**，且自 2023 年初创立以来推理量每年至少 10 倍增长。公告强调四点对用户不变：same mission, same name, same product, same roadmap——现有集成完全不动，路由决策仍只以"对用户最有利"为准则。

"为什么是 Stripe"部分的论证：两家公司共享同一种 DNA——把复杂基础设施与市场动态抽象成好用的 API（OpenRouter 多年来被称作 "Stripe for LLMs"）；Stripe 带来庞大的客户网络、互联网公司增长数据、全球可信基础设施的运营经验，以及"地球上最擅长处理欺诈与滥用"的能力，而这在 AI 公司看来会越来越难。作者的定位句是"支撑全球下一波 GDP 增长"——AI 已成美国最大经济增长驱动，推理正在成为每家公司最大的一笔开支。交易尚需惯常交割条件，预计数周内完成；团队约 90 人，收购后仍要保持初创的速度与人才密度。

- 运营数字本身就是行业信号：10T+ tokens/日、400+ 模型、10M+ 开发者、年 10 倍推理增长——这是评估 LLM 推理市场规模的直接锚点。

- 反欺诈/反滥用能力被列为并入的关键动因之一，呼应了 AI API 滥用激增的现实。

## English Summary

OpenRouter, the first and largest AI model marketplace and gateway, announced it is joining Stripe (2026-08-19; closing expected within weeks subject to customary conditions). The company reports processing 10+ trillion tokens per day across 400+ AI models for over 10 million developers and companies, with at least 10x growth in inference volume every year since its 2023 founding. The post commits that mission, name, product, and roadmap stay the same and existing integrations do not change; routing remains driven solely by what's best for the user. Rationale: shared DNA (both abstract complex infrastructure into delightful APIs — OpenRouter has long been called "Stripe for LLMs"), Stripe's customer network and global trusted-infrastructure experience, and best-in-class fraud/abuse management, framed as powering "the next wave of GDP growth" as inference becomes the largest line item for companies. The ~90-person team says it will preserve startup velocity and talent density.
