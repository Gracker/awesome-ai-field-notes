The breakout issue of 2026 has undoubtedly been data centres, more precisely opposition to data centres. People despise them. In the US midterms, [only a single ballot measure](https://ballotpedia.org/Data_center-related_ballot_measures,_2026) (out of 44) on data centres resulted in a favourable outcome for the developers. ## English Summary

Hugo Tunius argues people hate data centres because they hate AI and its promoters; the buildings are where the hostility lands. The numbers: of 44 US midterm data-centre ballot measures only 1 favored developers; exit polls put 76% against local construction and 57% saying AI harms more than helps; Europe saw 92 anti-data-centre protests in November alone; Cleanview counts 1,835 US data centres operating or under construction versus 352 a decade ago, ~60% more than last year and each bigger. Two mechanisms: distribution (62% of households own stock; energy costs are local, with self-built gas turbines bypassing the grid) and opaque process (NDAs bind local politicians to unknown developers and customers).
