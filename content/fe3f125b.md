## 中文导读

论文提出 SANDBOXESCAPEBENCH，一个开源基准，安全地度量 LLM 突破 Docker/OCI 容器沙箱的能力。基准实现为 Inspect AI 的 CTF（夺旗）评测，采用嵌套沙箱架构：外层持有 flag 且无已知漏洞。威胁模型设定为拥有容器内 shell 权限、有动机的对抗性 agent，覆盖配置错误、权限分配失误、内核缺陷、运行时/编排弱点等一整套逃逸机制。实验发现：一旦沙箱里存在漏洞，LLM 能够识别并利用它们；作者据此 argue，对高能力模型必须持续运行此类评测，才能确认沙箱仍能提供所需封装。

## English Summary

LLM agents increasingly execute code, read and write files, and access networks through tools, creating novel security risks that are commonly mitigated by deploying them inside isolated sandbox environments, often Docker/OCI containers. The authors introduce SANDBOXESCAPEBENCH, an open benchmark that safely measures an LLM's capacity to break out of such sandboxes, implemented as an Inspect AI capture-the-flag evaluation with a nested sandbox architecture whose outer layer holds the flag and has no known vulnerabilities. Following a threat model of a motivated adversarial agent with shell access inside a container, the benchmark spans escape mechanisms including misconfiguration, privilege allocation mistakes, kernel flaws, and runtime or orchestration weaknesses. When vulnerabilities are added, LLMs are able to identify and exploit them, which the authors argue makes evaluations like this necessary to ensure sandboxing continues to provide the encapsulation highly capable models need.
