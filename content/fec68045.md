# SLA: Scaling Spatial-Language-Action Model via Continual Pre-training

- **来源**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08373
- **作者**: Fuhao Li, Wenxuan Song, Qizhou Huang, et al.
- **发表**: 2026-11-05 · cs.RO
- **热度**: HF daily papers 81 upvotes（2026-11-08 抓取）

## Abstract（节选原文）

> Initialized from a pretrained pi05 policy, SLA jointly optimizes action prediction and alignment between intermediate visual embeddings and spatial representations from a frozen 3D perception model. We curate and unify heterogeneous robot datasets to support spatial learning across diverse embodiments, tasks, and scenes. … we introduce feature-dependent gates that adaptively aggregate visual embeddings from multiple VLA layers for spatial supervision. … All auxiliary components are used only during training, allowing direct reuse of pretrained VLA weights and preserving the original inference architecture.

## 中文摘要

空间-语言-动作模型工作（cs.RO，HF 81 热度）：从预训练 pi05 策略初始化，把隐式空间对齐扩展进持续预训练并在下游后训练中保持——动作预测与中间视觉嵌入对齐冻结 3D 感知模型空间表征同时优化。统一异构机器人数据支持跨本体/任务/场景；引入特征依赖门控，自适应聚合多层视觉嵌入供空间监督；教师利用部分可用的深度与相机标定，兼容异构几何标注；概率状态丢弃鼓励依赖视觉线索、降低对示范轨迹过拟合。所有辅助组件仅训练期使用，可直接复用预训练 VLA 权重、保持原推理架构。摘要未给出可引用的统一成功率。
