## English Original

Google released Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview on February 19, 2026, a next-generation model specifically designed for complex workflow orchestration. Featuring Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architecture for speed, 1 million token context window, and full multimodal understanding across text, images, audio, and video. Available through Google AI Studio, Vertex AI, and Google Antigravity, supporting various access methods including API keys, Google AI Pro, and Google AI Ultra.
