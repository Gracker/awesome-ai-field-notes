# 微软与 OpenAI 重签合作协议：取消独家许可，废除 AGI 条款


- **来源**：news
- **作者**：Reuters
- **原文链接**：https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/microsoft-end-exclusive-license-openais-technology-2026-04-27/
- **日期**：2026-04-27

---

微软与 OpenAI 宣布修改合作协议：IP 许可从独家改为非独家（延续至 2032 年）；删除 AGI 限制性条款；收入分成机制重设。此前 2025 年 10 月重组中 OpenAI 取消利润上限并给予微软 27% 股权。这次修改为 OpenAI 接入 AWS 等其他云平台扫清法律障碍，标志着 AI 行业最核心的商业关系发生结构性变化。

## English Summary

Microsoft and OpenAI revised their partnership: IP licensing changed from exclusive to non-exclusive (through 2032), AGI restrictive clauses removed, and revenue sharing restructured. This clears the path for OpenAI to access other cloud platforms like AWS, marking a structural shift in the AI industry's most critical business relationship.
