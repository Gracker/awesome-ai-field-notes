# 欧盟 AI 法案修改谈判破裂：12 小时协商未果，八月合规期限悬而未决


- **来源**：news
- **作者**：Reuters / POLITICO
- **原文链接**：https://www.reuters.com/sustainability/boards-policy-regulation/eu-countries-lawmakers-fail-reach-deal-watered-down-ai-rules-2026-04-29/
- **日期**：2026-04-29

---

欧盟成员国与欧洲议会经 12 小时谈判未能就 AI 法案修订达成一致。修订方案原计划推迟 2026 年 8 月的高风险系统合规期限、缩窄适用范围，并将 AI 义务与现有行业法规整合。主要分歧在于部分国家坚持已受行业安全法规约束的领域应豁免 AI 法案额外要求。5 月将是截止日期前的最后谈判窗口。

## English Summary

EU member states and European Parliament failed to reach agreement on AI Act revisions after 12 hours of negotiations. The revision aimed to delay the August 2026 high-risk system compliance deadline and narrow scope. Key disagreement: some countries insist sectors already under industry safety regulations should be exempt from additional AI Act requirements. May is the last negotiation window.
