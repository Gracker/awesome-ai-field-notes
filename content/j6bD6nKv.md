# 科技爱好者周刊#301：OpenAI 的图书馆工位

## English

Issue 301 of "Tech Enthusiast Weekly" titled "OpenAI's Library Workspaces" was published on May 24, 2024.

In this issue, Ruan Yifeng introduces OpenAI's headquarters in downtown San Francisco, a three-story building converted from a food factory. The article specifically mentions that OpenAI's headquarters houses a two-story library with a相当大的 area, containing books recommended by employees and decorated with photos of scientists. This library is not just a place for reading and relaxation, but also features many university-study-room-like workspaces where employees can focus on their work for an entire day.

Ruan Yifeng believes that this "library workspace" model offers numerous benefits, including providing a quiet environment that helps employees concentrate, engage deeply in work, and tackle difficult problems, which is particularly important for pioneering companies like OpenAI. Additionally, the relaxed atmosphere of the library helps alleviate work fatigue and may even inspire unexpected ideas. He contrasts this model with the common domestic internet company practice of open, crowded "workshop-style" workspaces, pointing out that while the latter may be suitable for "large-scale military-style" projects, it could be counterproductive for intellectual projects requiring deep thinking. Ruan Yifeng suggests that future innovative small companies, especially those working on AI models, could learn from OpenAI's approach by setting up comfortable libraries and study rooms within their offices where employees can work, expecting to achieve better results.

## 中文

这是《科技爱好者周刊》第301期题为"OpenAI 的图书馆工位"的文章，于2024年5月24日发布。

在这期周刊中，阮一峰介绍了OpenAI位于旧金山市区的总部，这是一栋由食品厂改造而成的三层小楼。文章特别提到，OpenAI的总部内设有一个面积相当大的两层图书馆，里面收藏了员工推荐的书籍，并装饰着科学家的照片。这个图书馆不仅是一个阅读和休息的场所，还设有许多类似大学自习室的工位，员工可以在这里专注地工作一整天。

阮一峰认为，这种"图书馆工位"有诸多益处，包括提供安静的环境以帮助员工集中注意力、投入工作和思考难题，这对于OpenAI这类开创性公司尤为重要。此外，图书馆的轻松氛围有助于减轻工作疲劳，甚至可能激发意想不到的灵感。他将这种模式与国内互联网公司常见的开放式、拥挤的"车间式工位"进行对比，指出后者虽然适合"大军团作战"的项目，但对于需要深度思考的智力项目可能适得其反。阮一峰建议，未来的创新型小公司，特别是从事AI模型的公司，可以借鉴OpenAI的做法，在公司内设置舒适的图书馆和自习室，允许员工在此办公，以期达到更好的效果。
