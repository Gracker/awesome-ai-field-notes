# What 81,000 People Want from AI（Anthropic 81K 用户访谈研究）

## 英文原文

Public conversation about AI often centers on abstract projections of its risks and benefits. What's largely missing is a vision for what "AI going well" means, grounded in the concrete aspirations of people around the world who already use AI and have begun developing a sense of what it might do for them.
So we asked our users about their hopes and concerns with AI, as well as how their perspectives connect to their actual experiences with the technology. Over one week in December, we invited everyone with a Claude.ai account to sit down with Anthropic Interviewer—a version of Claude prompted to conduct a conversational interview—and tell us about how they view AI. 80,508 people, across 159 countries and 70 languages, took the interview.

## 中文翻译

关于 AI 的公开讨论通常集中在对其风险和收益的抽象预测上。但很大程度上缺失的是对"AI 发展良好"意味着什么的愿景——这一愿景植根于全球已使用 AI 并开始了解 AI 能为他们做什么的人们的具体愿望。
因此，我们向用户询问了他们对 AI 的希望和担忧，以及他们的观点如何与他们使用该技术的实际体验相联系。在十二月的一周内，我们邀请所有拥有 Claude.ai 账户的人与 Anthropic Interviewer（一个被设定为进行对话访谈的 Claude 版本）坐下来，告诉我们他们如何看待 AI。80,508 人，来自 159 个国家和地区，说 70 种语言，接受了访谈。我们相信这是迄今为止规模最大、语言最多元化的定性研究。

---

## 英文原文

We asked Claude to identify and categorize what each person most wanted from AI:
01. Professional excellence (18.8%) - Improve effectiveness and lean into more meaningful work by having AI handle routine tasks so they can focus on higher-value strategic work, complex problem-solving, and professional mastery.
02. Personal transformation (13.7%) - Achieve personal growth, emotional wellbeing, or life transformation with AI as guide, coach, or support.
03. Life management (13.5%) - AI as comprehensive organizational support and cognitive scaffolding — e.g. managing schedules, reducing mental burden, executive function support.
04. Time freedom (11.1%) - Reclaim time from work and chores to be present with family or friends, pursue hobbies, travel, rest.
05. Financial independence (9.7%) - Achieve financial freedom or economic security through AI.

## 中文翻译

我们让 Claude 识别并分类每个人最希望从 AI 中获得什么：
01. 职业卓越（18.8%）- 通过 AI 处理日常任务来提高效率，专注于更有意义的工作，从而专注于高价值战略工作、复杂问题解决和专业精通。
02. 个人蜕变（13.7%）- 以 AI 为向导、教练或支持，实现个人成长、情绪健康或生活改变。
03. 生活管理（13.5%）- AI 作为全面的组织支持和认知脚手架——例如管理日程、减轻心理负担、执行功能支持。
04. 时间自由（11.1%）- 从工作和家务中夺回时间，与家人朋友共处，追求爱好、旅行和休息。
05. 经济独立（9.7%）- 通过 AI 实现经济自由或经济安全。

## 英文原文

When asked if AI had ever taken a step towards their stated vision, 81% of people said yes. We grouped those experiences into six main areas:
01. Productivity (32.0%) - AI dramatically sped up work and automated repetitive tasks.
02. AI hasn't delivered (18.9%) - AI fell short of expectations or isn't yet capable of what they envision.
03. Cognitive partnership (17.2%) - AI served as a thinking partner or creative collaborator.
04. Learning (9.9%) - AI helped learn a new skill or subject.
05. Technical accessibility (8.7%) - AI enabled building something previously out of reach.
06. Research synthesis (7.2%) - AI helped synthesize research or complex information.

## 中文翻译

当被问及 AI 是否曾向他们所述愿景迈出一步时，81%的人表示是的。我们将这些体验分为六个主要领域：
01. 生产力（32.0%）- AI 大幅加速工作并自动化重复性任务。
02. AI 尚未兑现（18.9%）- AI 未达预期，或尚不具备他们期望的能力。
03. 认知伙伴关系（17.2%）- AI 作为思维伙伴或创意合作者。
04. 学习（9.9%）- AI 帮助学习新技能或新主题。
05. 技术可及性（8.7%）- AI 使之前无法企及的事情变得可能。
06. 研究综合（7.2%）- AI 帮助综合研究或复杂信息。

## 英文原文

AI is used heavily for work, and so it's perhaps unsurprising that the largest group of people (19%) sought "professional excellence"—wanting AI to handle mundane tasks so they can focus on strategic, higher-level problems. Many others similarly started the interview talking about productivity, but after Anthropic Interviewer asked about their underlying hope behind it—what realizing this vision would enable for them—other priorities surfaced. It wasn't about doing better work, but increasing their quality of life outside of it.

## 中文翻译

AI 被大量用于工作，因此最大的群体（19%）寻求"职业卓越"也许不足为奇——他们希望 AI 处理琐碎任务，这样他们可以专注于战略性、高层次的问题。许多其他人在访谈开始时谈论生产力，但当 Anthropic Interviewer 询问他们背后更深层的希望——实现这一愿景能让他们实现什么——其他优先事项浮出水面。问题不在于更好地工作，而在于提高工作之外的生活质量。
