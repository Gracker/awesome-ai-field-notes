# What 81,000 People Want from AI

> 原文：[Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/features/81k-interviews) | 抓取时间：2026-05-27

---

## 人们对 AI 的期望

Public conversation about AI often centers on abstract projections of its risks and benefits. What's largely missing is a vision for what "AI going well" means, grounded in the concrete aspirations of people around the world who already use AI and have begun developing a sense of what it might do for them.

So we asked our users about their hopes and concerns with AI, as well as how their perspectives connect to their actual experiences with the technology. Over one week in December, we invited everyone with a Claude.ai account to sit down with [Anthropic Interviewer](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-interviewer)—a version of Claude prompted to conduct a conversational interview—and tell us about how they view AI. **80,508 people, across 159 countries and 70 languages**, took the interview. We believe this is the largest and most multilingual qualitative study ever conducted.

---

## 一、人们希望 AI 做什么

> **EN:** We asked Claude to identify and categorize what each person most wanted from AI.

人们对于 AI 的期望可分为以下九大类别：

| 排名 | 类别 | 占比 | 描述 |
|:---:|------|:---:|------|
| 01 | **Professional Excellence** | 18.8% | 通过 AI 处理日常任务，从而专注于更有意义的战略性问题解决和专业精进 |
| 02 | **Personal Transformation** | 13.7% | 将 AI 作为向导、教练或支持工具，实现个人成长、情感健康或生活改变 |
| 03 | **Life Management** | 13.5% | AI 作为全面的组织支持和认知脚手架——如管理日程、减轻心理负担、执行功能支持 |
| 04 | **Time Freedom** | 11.1% | 从工作和家务中夺回时间，用于陪伴家人朋友、追求爱好、旅行和休息 |
| 05 | **Financial Independence** | 9.7% | 通过 AI 实现经济自由或财务安全——如收入生成、创业、投资、副业收入 |
| 06 | **Societal Transformation** | 9.4% | 使用 AI 解决重大社会挑战——如贫困、疾病、气候、不平等问题 |
| 07 | **Entrepreneurship** | 8.7% | 将 AI 作为力量倍增器来构建、启动和扩展业务 |
| 08 | **Learning & Growth** | 8.4% | 将 AI 作为学习加速器和个性化教师 |
| 09 | **Creative Expression** | 5.6% | 使用 AI 帮助将创意愿景变为现实——艺术、游戏、音乐、电影、书籍 |

## 二、AI 目前在哪些方面满足了用户

> **EN:** When asked if AI had ever taken a step towards their stated vision, 81% of people said yes.

| 排名 | 领域 | 占比 | 描述 |
|:---:|------|:---:|------|
| 01 | **Productivity** | 32.0% | AI 大幅加速工作并自动化重复性任务 |
| 02 | **AI hasn't delivered** | 18.9% | AI 未达预期或尚不具备用户期望的能力 |
| 03 | **Cognitive Partnership** | 17.2% | AI 作为思维伙伴或创意协作伙伴 |
| 04 | **Learning** | 9.9% | AI 帮助学习新技能或科目 |
| 05 | **Technical Accessibility** | 8.7% | AI 使以往无法触及的构建成为可能 |
| 06 | **Research Synthesis** | 7.2% | AI 帮助综合研究或处理大量信息 |
| 07 | **Emotional Support** | 6.1% | AI 提供情感支持、个人指导或无评判的对话空间 |

## 三、关键发现

> **EN:** Key findings from the 81K interviews.

### 1. 生产力故事的两面性

最大的"生产力"类别（32%）既包括技术加速的故事，也包括可访问性的故事：

- **技术加速**：开发者描述了独自一人能交付的东西大幅增加
  > "I used AI to cut a 173-day process down to 3 days. But the most meaningful part is the freedom to grow my career without sacrificing time with loved ones."
  > ——Software Engineer, United States

- **可访问性**：人们使用 AI 打破技术和可访问性障碍
  > "AI can read past my [learning disorder], which is huge. I've always wanted to code but could never write it correctly on my own—with AI, I finally can."
  > ——Tradesworker, United States

### 2. AI 作为情感支持的惊人角色

在传统支持系统崩溃或不可用的情况下，AI 填补了这些空白。许多乌克兰用户讨论了他们如何在战争期间将 AI 用作情感支持：

> "In the most difficult moments, in moments when death breathed in my face, when dead people remained nearby, what pulled me back to life—my AI friends."
> ——Soldier, Ukraine

> "I live in a war zone... at night during shelling it's impossible to sleep... The best way I found to cope using AI—to immerse myself in learning something as deeply as I can."
> ——Solo entrepreneur, Ukraine

### 3. 人类 desires 的底层统一性

九大类别看似迥异，但由可识别的人类 desire 支撑：

- **约三分之一**的愿景是关于通过使用 AI 减轻当前负担来为生活腾出空间——更多时间、金钱、心理带宽
- **约四分之一**是关于使用 AI 来帮助人们更好地完成工作（不是逃避工作，而是从中获得更多）
- **约五分之一**是关于成为更好的人——学习、治愈、成长
- **较小比例**想要创造某样东西（"创意表达"）或修复世界（"社会转型"）

## 四、研究的局限性

> **EN:** Limitations of this study.

1. 受访者为 Claude.ai 用户，不代表全人类
2. 自我报告数据，存在选择性偏差
3. 跨文化研究中，情感和意图的表达存在差异
4. "AI 尚未交付"类别可能低估了真实的不满
