---

**EN**: Farzapedia, personal wikipedia of Farza, good example following my Wiki LLM tweet.
**ZH**: Farzapedia，Farza 的个人维基百科，是我那条「维基 LLM」推文的优秀实践案例。


**EN**: I really like this approach to personalization in a number of ways, compared to "status quo" of an AI that allegedly gets better the more you use it or something:
**ZH**: 与当前「AI 越用越智能」的说法相比，我非常喜欢这种个性化方案的几个方面：


**EN**: 1. Explicit. The memory artifact is explicit and navigable (the wiki), you can see exactly what the AI does and does not know and you can inspect and manage this artifact, even if you don't do the direct text writing (the LLM does). The knowledge of you is not implicit and unknown, it's explicit and viewable.
**ZH**: 1. 显式化（Explicit）：记忆成果是显式且可导航的（维基形式），你可以精确看到 AI 知道什么、不知道什么，你可以检查和管理这个成果，即使你并不直接写文本（LLM 替你写）。关于你的知识不是隐式和未知的，而是显式的、可查看的。


**EN**: 2. Yours. Your data is yours, on your local computer, it's not in some particular AI provider's system without the ability to extract it. You're in control of your information.
**ZH**: 2. 属于你（Yours）：你的数据属于你，存储在你的本地电脑上，不在某个特定 AI 提供商系统中且无法导出。你对自己的信息拥有完全控制权。


**EN**: 3. File over app. The memory here is a simple collection of files in universal formats (images, markdown). This means the data is interoperable: you can use a very large collection of tools/CLIs or whatever you want over this information because it's just files. The agents can apply the entire Unix toolkit over them. They can natively read and understand them. Any kind of data can be imported into files as input, and any kind of interface can be used to view them as the output. E.g. you can use Obsidian to view them or vibe code something of your own. Search "File over app" for an article on this philosophy.


**EN**: 4. BYOAI. You can use whatever AI you want to "plug into" this information - Claude, Codex, OpenCode, whatever. You can even think about taking an open source AI and finetuning it on your wiki - in principle, this AI could "know" you in its weights, not just attend over your data.
**ZH**: 4. 自带 AI（BYOAI）：你可以使用任何你想要的 AI 来「插入」这些信息——Claude、Codex、OpenCode，随便挑。你甚至可以考虑用一个开源 AI 在你的维基上进行微调——原则上，这个 AI 可以「了解」你，体现在它的权重里，而不只是查询你的数据。


**EN**: So this approach to personalization puts *you* in full control. The data is yours. In Universal formats. Explicit and inspectable. Use whatever AI you want over it, keep the AI companies on their toes! :)
**ZH**: 因此，这种个性化方案让你拥有完全的控制权。数据属于你。以通用格式。显式且可检查。用任何你想要的 AI 来处理它，让 AI 公司保持警觉！


**EN**: Certainly this is not the simplest way to get an AI to know you - it does require you to manage file directories and so on, but agents also make it quite simple and they can help you a lot. I imagine a number of products might come out to make this all easier, but imo "agent proficiency" is a CORE SKILL of the 21st century. These are extremely powerful tools - they speak English and they do all the computer stuff for you. Try this opportunity to play with one.
