# tibo_maker 复盘：从 9 个失败产品到 5 个 AI 产品月入 100 万美元的方法论
# tibo_maker Postmortem: From 9 Failed Products to 5 AI Products Generating $1M/month

## 中文原文 / Chinese Original

Tibo 在 5 个 AI 产品上全部采用 **bootstrap 路线**，展示小团队如何用 AI 工具实现规模化。

### 核心教训

1. **创始人最大错误是不想犯错** — 害怕失败才是真正的失败
2. **先验证想法，再用钱买时间** — 而不是反过来
3. **快速验证、快速失败** — 比完美更重要

### 方法论

**Idea 验证阶段**
- 用最低成本做出 MVP（通常 1-2 周）
- 找到 100 个真正付费用户之前，不大量投入工程资源
- 验证维度：用户愿意推荐 > 愿意付费 > 愿意留邮箱

**精益创业 + AI 工具**
- 把 AI 工具和精益创业方法结合
- 用 AI 辅助做市场调研、竞品分析、用户访谈摘要
- 每月复盘：哪些假设被验证、哪些需要 pivot

### 真实案例

Tibo 的 5 个 AI 产品覆盖不同场景，最终形成矩阵：
- 产品 A：解决某类创作者的内容生产问题
- 产品 B：面向小型 B2B SaaS 的效率工具
- 产品 C：个人生产力工具

每个产品都从 bootstrap 开始，验证后再决定是否规模化。

### 核心结论

**"失败得快，才能成功得快"** — 不是鸡汤，是可操作的方法论。

把 AI 工具和精益创业结合，让独立开发者可以用最小成本试错，快速验证市场需求。

## English Translation

Tibo used **bootstrap approach** across all 5 AI products, demonstrating how small teams can scale using AI tools.

### Key Lessons

1. **The biggest founder mistake is not wanting to make mistakes** — fear of failure is the real failure
2. **Validate the idea first, then spend money to buy time** — not the other way around
3. **Fail fast, validate fast** — perfection is the enemy of progress

### Methodology

**Idea Validation Phase**
- Build MVP at lowest cost (typically 1-2 weeks)
- Don't pour significant engineering resources until you find 100 paying users
- Validation hierarchy: users willing to recommend > willing to pay > willing to leave email

**Lean Startup + AI Tools**
- Combine AI tools with lean startup methodology
- Use AI for market research, competitive analysis, user interview summaries
- Monthly review: which assumptions were validated, which need to pivot

### Real Case

Tibo's 5 AI products span different scenarios, eventually forming a portfolio:
- Product A: Solves content production for a certain type of creator
- Product B: Efficiency tool for small B2B SaaS
- Product C: Personal productivity tool

Each product started bootstrap, validated, then decided whether to scale.

### Core Takeaway

**"Fail fast, succeed fast"** — not motivational fluff, but actionable methodology.

Combining AI tools with lean startup lets indie developers test with minimum cost and quickly validate market demand.
