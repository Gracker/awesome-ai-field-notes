## English

How @tibo_maker turned a $2K MRR failing product into a $600K MRR business: "When I acquired Typeframe, it was doing $2K MRR. I spent money on this product and I just wanted to not be wrong. This is the #1 mistake that founders make. It's more important for them to not be wrong than to be successful. If you force the selling of your products, you're not listening to people telling you there's another opportunity that might be bigger." After this, Tibo noticed that people wanted to make viral shorts on social media, so he pivoted the product to Revid and now it's making $600K+ MRR.

## 中文

@tibo_maker 是如何把一个月收入 2K 美元的低谷产品变成月收入 60 万美元业务的："当我收购 Typeframe 时，它每月收入约 2000 美元。我在这个产品上投入了资金，我只是不想犯错误。这是创始人最容易犯的头号错误。对他们来说，不犯错比成功更重要。如果你强推自己的产品，你就听不进去人们告诉你的——还有另一个可能更大的机会。"随后 Tibo 发现人们想在社交媒体上制作病毒式短视频，于是他将产品转向了 Revid，现在月收入已超过 60 万美元。
