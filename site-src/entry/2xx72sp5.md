# THE 2028 GLOBAL INTELLIGENCE CRISIS

> AI 经济死亡螺旋的情景推演：幽灵 GDP 与人类智能替代螺旋

## English

### The Scenario

A thought experiment, not a prediction. By 2028, unemployment reaches 10.2%. The S&P 500 has pulled back 38% from its highs. The conventional economic indicators say we're in a recession — but GDP is growing at 4.1%.

### Ghost GDP

The core concept: **Ghost GDP** — output counted in national accounts but not flowing into the real economic cycle. AI-driven productivity gains inflate GDP numbers while simultaneously displacing human workers whose wages would have fueled consumption.

The accounting says growth. The humans feel depression.

### The Intelligence Substitution Spiral

There's no natural braking mechanism. As AI capability increases:
1. More tasks get automated → more workers displaced
2. Displaced workers have less income → less consumption
3. Less consumption → more pressure to cut costs → more automation
4. The cycle accelerates

Unlike previous technological revolutions, this one targets cognitive labor — the sector that was supposed to be immune.

### The SaaS Death Spiral

Enterprise SaaS companies face a paradox: AI makes their products better but also makes them easier to replicate. When AI can build a competitor in a weekend, moats evaporate.

Revenue multiples compress. R&D budgets shift from building features to building AI agents. The agent you built might replace your own product team.

## 中文

### 情景设定

这不是预测，是情景推演。到 2028 年，失业率达 10.2%，标普 500 自高点回撤 38%。传统经济指标说这是衰退——但 GDP 在以 4.1% 的速度增长。

### 幽灵 GDP

核心概念：**幽灵 GDP**——计入国民经济账户但不流入真实经济循环的产出。AI 驱动的生产力提升膨胀了 GDP 数字，同时取代了那些工资本应支撑消费的劳动者。

账面上说增长。人感受到的是萧条。

### 人类智能替代螺旋

没有自然刹车机制。随着 AI 能力提升：
1. 更多任务被自动化 → 更多工人被取代
2. 被取代的工人收入减少 → 消费下降
3. 消费下降 → 降本压力增大 → 更多自动化
4. 循环加速

与以往的技术革命不同，这一次瞄准的是认知劳动——本应免疫的领域。

### SaaS 死亡螺旋

企业 SaaS 公司面临一个悖论：AI 让产品变得更好，但也让竞品更容易被复制。当 AI 能在一个周末内构建出竞品时，护城河就蒸发了。

收入倍数压缩。研发预算从构建功能转向构建 AI Agent。而你构建的 Agent 可能会取代你自己的产品团队。
