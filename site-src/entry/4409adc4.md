## English
Movement Matters – A Turing Test for Robot Interaction 🤖

This study proposes a novel approach to evaluating AI robot capabilities through interactive movement tasks. The research focuses on:

1. Natural human-robot interaction scenarios
   - Collaborative tasks requiring physical coordination
   - Adaptive behavior in dynamic environments
   - Social cue recognition and response

2. Evaluation metrics beyond traditional tests
   - Movement fluency and naturalness
   - Response time and adaptability
   - Social engagement quality

3. Implications for AI safety and alignment
   - Physical embodiment effects on cognition
   - Social interaction as alignment constraint
   - Real-world testing limitations

The findings suggest that movement-based evaluation provides unique insights into AI capabilities that purely language-based tests miss.

运动很重要 - 机器人交互的图灵测试 🤖

1. 自然人机交互场景
   - 需要身体协调的协作任务
   - 动态环境中的适应性行为
   - 社会线索识别和响应

2. 超越传统测试的评估指标
   - 运动流畅度和自然性
   - 响应时间和适应性
   - 社交参与质量

3. 对AI安全和 alignment 的影响
   - 物理 embodiment 对认知的影响
   - 社交交互作为alignment约束
   - 现实世界测试的限制

研究结果表明，基于运动的评估提供了纯粹语言测试无法获得的AI能力独特见解。
